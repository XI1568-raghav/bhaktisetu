# Design Context — Poojly

## Target Audience
- **Primary**: Indian devotees (25-55 years), both urban and semi-urban, who want convenient access to temple darshan, puja bookings, and spiritual content
- **Secondary**: NRI diaspora (30M+ in US/UK/UAE) who want to stay connected to their spiritual roots from abroad
- **Tertiary**: Younger spiritual seekers (18-30) exploring meditation, mantras, and astrology

## Use Cases
1. Watch live temple darshan from home or abroad
2. Book authentic pujas with verified priests (with video proof)
3. Listen to aartis, mantras, and chalisas during daily practice
4. Check daily panchang (Hindu calendar) for auspicious timings
5. Get AI-powered spiritual guidance (Ask a Pandit chatbot)
6. Shop for puja essentials (thalis, rudraksha, incense)
7. Adopt a cow at a verified gaushala
8. Consult with verified astrologers

## Brand Personality / Tone
- **Warm** — like a trusted family pandit, not a cold SaaS product
- **Modern** — technology-forward but culturally rooted
- **Trustworthy** — verified priests, video proof, transparent pricing
- **Accessible** — simple enough for a 55-year-old parent, refined enough for a 30-year-old NRI professional
- NOT preachy, NOT overly traditional, NOT generic spiritual-purple

## Aesthetic Direction
**"Sacred Minimalism"** — Clean, warm, spacious interfaces that feel like a modern interpretation of temple architecture. Saffron warmth meets Notion-level restraint.

## Color Story
- **Dominant**: Saffron (#E16318) — devotion, auspiciousness, energy
- **Accent**: Temple Gold (#D4A438) — divine, prosperity
- **Neutrals**: Warm off-whites and warm near-blacks (not cold grays)
- **Support**: Sacred red, tulsi green, ganga blue (used sparingly)

## Typography
- **Primary**: Noto Sans (clean, modern, excellent Devanagari support)
- **Display**: Same family, semibold weight with tight tracking for headings
- **Body**: 16px base, 1.6 line-height, surface-600 color

## Quality Bar
- **Flagship** — this is the main marketing site AND the product. Every pixel matters.
