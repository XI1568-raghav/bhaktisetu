## Slide 2: The Problem

### India's $58.56B Religious Market Is Massively Fragmented

**For Devotees:**
- A typical devotee uses **5-10 different apps** to fulfill their spiritual needs: one for darshan, another for pujas, another for spiritual content, another for astrology, another for prasad delivery
- There is **no single platform** that connects the full devotional journey end-to-end
- The experience is broken, unreliable, and littered with unverified service providers

**For the 30M+ NRI Diaspora:**
- NRIs have **NO reliable way** to stay connected to their spiritual roots from abroad
- They cannot witness temple rituals, book pujas for family back home, or receive authentic prasad
- They are willing to pay a premium but have nowhere trustworthy to spend

**For Temples:**
- Temples **lose 40-60% of potential revenue** to intermediaries and aggregators
- Most temples lack the technology to reach digital audiences directly
- Donation transparency is poor, eroding trust

> **The bottom line:** A trillion-dollar faith economy is stuck in the analog era, and no one has built the super-app to digitize it.
