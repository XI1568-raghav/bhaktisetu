## Interview script (30 min)

1. **Warm-up (3 min)** — Who do you usually do puja with? (family pandit / temple / no one)
2. **Discovery (5 min)** — How did you find Bhaktisetu? What were you trying to solve?
3. **Booking experience (5 min)** — Walk me through your last booking. What was easy? Where did you pause?
4. **Puja experience (5 min)** — What happened on the day? Did it match what you expected?
5. **Aftermath (5 min)** — Did you receive video proof? Receipt? Did you feel the puja was complete?
6. **Wow + churn (5 min)** — One moment that surprised you (good). One that made you consider canceling.
7. **NPS + close (2 min)** — On a scale of 0-10, how likely are you to recommend Bhaktisetu to a friend or family member?
