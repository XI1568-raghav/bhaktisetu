1/8 First 10 paying customers on Bhaktisetu — what they taught me about devotion, trust, and where temple-tech is actually broken. <!-- 132/280 -->

2/8 Customer #1 was my own mother. She booked a Satyanarayan puja in our hometown temple. Not because she trusted the tech — because she wanted me to ship. The bug she found in checkout cost ₹0 to fix and saved every customer after her. <!-- 261/280 -->

3/8 Customer #2 was a stranger from Indore who searched "online rudrabhishek booking". She paid ₹2100. The pandit confirmed within 4 hours. She left a Google review. That review brought customer #3, #4, and #5. <!-- 219/280 -->

4/8 Customer #6 was the hardest. A widow in Pune wanted a shradh puja for her late husband. The pandit we'd assigned was unavailable that day. I personally called 3 other pandits at 7am to find a replacement. She got her puja. We refunded the urgency fee. <!-- 280/280 -->

5/8 Lesson from #6: every devotional product has a "no second chance" event. A shradh on the wrong day is worse than no shradh. We added a same-day backup-pandit policy after her booking. <!-- 197/280 -->

6/8 Customers #7 and #8 were NRIs in the US. Both asked the same q: "how do I know it actually happened?" Video proof became the #1 product feature, not a nice-to-have. The video is shorter than 90 seconds. We send it via WhatsApp same day. <!-- 247/280 -->

7/8 Customer #9 left a 1-star review. The pandit recited the wrong gotra. We refunded fully and added a gotra confirmation step before puja day. The reviewer updated to 4 stars after the refund. Honest losses are worth more than fake wins. <!-- 252/280 -->

8/8 Customer #10 booked because their cousin (customer #6) recommended us. Word-of-mouth in devotional commerce is everything. Browse what they booked: bhaktisetu.com/pujas <!-- 180/280 -->
