## New measurable success criteria

- 12 build-in-public threads drafted and committed to `docs/agent-reports/cmo/2026-05-founder-twitter-pack/` with frontmatter (hook ≤280 chars, tweet_count 5-12, audience tag, CTA, schedule week + day hint).
- `cadence.md` defines 3 posts/week × 4 weeks for Month 5, with explicit IST + PT timing and queue-prep workflow.
- `engagement-playbook.md` defines a <2h reply SLA during the first 4h of each post, a DM funnel template that drives to `bhaktisetu.com`, quote-vs-reply rules, and blocklist criteria.
- Founder posts at least 10 of the 12 threads during Month 5 and updates `metrics-tracker.md` weekly with followers, impressions, profile clicks, and link clicks.

## KPI ratchet recommendation

- Original roadmap target: **1000 followers by end of Q2** (cumulative cold-start across ~3 months).
- Realistic Month 5 milestone: **+250 followers in 30 days** (25% of cycle), measured manually each Sunday.
- Recommendation: keep the 1000 cumulative Q2 target as the headline KPI, but commit to ratcheting *down* to 250/month sustained if Month 5 closes below +100. A cold start with no paid amplification typically delivers 50-200 followers in the first month — 1000 in 90 days assumes one viral thread or a podcast lift.
- Founder should review the Week 4 numbers in `metrics-tracker.md` and decide whether to:
  - Keep 1000/Q2 (default — push through with paid amp in Month 6 if needed)
  - Ratchet to 250/month sustained (safer — keeps the KPI honest and the funnel measurable)
  - Pause Twitter and reallocate to a higher-leverage channel if Month 5 closes below +50 followers

## Why this is honest

The prior placeholder existed because nobody had stress-tested 1000 in 90 days against a cold-start founder account with no paid spend. This pack ships the content side; the KPI honesty is on the founder to confirm after measuring.
