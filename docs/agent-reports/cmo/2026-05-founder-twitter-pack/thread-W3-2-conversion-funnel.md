1/9 Bhaktisetu conversion funnel breakdown — from search query to puja video delivered. 6 steps, 4 leaks, 1 surprise. For India audience + indie hackers. <!-- 153/280 -->

2/9 Step 1 — Landing: 100% (baseline). Mostly via "rudrabhishek booking near me" / "online puja varanasi" / direct. Bounce rate 41% — better than I expected for a religious-commerce category. <!-- 213/280 -->

3/9 Step 2 — Browse pujas: 59% of landings. The /pujas page is the choke point. Devotees want to read the puja description, check the pandit photo, see sample video. We added all three. <!-- 209/280 -->

4/9 Step 3 — Open puja detail: 34%. The 25-point drop is mostly intent filter: "I came here to read about Rudrabhishek, not buy one." That's fine — they often return. We track repeat visits at 7d/30d. <!-- 230/280 -->

5/9 Step 4 — Click "Book now": 19%. Big leak here. Top reason from session replays (anonymized): pricing surprise. Devotees expect ₹251, see ₹1500-3500. We added the price breakdown card. Leak narrowed to ~15%. <!-- 251/280 -->

6/9 Step 5 — Complete checkout (Razorpay): 11%. Leak: OTP / UPI handoff. We can't fix Razorpay UI, but we added a "save your booking, pay later" option that recaptured ~30% of dropouts within 24h. <!-- 232/280 -->

7/9 Step 6 — Video proof delivered: 10.6%. The 0.4% gap between booking and delivery is pandits flagging gotra / muhurat issues that we resolve before puja day. Better to delay than to ship wrong. <!-- 232/280 -->

8/9 The surprise: NRI users have a different funnel. They land less (4%) but convert at 3.2% end-to-end vs 1.1% domestic. NRI ARPU is ~9x domestic. NRI funnel discipline matters more per visitor. <!-- 235/280 -->

9/9 The funnel is honest and the leaks are fixable. Want to walk it yourself? bhaktisetu.com/pujas — and reply with any UX friction you hit. We read every one. <!-- 175/280 -->
