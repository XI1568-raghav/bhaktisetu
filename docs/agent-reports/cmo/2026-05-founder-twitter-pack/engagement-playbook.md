# Engagement Playbook — Founder Twitter

## Reply SLA

- **First 4 hours** after a thread posts: reply to every comment within **2 hours**. This is when X's algorithm decides whether to surface the thread further. Early-engagement multipliers are real.
- **Hours 4-24:** reply within 6 hours.
- **After 24h:** reply within 24h or skip (replying to a 3-day-old comment looks needy).

## DM funnel template

When someone replies with intent ("I'd love to try this" / "Do you serve LA?" / "Send me the link"), move it to DM. Reply publicly first ("DM'ing you details"), then send:

```
Hi [name] — thanks for engaging on the thread.

Quick start:
- Browse pujas: https://bhaktisetu.com/pujas
- Live darshan schedule: https://bhaktisetu.com/today
- NRI gift puja (USD): https://bhaktisetu.com/gift

If you have a specific occasion (birthday, anniversary, shradh), reply and I'll suggest the right seva.

— [founder name]
```

Do NOT auto-DM. Manual only.

## Quote-tweet vs reply

- **Reply** when the comment is a question, a small reaction, or feedback you want to acknowledge.
- **Quote-tweet** when (a) someone shares a Bhaktisetu booking publicly with a positive review, (b) a builder asks a stack question that deserves a longer answer, or (c) a competitor or commentator says something that sets up a clean stat or story. Quote-tweets amplify the original poster — use sparingly so it doesn't feel performative.

## Blocklist criteria

Block (don't mute, don't ignore) accounts that:

1. Use sectarian or communal slurs in replies.
2. Argue that puja booking is "fake devotion" or attack pandits personally.
3. Spam the same comment across multiple threads.
4. DM with crypto / forex / "growth hacking" pitches.

Mute (not block) accounts that are merely off-topic or low-quality but not hostile.

## What to do when a thread underperforms

- <50 impressions in 4h: post a follow-up reply with the strongest single insight as a standalone tweet, link back to the thread.
- 50-500 impressions: leave it. Not every thread hits.
- 500+ impressions, low replies: ask a direct question in the last reply ("which of these resonated? reply with a number") — turns lurkers into commenters.

## What to do when a thread takes off

- Pin to profile for 7 days.
- Repost (native retweet) at 36h to catch the second timezone wave.
- Convert the top 2 insights into a LinkedIn post (different audience, similar copy).
- Reply with the next thread's hook as a teaser — chains compound.

## Voice guardrails

- Always say "we" when describing the team (even if it's a solo founder + a few contractors). Investors and customers read "I" as fragility.
- Never share booking numbers, donor names, or pandit personal details — even anonymized. The trust contract with users is absolute.
- It's fine to share aggregate numbers (MRR, NRI mix, booking count). It's not fine to share customer-identifying stories without explicit written consent.
