# Outreach copy — 3 DM variants + follow-up

> Send from founder personal handle, NOT brand account. Warmer, higher response rate per spec.
> Each variant ≤120 words. Subject lines are for email-channel fallback (some creators only respond via business email).
> Replace `{{token}}` placeholders before sending. Tokens are intentionally generic so the founder fills per candidate context.

---

### V1 — Warm (for creators who already mention puja booking pain)

**Use when:** candidate has posted within last 90 days about temple-distance friction, NRI puja struggle, finding pandits abroad, or remote darshan limitations.

**Subject (email fallback):** Saw your {{post_topic}} post — quick thought from a fellow bhakta

**DM body:**

Namaste {{first_name}},

Saw your post about {{post_topic_specific}} — really resonated. I'm building Darshya (darshya.online), a verified-pandit puja booking platform with mandatory video proof for every ceremony. We solve exactly what you described.

Would love to send you a free puja (any deity, any temple in our network — pick whatever you'd actually do) so you can experience the flow firsthand. If it's useful, only then would I ask if you'd share with your audience — no pressure either way.

Are you open to it?

— {{founder_first_name}}
Founder, Darshya

---

**5-day follow-up (if no response):**

Hi {{first_name}}, circling back gently — the free puja offer still stands. Even if it's not a fit, would love your raw feedback on the flow. Have a good week. — {{founder_first_name}}

### V2 — Cold value-first (for creators with no signal of puja pain — lead with offer)

**Use when:** candidate is generic devotional/bhakti content (mantra-recitation, sanskrit-learning, temple-vlog) without specific booking-pain signal.

**Subject (email fallback):** Free verified puja for you — no strings

**DM body:**

Namaste {{first_name}},

I run Darshya (darshya.online) — we book pujas at verified temples across India and ship video proof of every ceremony. Your {{niche}} content is exactly the audience we serve.

Reply with the puja you'd like and your sankalp details. I'll set it up this week.

**5-day follow-up:**

Hi {{first_name}}, just bumping this in case it got buried — free puja still on the table, no strings. Happy to skip the follow-up too if you'd prefer. — {{founder_first_name}}

**DM body:**

## Tone notes (apply to all 3 variants)

- **No emojis.** Devotional audience reads tonally serious; emojis cheapen.
- **No hard CTAs.** "Would love to" beats "Click here to". "Are you open to it?" beats "Reply yes!".
- **Name-drop competitors carefully.** Don't say "we're better than Sri Mandir" in cold outreach — creators will read it as insecurity.
- **Be specific about what you saw.** `{{post_topic_specific}}` matters. Generic warm-DMs read as templated.
- **Sign with first name only.** Not "Best regards, Founder & CEO" — too corporate for this niche.
- **Mention darshya.online once.** Don't link it three times.
