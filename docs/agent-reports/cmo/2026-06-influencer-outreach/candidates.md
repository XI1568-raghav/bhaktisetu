## Vetting gate (all 5 must pass)

1. **Follower count** 5K-50K (record exact at time of vet, screenshot URL).
2. **≥3 devotional posts in last 30 days** (puja/temple/bhakti/sanskrit/dharma content — not generic spirituality or yoga-only).
3. **Engagement rate ≥3%** = average likes on last 5 posts / follower count × 100. Calc manually.
4. **NRI audience signal** — bio mentions USA/UK/Canada/Australia/diaspora/NRI OR ≥30% of comments mix English with Devanagari/Tamil/Telugu/Bengali script OR creator self-identifies as living abroad.
5. **Reachable** — public IG DM OR business email visible in bio. Reject if DM closed AND no email.

Any fail → reject, document reason in tracker `notes` column. Overshoot 15 → 10 confirmed; ~30% candidate-to-partnership conversion expected for cold outreach.

## Vetting tips (founder time-savers)

- **Engagement-rate calc shortcut**: open last 5 posts in tabs, eyeball likes, average mentally, divide by follower count. Don't burn 20 min on precision — 3% vs 3.4% is noise, 1% vs 5% is signal.
- **Reject fast on bot signal**: if last 5 posts each have ~exact same like count (e.g. 142/144/141/143/140), buyer or bot pod — reject.
- **NRI audience confirm**: open 3 random posts, scan top 10 comments. If you see consistent English-Devanagari mix or US/UK timestamps (PT/EST in bio responses), pass.
- **Bonus signal**: if creator already runs a paid offering (course, ebook, paid newsletter), they're commercially literate — outreach response rate is ~2x higher.
