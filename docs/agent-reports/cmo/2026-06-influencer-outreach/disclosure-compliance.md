# Disclosure compliance — ASCI India + FTC US

> Mandatory disclosure for every paid, barter, or rev-share partnership post. Non-compliance puts both the creator and Darshya at regulatory risk.
> This is a working compliance note for the founder + creators. Not a substitute for legal review; for complex cross-jurisdiction edge cases, consult an advertising-law attorney.

## ASCI India guideline (Advertising Standards Council of India)

**Applicable to:** every creator with audience in India, regardless of where the creator lives.

**Rule:** ASCI's 2021 Guidelines for Influencer Advertising on Digital Media require a clear, upfront disclosure label on any post where the creator has received material consideration (cash, free product, free service, discount, gift, family trip, etc.).

**Acceptable labels (any one of these is sufficient):**

- `#ad`
- `#advertisement`
- `#sponsored`
- `#partnership`
- `#collab`
- `#promo`
- `#paidpartnership`

**Placement rules:**

- Must appear in the first 1-2 lines of the caption (above the "more" fold).
- Must NOT be hidden in a hashtag block at the end mixed with 30 other tags.
- For video content (reels, shorts, YouTube): also include a visible on-screen text overlay in the first 3 seconds OR a verbal mention.
- For Stories: use the official "Paid partnership" tag in Instagram's tools (built-in feature), OR add `#ad` text sticker.

**Free puja or barter explicitly counts as "material consideration" under ASCI** — disclosure required even if no cash changes hands.

## FTC US guideline (Federal Trade Commission)

**Applicable to:** any creator whose audience includes US viewers (typically anyone with NRI USA reach or US-based subscribers).

**Rule:** FTC's Endorsement Guides (16 CFR Part 255) require "clear and conspicuous" disclosure of any material connection between an advertiser and an endorser.

**Acceptable labels:**

- `#ad` (preferred — FTC explicitly endorses this)
- `#sponsored`
- "Paid partnership with @darshyaofficial" (preferred for video voiceover)

**Placement rules (stricter than ASCI):**

- Must appear in the first 3 lines of caption — NOT collapsed under "more".
- Must be in the same language as the post (i.e., if caption is English, disclosure is English; if Hindi caption, `#विज्ञापन` works).
- For YouTube videos: voiced verbal disclosure in first 30 seconds is the gold standard. Description-only disclosure is insufficient.
- For Instagram: native "Paid partnership" tag handles it cleanly.
- Avoid: `#thanksDarshya`, `#sp`, `#partner` — FTC has explicitly called these out as insufficient.

## What founder includes in every outreach

Paste this clause into the outreach DM/email once the partnership is confirmed (not in the initial cold DM — too transactional):

> "Standard compliance note: every post for Darshya needs to be tagged with `#ad` or `#partnership` per ASCI India guidelines. If your audience includes US viewers (NRI reach), please also put `#ad` in the first 3 lines per FTC. For Reels/Shorts: a verbal mention or on-screen 'Paid partnership' overlay in the first 3 seconds. Instagram's native 'Paid partnership' tag handles both cleanly if you'd prefer that route. Let me know if any of this is unclear."

## What happens if a creator skips disclosure

1. **First offense:** founder DMs the creator privately, requests edit within 24h.
2. **No edit within 24h:** founder reports the post to Instagram's "Paid partnership" review tool (Instagram will retroactively flag it as undisclosed advertising).
3. **Repeat offense:** partnership terminated; creator removed from future cycles; promo code disabled; pending rev-share payout withheld pending compliance.
4. **High-severity (creator denies sponsorship publicly):** founder issues a public clarification post on Darshya's brand account citing the partnership. Consult lawyer if creator escalates further.

## Disclosure language for Darshya's own posts (when re-sharing creator content)

When Darshya re-shares a creator's post to brand account:

> "🤝 In partnership with @[creator_handle] — sharing their experience booking [puja_name] through Darshya. #partnership"

## Audit trail (founder maintains)

Keep screenshots of every creator's post within 48 hours of going live. If a post is later edited or deleted, the screenshot is the only evidence the disclosure was originally compliant. Store in a folder: `2026-06-influencer-outreach/proof/{handle}-{post_id}.png`.

## Edge cases

- **Story-only posts (24h expiry):** still need `#ad` text sticker. Save screenshot before expiry.
- **Multiple platforms cross-posted (IG → YouTube short):** disclosure required on each platform independently. Native tag on one doesn't satisfy the other.
- **Giveaways / contests funded by Darshya:** treat as sponsored. Full disclosure required even if creator presents it as their own initiative.
- **Negative review (creator dislikes the puja experience):** the creator is free to post a negative review; disclosure is still required because they received the product. Better to find out about poor experiences early than to silence them.
