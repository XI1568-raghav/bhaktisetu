# Creative 03 — 80G / 501c3 Tax Receipt (NRI donor)

## ⚠️ Founder legal check before launch

- **80G** is an **Indian Income Tax Act** deduction. NRIs with Indian taxable income can claim it; US-only residents with no Indian taxable income cannot.
- For **US donors** to claim a US federal deduction, the receiving entity must be a **501(c)(3)** US charity OR donations must route through a US fiscal sponsor.
- Until Bhaktisetu has a US fiscal sponsor or 501c3 partner, this creative must say "Section 80G (India) where eligible" — NOT promise a US tax deduction.
- **If no 501c3 partner exists yet → swap this creative for a 2nd gift-puja angle (e.g. festival puja) before launch.** Founder must confirm before going live.
