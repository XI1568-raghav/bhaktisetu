# Qualification Rubric — Gaushala Onboarding (R-Q1-S-008)

5-dimension scoring, 1-5 each. Total max 25 (un-weighted) or apply weights below for weighted max 100.

## Dimensions

### 1. Legal status (weight x4 - hardest gate)

| Score | Criteria |
|---|---|
| 5 | 12A + 80G + state Gau Seva Aayog registration all current and valid |
| 4 | 12A + 80G current; state Aayog pending renewal |
| 3 | 12A current; 80G application in progress |
| 2 | Society/trust registration only; no 12A yet |
| 1 | No formal registration; informal community gaushala |

### 2. Transparency (weight x3)

| Score | Criteria |
|---|---|
| 5 | Annual audit published online, monthly donor reports, named trustees, public bank account |
| 4 | Annual audit available on request, quarterly donor reports |
| 3 | Annual audit available, ad-hoc donor reports |
| 2 | No published audit, willing to share on request |
| 1 | No reporting practice; refuses to discuss financials |

### 3. Cow welfare practice (weight x3)

| Score | Criteria |
|---|---|
| 5 | Veterinary care on-site, individual cow records, no dairy commercial sale, no cow trading, separated bulls/cows/calves housing |
| 4 | Visiting vet weekly, group records by herd, occasional milk donation to community |
| 3 | Monthly vet visits, basic record keeping, some milk sale for gaushala funding |
| 2 | Vet only on emergency, no individual records, commercial dairy operation alongside |
| 1 | No vet protocol, cows in poor condition visible, mixed with commercial operation |

### 4. Digital readiness (weight x1 - we can train)

| Score | Criteria |
|---|---|

| 4 | Website + WhatsApp + UPI |
| 3 | WhatsApp + UPI + occasional email |
| 2 | WhatsApp only, bank transfer for donations |
| 1 | Phone only, cash/check donations |

### 5. Geographic fit (weight x1 - already filtered)

| Score | Criteria |
|---|---|
| 5 | Pathmeda / Vrindavan / Mathura / Govardhan / Barsana / Kurukshetra |
| 4 | Hisar / Rohtak / Karnal / Jaipur |
| 3 | Other Rajasthan / UP / Haryana within 100km of anchor |
| 2 | Adjacent state border (Punjab, MP, Gujarat) - case by case |
| 1 | Out of scope geography (Chennai, Pune, Kolkata, etc.) - DROP |

## Total scoring and GO/HOLD/DROP thresholds

**Un-weighted total (out of 25):**
- 22-25: GO immediately, fast-track to onboarding
- 16-21: GO with conditions (clarify gaps in first call)
- 11-15: HOLD - revisit in 60 days after improvement
- 5-10: DROP - not aligned

**Weighted total (out of 100):**
- weighted = legal*4 + transparency*3 + welfare*3 + digital*1 + geo*1
- 80-100: GO immediately
- 60-79: GO with conditions
- 40-59: HOLD
- 12-39: DROP

## Hard DROP triggers (any one = DROP regardless of total)

1. Legal status = 1 (no formal registration)
2. Cow welfare = 1 (visible mistreatment, commercial-only)
3. Geographic fit = 1 (out of scope city)
4. Sampradaya/lineage cannot be verified at all
5. Recent news of financial scandal or animal cruelty case
6. Refuses to sign data-sharing partnership agreement
