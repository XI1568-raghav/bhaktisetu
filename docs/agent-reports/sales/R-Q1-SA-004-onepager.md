## Darshya at a glance

**Darshya.online** is a pan-India devotional super-app connecting NRI / diaspora Hindus with verified pujas, live darshan, temple donations, gaushala seva, and pilgrim yatra packages — every transaction issued with a video-proof artifact and (where eligible) 80G tax receipt.

- **Live**: darshya.online (web), iOS + Android shipping Q2 2026 (TestFlight + Play closed-beta now)
- **Coverage**: 30+ partner temples across 25+ Indian cities, expanding to 100+ by end-Q3
- **NRI lane**: dual-currency checkout (INR / USD / GBP / CAD / AUD), gift-puja recipient WhatsApp delivery, 80G receipts for India-side donations

## The diaspora pain we solve

| Pain | Today | With Darshya |
|---|---|---|
| Funding pujas back home | Informal WhatsApp networks, no receipts, no proof | Online booking, video-proof, 80G receipt |
| Gaushala / temple donations | Wire transfer + trust-based | Verified donee, monthly impact report |
| Pilgrimage planning | Multiple agents, language barriers | Curated yatra packages with English support |
| 80G tax compliance | Hand-collected paper receipts | Auto-issued digital receipts (DR-YYYYMMDD-XXXX format) |
| Gift puja for elders in India | Phone call to relative, ad-hoc | Recipient gets WhatsApp link + video on completion |

## Why partnership with HAF / Sewa unlocks scale

Your members are exactly the audience we struggle to reach via paid acquisition:

- **Trust gap**: NRI donors are (rightly) cautious about online India-side platforms. A trusted diaspora-org endorsement collapses 6-12 months of organic trust-building.
- **Receipt clarity**: HAF / Sewa already navigate US 501(c)(3) + India 80G landscape — partnership lets us co-package compliance for your members in a way no India-side platform can do alone.
- **Mission alignment**: Both orgs invest in temple preservation, dharma education, civic engagement — every transaction on Darshya funds verified Indian temples and traditional practitioners.

## What partnership looks like (proposal — refined on call)

1. **Referral revenue share**: per-transaction commission on member-attributed bookings (gift-puja, yatra, gaushala donation). Slab finalized post intro-call.
2. **Co-branded landing**: darshya.online/partners/<org-slug> with partner branding, mission alignment statement, transparent per-transaction breakdown.
3. **Member benefits**: small discount or "first puja free" type onboarding incentive for member households (founder decision, exact incentive TBD).
4. **Impact transparency**: monthly partner dashboard — pujas funded, temples supported, donations processed, attributed via member channel.
5. **Compliance handshake**: co-document the 80G / 501(c)(3) pathways for partner members on a shared FAQ page.
