# R-Q1-S-011 — Qualification Rubric

> Scoring after 15-min discovery call. Each criterion 0-3 pts. Max 21 pts. Threshold = 14 pts to onboard.

## Scoring grid

| Criterion | 0 (drop) | 1 (weak) | 2 (acceptable) | 3 (strong) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **GST status** | Not registered, not willing | Promised within 30d | Registered, willing to share | Active GSTIN, files monthly |
| **Shipping / COD capable** | No shipping, no willingness | Local-only, no logistics | Tied to one local courier | Pan-India multi-courier history |
| **Inventory size** | < 20 SKUs | 20-50 SKUs | 50-200 SKUs | 200+ SKUs, deep catalog |
| **Digital readiness** | No smartphone, paper-only | Owner uses WhatsApp | Has POS / accounting software | Active on JustDial/IndiaMART/own site |
| **Price competitiveness** | 20%+ above market | 10-20% above | At market | 5-15% below for our volume |
| **Hygiene (prasad only)** | No FSSAI, visible issues | FSSAI pending, basic clean | FSSAI active, clean kitchen | FSSAI + audit history + cold-chain |
| **Years in business** | < 2 yrs | 2-5 yrs | 5-15 yrs | 15+ yrs heritage |

## Category-specific gates

### Prasad (mandatory)

- **FSSAI license active** = hard gate. No FSSAI = drop or send to "needs-founder-verify" hold.
- **Cold-chain capable** if shipping beyond city = T2 acceptable, T1 requires pan-India proof.
- **Packaging SOP** demo at walk-in. Sealed, tamper-evident, labeled.

### Brass (mandatory)

- **Weight classification** per SKU. >5kg = flag for logistics-partner roadmap, not in main onboard.
- **Custom-order capacity** = scoring bonus (devotee gotra/family engraving = premium SKU).

### Texts (mandatory)

- **Language coverage** = scoring bonus (Sanskrit + 2+ regional = T1).
- **Returns policy alignment** — damage-only must be accepted.

## Decision matrix

| Score | Decision |
|---|---|
| 18-21 | T1 — onboard immediately, founder-managed |
| 14-17 | T2 — onboard w/ shipping/digital support plan |
| 10-13 | Hold list — revisit Q2 after gap-close |
| < 10 | Drop — politely decline |

## Mandatory note fields (capture during call)

- Decision-maker name + role (owner / son / manager)
- Best contact channel (WA / call / email)
- Existing platforms (JustDial, IndiaMART, own site, Flipkart, Amazon)
- Current monthly order volume + average ticket size
- Peak season (festival months)
- Hygiene certifications photo / scan (prasad)
- 3 sample SKU photos + price list

## Red flags (auto-drop regardless of score)

1. Asks for cash-only / no GST invoice
2. Refuses returns policy
3. History of FSSAI suspension (prasad)
4. Demands exclusivity from day 1
5. Pricing > 20% above market w/o justified premium
6. Cannot share GSTIN within 7 days post-call
