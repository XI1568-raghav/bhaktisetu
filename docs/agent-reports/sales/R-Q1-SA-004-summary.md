## Why these two orgs

Both are the largest US-based Hindu/diaspora civic organizations with established 501(c)(3) status, mature donor networks, and recurring temple/charity partnership programs. Engagement with either unlocks:

- **80G + 501(c)(3) co-receipt clarity** for US donors funding India puja / gaushala / yatra programs
- **Diaspora gift-puja referral pipeline** — HAF / Sewa member newsletters reach tens of thousands of NRI households who currently fund pujas via informal WhatsApp networks
- **Partnership credibility** — listed-partner status on a recognized diaspora org page is a moat against new Indian-side competitors trying to enter the NRI lane

## Partnership angle (single pitch)

1. **Referral commission**: per-transaction commission on any gift-puja, yatra package, or gaushala donation originated via HAF / Sewa member channel. Commission slab to be finalized on intro call (typical industry range: 5-15% net of payment processing).
2. **80G compliance bundle**: India-side 80G receipts for partner temples + US-side 501(c)(3) co-issuance pathway (where structurally possible via partner's fiscal sponsorship or pass-through grant model). Removes the most-cited blocker for US donor giving to India.
3. **Co-branded NRI landing page** on darshya.online for each partner — drives SEO + donor trust + partner reporting transparency.
4. **Aggregated impact reporting** for partner: monthly dashboard of pujas funded, temples supported, gaushalas fed via partner-attributed traffic.
