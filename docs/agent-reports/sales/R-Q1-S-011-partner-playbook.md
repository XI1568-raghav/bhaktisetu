# R-Q1-S-011 — Partner Playbook

> Operating manual for store-owner partners post-onboarding.
> Covers commission, settlement, returns, packaging, listing.

## 1. Commission models

### Default — volume-tiered (recommended)

| Monthly GMV | Prasad | Brass | Texts |
|---|---|---|---|
| < ₹50K | 12% | 15% | 10% |
| ₹50K-2L | 10% | 13% | 9% |
| ₹2L+ | 8% | 11% | 8% |

### Alternative 1 — flat % (simpler, T2 partners)

- Prasad: 10% flat
- Brass: 13% flat
- Texts: 9% flat

### Alternative 2 — freemium listing (low-volume specialty partners)

- ₹0 commission first 3 months on first 50 orders
- Standard tiered rates kick in month 4
- Use case: niche Sanskrit publishers, heritage brass workshops

### Decision rule

- T1 partners → volume-tiered (incentivizes scale)
- T2 partners → flat % (simpler accounting)
- Specialty / heritage → freemium-listing first 3 months

## 2. Settlement cadence

| Cadence | Partner choice | Cash-flow impact |
|---|---|---|
| **T+7** | Default for prasad (perishable, fast turnover) | Better cash-flow for partner |
| **T+14** | Default for brass (return window 7d) | Better risk-cover for platform |
| **T+30** | Available on request | Texts (low margin, batched) |

**Settlement mechanics**:
- Razorpay Route or manual bank transfer (first 3 months)
- Statement email + WA on settlement day
- Settlement reconciliation portal (admin dashboard) — Q2 deliverable
- Disputes resolved within 3 business days

## 3. Returns policy

| Category | Window | Conditions | Refund |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Prasad** | None | Non-returnable (FSSAI hygiene) | Refund only if delivery failed or visibly damaged on arrival (photo proof < 24h) |
| **Brass** | 7 days | Unused, original packaging, no engraving | Full refund minus return shipping; partner restocks |
| **Texts** | Damage-only | Pages torn / printing defect / wrong edition | Replacement first, refund if no stock |

**Customer-side notes**:
- Returns initiated via order page button
- Pickup arranged by platform (partner doesn't coordinate)
- Refund processed within 7 days of pickup confirmation

**Partner-side notes**:
- Damage rate target < 2% per quarter
- > 5% damage rate triggers packaging audit
- Repeated abuse → partnership review

## 4. Packaging SOP — Prasad (mandatory)

### Hygiene gates

1. FSSAI license number printed on every package
2. Date of preparation + best-before date (max 7 days from prep for sweets, 3 days for laddoo/peda)
3. Ingredients list in English + Hindi
4. Allergen warnings (nuts, dairy, gluten)

### Packaging materials

- Food-grade plastic / wax-paper inner wrap
- Sealed tamper-evident outer box (corrugated or kraft)
- Bubble-wrap layer for fragile items (chhena, milk-based)
- Cold-pack for shipments > 24h transit (summer mandatory)

### Labeling

- Front: shop name + product + weight + best-before
- Back: FSSAI + ingredients + allergens + partner address + Darshya order ID + customer support number
- Side: "Handle with care" / "Keep refrigerated" (where applicable)

### Cold-chain (shipments > 200km or summer)

- Insulated foam box + 2x gel packs
- Courier: Bluedart Temperature Controlled / Delhivery Express
- Transit window: < 36 hours guaranteed
- Failed cold-chain = full refund to customer, partner debit waived (first 2 incidents)

## 5. Packaging SOP — Brass

- Bubble-wrap each item individually
- Cardboard layer between item and outer box
- Outer box rating: 5-ply corrugated for items > 2kg
- Heavy items (3-5kg): extra padding + double box
- Items > 5kg: flag for logistics-partner roadmap, not standard
- Fragile sticker mandatory
- Insurance recommended for items > ₹5000 retail

## 6. Packaging SOP — Texts

- Cardboard sleeve to prevent corner damage
- Plastic bubble-mailer for single books
- Carton for orders > 3 books
- Moisture-resistant wrap for monsoon season (Jul-Sep)

## 8. FSSAI guidance (prasad partners)

### If FSSAI registered
- Share license number + scanned copy
- Verify online via fssai.gov.in (ops responsibility)
- Display number on every package

### If FSSAI not registered
- Direct partner to fssai.gov.in registration portal
- Basic registration (< ₹12L turnover): ₹100/year
- State license (₹12L-20Cr): ₹2000-5000/year
- Central license (> ₹20Cr): ₹7500/year
- Partner cannot go live until FSSAI active
- Founder may support registration paperwork (founder-action-required)

### Cold-chain compliance
- Sweets w/ dairy (chhena, peda, barfi) require FSSAI cold-storage compliance
- Mandatory for shipments > 200km
- Partner declares cold-chain capability at onboarding
- Audit photos required (kitchen + storage) — uploaded to partner profile

## 9. Brass item logistics

### Standard ship (≤5kg)
- Delhivery / Bluedart / DTDC
- Platform negotiates rates with courier (better than partner's local)
- Partner packs, courier picks up from store (free pickup)
- Tracking auto-shared with customer

### Heavy ship (>5kg)
- **Flag for separate logistics-partner roadmap (not in this onboarding)**
- Hold-list these SKUs in partner profile (mark `heavy_logistics_pending`)
- Surface to customer only after logistics partner onboarded
- Estimated Q2 2026 deliverable

## 10. Texts subscription model (recurring revenue)

- Devotee subscribes to "Daily Gita verse pothi" / "Monthly Ramayan chapter" / "Vrat-katha bundle"
- Platform aggregates demand by region
- Bulk shipment to partner monthly
- Partner ships individual SKUs (or platform-managed fulfillment via Q3 2026)
- Commission on subscription = 8-10% recurring
- Partner sees subscriber count + churn dashboard

## 12. Termination clauses

- Either party may terminate with 30-day notice
- Outstanding settlements paid within 14 days of termination
- Customer orders in-flight honored or refunded
- Partner inventory listings removed within 7 days
- No exclusivity, no penalty
