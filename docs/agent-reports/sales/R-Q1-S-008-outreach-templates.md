## 3. 90-second call script

### English

**0-15s — Opener:**
"Namaste [contact_name] ji, this is [founder_name] from Bhaktisetu. Is this a good 90 seconds? I called specifically because of [gaushala_name]'s reputation for [specific praise]."

**15-45s — Value prop:**
"We are a devotional platform connecting Indian and NRI Hindu devotees with verified gaushalas through monthly adopt-a-cow. Unlike one-time campaigns, we build a recurring donor pipeline. NRIs pay in USD at 9-10x rates. Zero onboarding fee, transparent reporting back to donors with photos and updates."

**45-75s — Ask:**
"We are selecting only five gaushalas this quarter. I'd like a 30-minute follow-up to walk through how reporting works, what cow welfare data you already track, and whether your 80G registration is current. Would Tuesday or Thursday this week work, morning or evening?"

**75-90s — Close + objection prep:**
- If YES: "Wonderful. I'll send a WhatsApp confirmation in 10 minutes with a Zoom link."
- If hesitant: "I completely understand. May I send you a one-page brief on email or WhatsApp so you can review at your pace? What's the best address?"
- If NO: "Thank you for your time. May I check back in two months as we expand?"

### Hindi (Devanagari)

**0-15s — शुरुआत:**
"नमस्ते [contact_name] जी, मैं [founder_name] भक्तिसेतु से बात कर रहा हूँ। क्या 90 सेकंड का समय है? मैंने विशेष रूप से [gaushala_name] की [विशिष्ट प्रशंसा] की प्रतिष्ठा के कारण call किया।"

**15-45s — मूल्य प्रस्ताव:**
"हम एक devotional platform हैं जो भारतीय और NRI हिंदू भक्तों को मासिक adopt-a-cow के माध्यम से सत्यापित गौशालाओं से जोड़ता है। एक-बार के अभियानों के विपरीत, हम नियमित दानदाता पाइपलाइन बनाते हैं। NRI USD में 9-10 गुना दरों पर भुगतान करते हैं। कोई onboarding शुल्क नहीं, दानदाताओं को फोटो और updates के साथ पारदर्शी रिपोर्टिंग।"

**45-75s — माँग:**
"हम इस तिमाही में केवल पाँच गौशालाओं का चयन कर रहे हैं। मैं 30-मिनट का follow-up चाहता हूँ — रिपोर्टिंग कैसे काम करती है, आप पहले से कौन-सा गौ-कल्याण data track करते हैं, और क्या आपका 80G पंजीकरण मान्य है। इस सप्ताह मंगलवार या गुरुवार, सुबह या शाम सुविधाजनक है?"

**75-90s — समापन + आपत्ति-तैयारी:**
- हाँ: "बहुत अच्छा। 10 मिनट में Zoom link के साथ WhatsApp confirmation भेजता हूँ।"
- संकोच: "बिल्कुल समझता हूँ। क्या मैं email या WhatsApp पर एक-पृष्ठ का brief भेज सकता हूँ? सबसे अच्छा पता क्या है?"
- नहीं: "आपके समय के लिए धन्यवाद। क्या मैं दो माह बाद विस्तार के समय फिर संपर्क कर सकता हूँ?"

## 4. Objection handlers

### Trust / "Who are you, never heard of Bhaktisetu"

"That's a fair question. We are darshya.online, live since 2026. We work with verified temples for puja booking and live darshan. You can verify us via our website, our Razorpay merchant ID, and we are happy to provide our 12A/80G registration if helpful. We are not asking for any payment from you — only a partnership where donors flow to you."

### Fees / "What does it cost us"

"Zero onboarding fee. Zero commission on any legacy donors you bring to the platform. Only 20-30% platform commission on net-new donors we acquire for you — this covers payment processing, donor support, and reporting infrastructure. You receive 70-80% of every new recurring donation, with full transparency on each transaction."

### Tech-savviness / "We don't have computer team"

"Completely understood. The only thing we need from your side is monthly photos and a one-line update per cow that has been adopted — can be done from any WhatsApp. Our team handles all the technology, donor communication, and receipt generation. We have onboarded gaushalas with no internet team before."

### Donor data privacy / "Will donors contact us directly"

"Donors see your gaushala name, location, and cow details. Donor contact is held by Bhaktisetu and not shared without your consent. You receive aggregated reports — 'this month 47 new donors adopted 12 cows at your gaushala, generating Rs.X' — without any individual contact spam to your office. If a donor specifically requests a temple visit, we coordinate with you first."

**For your gaushala:**
- Monthly recurring donor pipeline (not one-time campaigns)
- Zero onboarding fee, zero commission on legacy donors
- 20-30% platform commission only on net-new donors we bring you
- 70-80% of every new recurring donation flows to you
- Transparent transaction-level reporting
- 80G receipt automation (if your registration is current)
- Donor communication and support handled by Bhaktisetu
- NRI lane pays in USD at 9-10x domestic rates

**For donors:**
- Monthly adopt-a-cow at Rs.500-2500/month domestic or $25-100/month NRI
- Photos and one-line cow updates monthly
- 80G receipt issued automatically
- Verified gaushalas only (we audit before listing)
- Stop / pause / change cow anytime

**Onboarding ask from gaushala:**
- Verified registration documents (12A/80G/state Gau Seva Aayog)
- 5-10 cow photos with names and brief stories
- Monthly photo + one-line update commitment per adopted cow
- Bank account for donor transfers (NEFT/UPI/RTGS)
- Single point-of-contact at gaushala (manager or trustee)
