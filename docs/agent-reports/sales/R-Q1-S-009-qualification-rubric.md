# Qualification Rubric — R-Q1-S-009 Gurukul / Vedic Pathshala

> Score each prospect 0–100 across five weighted axes. Use the score to assign a priority tier and decide outreach order. Gurukul decisions are slower than gaushala — disqualifiers (Section 6) are strict and override aggregate score.

## 1. Parampara legitimacy — weight 30

| Score | Criteria |
|---|---|
| 25–30 | Documented unbroken guru-shishya parampara; institution registered with Sansthan / Pratishthan / sampradaya apex body (RSS, MSRVVP, ISKCON Vrindavan Education Committee, Chinmaya International Foundation, Ashta-Matha, Arya Samaj central body). |
| 15–24 | Recognised lineage but secondary documentation (sampradaya directory listing, news coverage, peer-pathshala references). |
| 5–14 | Lineage claimed but only single-source verification; founded < 25 years ago and not yet a self-perpetuating institution. |
| 0–4 | No documented parampara, or "guru" with no traceable initiation lineage. **Hard disqualifier** (see Section 6). |

## 2. Student count — weight 20

| Score | Criteria |
|---|---|
| 17–20 | ≥ 200 residential brahmacharis / vedadhyayis under active instruction. |
| 11–16 | 75–199 students. |
| 5–10 | 25–74 students. |
| 0–4 | < 25 students OR unable to provide any figure (even `est?`). |

Note: small pathshalas (Tier C) are still valuable — they often have the deepest parampara purity. Score this axis honestly, then weight against parampara legitimacy.

## 3. Financial transparency — weight 20

| Score | Criteria |
|---|---|
| 17–20 | 80G regn current; PAN issued in institution name; published annual report or audited statement; willing to share donor-attribution ledger. |
| 11–16 | 80G + PAN present; audit on request only. |
| 5–10 | 80G OR PAN missing; verbal accountability only. |
| 0–4 | Neither 80G nor institutional PAN; donations routed to individual accounts. **Hard disqualifier** (see Section 6). |

## 4. Digital readiness — weight 15

| Score | Criteria |
|---|---|
| 13–15 | Active website with current contact details; institutional email; willing to accept online donation flow; designated office person responds within 5 working days. |
| 8–12 | Website exists but stale; institutional email present; phone-first communication preferred. |
| 3–7 | No website OR only social-media presence; communication only via WhatsApp on personal numbers. |
| 0–2 | No reachable digital surface; outreach requires physical visit. (Not disqualifying — just deprioritized for Q1.) |

## 5. Geographic fit — weight 15

| Score | Criteria |
|---|---|
| 13–15 | Located in Varanasi, Vrindavan, Haridwar, or Bengaluru. (Primary Q1 geo focus per founder directive.) |
| 8–12 | Located in Mumbai AND meets Sandipani-grade / Somaiya-grade vetting bar. |
| 0–7 | Located outside the five-city allow-list. **Hard disqualifier for Q1** (see Section 6). |

## Tier assignment

| Tier | Score range | Action |
|---|---|---|
| **Tier A — Priority outreach** | ≥ 75 | Founder personal outreach within 7 days of dossier shipment. Goal: schedule call within D14. |
| **Tier B — Standard outreach** | 50–74 | Cadence outreach per `R-Q1-S-009-followup-cadence.md`. Founder steps in only if D14 touch lands. |
| **Tier C — Watch list** | < 50 | Quarterly check-in. Reconsider only if parampara legitimacy ≥ 25 and missing dimensions improve. |

## Disqualifiers (any one = drop from list)

1. **No traceable parampara.** "Self-realised" / "lineage-less" gurus. Bhaktisetu cannot vouch for institutions outside an established sampradaya.
2. **Pending legal or FCRA dispute.** If `gov.in` or sampradaya apex body has flagged the institution, defer indefinitely.
3. **Donations routed to individual accounts.** Bhaktisetu only routes funds under institutional 80G regn.
4. **Outside Q1 geo allow-list** (Varanasi / Vrindavan / Haridwar / Bengaluru / Mumbai-Sandipani-grade).
5. **Cross-sampradaya bundling demanded.** If the institution insists on being co-promoted with a different parampara on the same surface, drop — Bhaktisetu's brand commitment is sampradaya-purity.
6. **Refuses transparent ledger.** If the institution will not provide donor-attribution visibility to its own trustees, the trust gap is too large for Q1.
7. **History of fabricating student counts** or refusing to mark `est?` on unverified figures.
