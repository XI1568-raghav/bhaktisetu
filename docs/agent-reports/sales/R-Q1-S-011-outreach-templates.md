# R-Q1-S-011 — Outreach Templates

> 3 channels: WhatsApp opener, email body, walk-in pitch script.
> Categories: prasad, brass, texts. Each gets distinct value-prop.
> No commission % promised — range only ("8-15% per category, finalized post-call").

## 3. Walk-in pitch script (founder / feet-on-street)

> **Use language tags**: Hinglish core, switch to Bhojpuri (Varanasi) / Kannada (Bengaluru) / Marathi (Mumbai) for warmth.

### 3a. Opening (30 sec, walk-in)

> "Namaste {ji/sir/madam}, main Darshya.online se aaya hoon — hum pan-India devotional marketplace bana rahe hain.
>
> Aapki dukaan ka naam suna hai — {specific compliment: e.g., 'aapki peda ki taareef Kashi mein sabse upar hai' / 'aapki murti work bohot fine hai' / 'aapka Sanskrit collection unique hai'}.
>
> 2 minute baat ho sakti hai? Main aapse partnership ki ek opportunity discuss karne aaya hoon."

### 3b. Value-prop (90 sec, after engagement)

> **Prasad version**: "{Locality} ke devotees toh aap already serve karte hain. Hum aapko Mumbai, Bengaluru, Delhi ke devotees + US/UK NRI buyers se demand laa sakte hain — jo apne parivar ke naam pe puja-prasad gift karna chahte hain.
>
> Aap apni signature peda/laddoo banaate raho — packaging+shipping hum standardize karenge. FSSAI license aapke paas hai? Hygiene+packaging par ek SOP follow karna hoga, woh hum batayenge."
>
> **Brass version**: "Aapki murti+utensils ka quality bohot top-tier hai. Hum aapko pan-India + NRI demand connect dilwa sakte hain. 5kg se chhoti items aap ship kar sakte hain — bade items ke liye hum logistics partner alag se discuss karenge.
>
> Custom orders bhi accept kar sakte hain — devotees apne gotra/family name engrave karwana chahte hain, that's a premium SKU."
>
> **Texts version**: "Aapka collection — Sanskrit+regional language pothi+chalisa+stotra — devotees ke liye treasure hai. Hum subscription model launch kar rahe hain — devotees monthly Gita-chapter, daily vrat-katha pothi mangwate hain.
>
> Recurring orders, low margin, volume-driven. Aapke inventory ka best use hoga."

### 3c. Ask + close (60 sec)

> "Commission category-wise 8-15% range mein hota hai — exact slab aapki volume+packaging+shipping ke basis par baat karenge. Settlement T+7 ya T+14, aapki choice. Returns policy bhi flexible hai — prasad non-returnable, brass 7-day, texts sirf damage-only.
>
> Main aapse ek 15-20 min ka call ya in-person meeting fix karna chahta hoon is hafte — agar aap interested ho. Aap kab convenient ho sakte hain?
>
> {If yes}: Toh main aapka {phone/email} le leta hoon — kal/parson WhatsApp pe ek meeting link bhej dunga. Aap apna GST + FSSAI {if prasad} details ready rakhna.
>
> {If hesitant}: Koi baat nahin — main aapko ek WhatsApp pe brief catalog+commission table bhej dunga. Aap dekh ke decide kar lijiyega. Aap apna number share karenge?"

## Caveats (read before sending)

1. **No exact commission %** in any template — always range.
2. **No fake urgency** ("limited slots") — devotional vertical, trust-based.
3. **FSSAI must be asked upfront** for prasad — blank = needs-founder-verify.
4. **Brass >5kg items** — flag separately, don't promise shipping.
5. **Founder/field rep walks markets** — agent does NOT cold-call.
6. **Email subject line** must include city to avoid spam filters.
