**Bootstrap-to-pre-seed in 90 days. Founder Sunil Kumar to scale verified pandit + video proof platform.**

**BENGALURU, India — [DATE]:** Bhaktisetu Foundation, India's first verified-pandit puja booking platform with same-day video proof for both Indian and NRI Hindu families, today announced the close of its pre-seed funding round of $XXX, led by [LEAD INVESTOR] with participation from [Angel 1], [Angel 2], [Angel 3], and [Angel 4].

The company will use the funding to expand its verified pandit network from 100 to 500+ across India, launch on iOS and Android, and scale NRI customer acquisition across US, UK, Canada and Australia where dual-currency pricing has driven 65% Day-30 retention versus 42% domestic baseline.

Founded 18 months ago by former TCS engineer Sunil Kumar, Bhaktisetu launched in stealth mode with zero outside funding and reached 5,000+ monthly active users and ₹10L monthly recurring revenue in its first 90 days post-launch — entirely through programmatic SEO, an autonomous agent fleet, and word-of-mouth referrals.

"India has a $58 billion devotional wellness market, and most of it is still offline, opaque, and built on personal trust networks that don't extend to NRI families," said Sunil Kumar, Founder and CEO of Bhaktisetu Foundation. "When my brother in San Francisco needed to book a Rudrabhishek for our father in Bengaluru, he was paying ₹2000 to a WhatsApp contact and hoping for a photo. We're building the trust layer — verified pandit, same-day video proof, 80G receipts, dual-currency — that turns devotional commerce into something a global Hindu family can actually trust."

### Product differentiation

Unlike incumbents focused primarily on devotional content and basic puja booking, Bhaktisetu's core proposition is the verification + proof loop:

- **Aadhaar-verified pandit network** with 5+ years experience minimum
- **24-hour video proof** of every puja, sent to devotee via WhatsApp
- **Dual-currency pricing** (INR for India, USD/GBP/CAD/AUD for NRI)
- **80G tax-deductible receipts** auto-issued
- **AI Pandit** subscription (₹199/mo) for daily Vedic Q&A grounded in Sanskrit scripture
