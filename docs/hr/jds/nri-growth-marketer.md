# NRI Growth Marketer — Contractor (Part-Time)

## About Bhaktisetu

Bhaktisetu is India's devotional super-app (`darshya.online`) — live darshan, video-proof puja booking, gaushala seva, devotional commerce. Our NRI (Non-Resident Indian) tier prices in USD at a 9–10x premium over domestic. Annual plan margin >80%. Sri Mandir (the category leader) has zero USD billing — this is a real gap and your job is to widen it.

## Role Snapshot

- **Type**: Contractor, part-time (NOT full-time employment)
- **Location**: United States time zones (Pacific or Eastern; flexible within US daylight hours)
- **Hours**: ~15 hours/week
- **Compensation**: USD 500 / month  *(≈ ₹41,500 at 83 INR/USD as of 2026-06-11 — exchange rate footnote, not a contract guarantee; payment in USD via international transfer)*
- **Reports to**: Founder
- **Start**: Rolling, fired after CS lead + content ops are stable (sequencing rationale in `docs/hr/jds/README.md`)

## Responsibilities

- Run paid acquisition experiments across Meta (Facebook + Instagram) and Google (Search + YouTube).
- Target US Hindu diaspora — primarily aged 35–65, household income $100k+, second-generation NRIs supporting parents back in India, urban + suburban concentrations (Bay Area, NJ/NY, DFW, Atlanta, Chicago, Seattle).
- A/B test landing pages — variations of the existing `/nri` route and new geo-specific variants.
- Track CAC, payback period, LTV per channel weekly.
- Identify and test 1 new creative format per fortnight (UGC testimonial / animated explainer / pandit-to-camera / temple b-roll).
- Weekly experiment report — what ran, what worked, what was killed, what is next.
- Budget authority: up to USD 200/month ad spend without further approval; anything above goes to founder.
- Coordinate with content ops on temple-photo + festival-calendar assets you need for creative.
