# Customer Support Lead — Contractor (Part-Time)

## About Bhaktisetu

Bhaktisetu is India's devotional super-app (`darshya.online`) — live darshan, video-proof puja booking, gaushala seva, gurukul admissions, devotional commerce, astrology, wellness. Solo-founder, $0 budget, pre-revenue moving to first paying users.

## Role Snapshot

- **Type**: Contractor, part-time (NOT full-time employment)
- **Location**: India remote
- **Hours**: ~20 hours/week, weekday afternoons IST overlap with founder preferred
- **Compensation**: ₹25,000/month (anchored to `docs/company/06-talent.md` §5 line 58)
- **Reports to**: Founder
- **Start**: Rolling, fired by trigger doc `docs/hr/first-hire-trigger.md`

## Responsibilities

- Own the WhatsApp + email support inbox end-to-end. Devotee writes in Hindi or English; you reply in the language they wrote in.
- Hit first-response SLA <2 hours during 09:00–19:00 IST business hours; auto-acknowledge outside hours.
- Triage incoming tickets into 4 buckets within 5 minutes: refund, puja-not-performed, video-proof-quality, general.
- Apply existing refund policy (`apps/web/app/refunds/page.tsx` and incoming v2 at `docs/legal/refund-policy-v2-FINAL.md`) consistently; escalate anything outside the policy to founder, never invent terms.
- Run the daily Razorpay refund queue: process refunds within 24h of approval, update ticket status, send confirmation message.
- Document recurring complaint patterns weekly; propose canned-reply macros for top 3 each week.
- Coordinate with assigned pandits via WhatsApp when a puja issue surfaces; do NOT make payout decisions.
- Maintain the bereavement queue (high empathy required, escalates straight to founder review).

## Success Metrics

### 30 days

- 100% of incoming tickets responded to within 2-hour SLA during business hours
- Triage SOP doc shipped to `docs/cs/triage-sop.md` (you author, founder reviews)
- 5 canned-reply macros identified and shipped

### 60 days

- Customer satisfaction (CSAT) micro-survey live (single 1–5 question post-resolution), baseline ≥ 4.2/5
- Refund processing turnaround ≤ 24h end-to-end (request → Razorpay refund executed)
- Top-10 complaint categories documented with frequency

### 90 days

- <2h response SLA sustained across the full 90-day window
- Refund + cancellation SOP owned end-to-end (you write, founder approves)
- Zero policy-violation escalations to founder for tickets covered by published refund policy
- Identify 1 product-side fix per month that would eliminate ≥ 10 tickets/month (these become roadmap items)

## You Are

- Hindi + English fluent (read, write, speak)
- Comfortable holding empathy in difficult conversations (grief, refund disputes, devotee complaints about ritual quality)
- Patient with elderly devotees on the phone
- Comfortable with WhatsApp Business, Gmail, basic Google Sheets, Notion or Linear
- Time-zone aligned with India working hours
- Independent — you can run a 4-hour shift without supervision

- Prior BPO / customer-support experience at a small Indian startup
- Familiarity with Hindu ritual vocabulary (helps with puja-not-performed disputes)

## Sample devotee complaint (use for application)

> **Devotee (WhatsApp)**: "मैंने 14 दिन पहले काशी विश्वनाथ रुद्राभिषेक बुक किया था। वीडियो प्रूफ अभी तक नहीं मिला है। पैसा वापस चाहिए या पूजा फिर से करवाओ। मंदिर वालों ने कहा पूजा हो गयी थी लेकिन कोई वीडियो नहीं। मैंने 5100 रुपये दिए थे।"
> *(English: I booked a Rudrabhishek at Kashi Vishwanath 14 days ago. No video proof yet. Want refund or re-do. Temple staff say it was performed but no video. I paid ₹5,100.)*

Write the reply you would send. Maximum 200 words. Show: (a) empathy, (b) acknowledgement of the policy that applies, (c) what action you would take next, (d) a clear timeline. Do NOT promise a refund — that is the founder's call until you are trained.
