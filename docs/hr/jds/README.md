## Hiring sequence (do NOT post all three at once)

1. **CS Lead first.** Highest leverage on founder time. Trigger comes from `first-hire-trigger.md` (any of T1 / T2 / T3 fires).
2. **Content Ops second.** Post only after pending-temple queue exceeds 500 entries OR moderation eats >15h/week of founder time. Until then, founder + Claude-augmented review covers it (`docs/company/06-talent.md` §4 line 43).
3. **NRI Growth third.** Post only after India market shows sustained traction. NRI scaling without India PMF is premature spend.

Founder breakpoint analysis ( `docs/company/06-talent.md` §1 line 22): at 400–500 active users the marketplace eats the founder. CS Lead is the first lever to push that ceiling out.
