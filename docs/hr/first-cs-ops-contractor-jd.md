## JD

### Role

CS + Ops generalist contractor. 20 hr/wk. Remote India.

### Compensation

₹50K/mo flat (~₹625/hr). 3-month initial contract, renewable.

### Requirements

- 2+ years CS / ops exp in consumer-internet startup
- Hindi + English fluent (Tamil/Telugu/Marathi bonus)
- WhatsApp + Excel + Notion comfortable
- Devotional context awareness (respectful framing, sastra-sensitive)
- Available 9AM-6PM IST weekdays (founder backup weekends)

### Sourcing

- AngelList India + Wellfound
- Founder Twitter open hire (1-week amp)
- College alumni networks (Delhi University / IIM / etc.)
- 2-3 candidate referrals from existing advisors

### Interview process

1. 30-min intro call (culture + motivation)
2. 60-min hands-on test (mock CS triage + pandit onboarding intake)
3. 30-min reference call (1 previous employer)

### Onboarding (first 30 days)

- Week 1: founder pair-sessions on existing CS workflow
- Week 2: shadow CS tickets (founder reviews replies)
- Week 3: independent CS triage (founder reviews end-of-day)
- Week 4: takes 100% CS load, founder available for escalations only
