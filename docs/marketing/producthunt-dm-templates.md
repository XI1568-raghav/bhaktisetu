## Cadence rules

- First DM: T-7 days (warm batch of 8)
- Second DM: T-3 days (next 8 of warm + diaspora)
- Third DM: T-1 day (final 4 cold-aligned)
- Launch-day pings: every 90 min in IST window (2pm, 3:30pm, 5pm, 6:30pm, 8pm)
- Diaspora ping: 11:30pm IST (catches US east coast morning)
- One follow-up only per hunter who has not responded
- Zero follow-ups after launch closes (T+24h)

## Anti-spam rules

- Personalise the {their_topic} field for every cold-1 send
- Never send to a hunter you have no prior connection or shared interest with
- If a hunter says no, mark response in CSV and stop contact
- If a hunter does not respond within 48h, single follow-up only
- Do not mention the upvote ask in launch-day pings (just share the link)
