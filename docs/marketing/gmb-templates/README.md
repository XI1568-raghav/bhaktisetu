# GMB Post Templates — 25 Cities × 4 posts = 100 ready-to-schedule posts

Companion to `docs/marketing/local-seo-25-city-program.md` + R-Q4-M-005 handoff.

## Usage

1. Founder picks a city + post-type combo.
2. Copies the template, fills `{tokens}` (city name, deity, festival, date).
3. Pastes into GMB → Posts → New post → adds image from `docs/marketing/assets/` or city-specific stock.
4. Schedules via Buffer (₹400/mo) for the appropriate Tier-1/2/3 cadence.

## Post types (4 per city)

### Type 1: Live darshan offer
**Headline**: `Live Darshan from {famousTempleInCity} — Watch tonight 7pm IST`
**Body**:
> Devotees of {deityOfTemple}, join Bhaktisetu for tonight's live aarti from {famousTempleInCity}. HD video, no buffering, verified pandits performing the ritual on your behalf. NRI devotees — local timezone conversion built-in.
>
> Book your aarti participation slot or just watch live → bhaktisetu.com/darshan
**CTA**: Learn more → /darshan
**Image**: aarti/deity hero asset for that temple.

### Type 2: Puja booking offer
**Headline**: `{festival} {year} pujas now booking — {city}'s top pandits`
**Body**:
> {festival} falls on {date}. Bhaktisetu has 5 verified pandits in {city} ready to perform {top3PujasForFestival} on your behalf. Video proof of every ritual delivered within 24 hours. 80G receipt included.
>
> Reserve your slot → bhaktisetu.com/city/{slug}
**CTA**: Book now → /city/{slug}
**Image**: pandit-performing-puja photo + festival graphic.

### Type 3: Panchang of the day
**Headline**: `{city} Panchang — {date}`
**Body**:
> Today in {city}: Sunrise {time}, Sunset {time}. Tithi: {tithi}. Nakshatra: {nakshatra}. Rahu Kaal: {start}-{end}. Abhijit Muhurat: {start}-{end} (auspicious for new beginnings).
>
> Full local panchang → bhaktisetu.com/panchang/{slug}
**CTA**: View full panchang → /panchang/{slug}
**Image**: panchang-card auto-generated from /shloka-card style.

### Type 4: Pilgrimage tip
**Headline**: `Visiting {famousTempleInCity}? Know before you go`
**Body**:
> Planning a {famousTempleInCity} darshan? Best time to visit: {bestTime}. Avoid Rahu Kaal: {avoidWindow}. Nearby tirthas you can combine: {nearby1}, {nearby2}. Dress code: {dressCode}.
>
> Complete pilgrim guide → bhaktisetu.com/tirth/{templeSlug}
**CTA**: Read pilgrim guide → /tirth/{templeSlug}
**Image**: temple-exterior photo.

## Frequency per Tier

| Tier | Cities | Cadence | Posts/city/Q4 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tier-1 | 5 | Weekly | 13 (1 per week × 13 weeks) |
| Tier-2 | 10 | Fortnightly | 6-7 |
| Tier-3 | 10 | Monthly | 3 |

Total posts Q4: (5 × 13) + (10 × 6) + (10 × 3) = 65 + 60 + 30 = **155 posts** across 25 cities.

Founder schedules via Buffer batch-upload — 1 paste session = 1 month of all 25 cities (4-6 hours).
