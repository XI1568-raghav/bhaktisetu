## Thread 2 — "10 things I learned about pandit verification"

```
1/ Onboarded 100+ verified pandits across India in 90 days for @bhaktisetu.

Hardest part of a devotional super-app isn't tech. It's TRUST.

10 things I learned the hard way. 🧵

2/ Aadhaar verification is the floor, not the ceiling.

Anyone can pass KYC. Doesn't mean they're qualified to conduct a Rudrabhishek. We added: 5+ years experience minimum + 2 reference video proofs + Sanskrit certification.

3/ Pandits don't apply through forms.

They join through community recommendations. Bengaluru Brahmin Samaj. Kashi Punditgan Sabha. Mumbai Brahmin Mahasangh. Founder physical meetings beat any online funnel.

4/ Pricing is regional, not flat.

Rudrabhishek in Bengaluru: ₹2000. Same puja at Kashi: ₹3500. NRI pricing: $30. We learned this only after 50 disputes. Now: per-city pricing tables.

5/ Video proof was the moat.

90% pandits resist video at first ("I've never been recorded"). Once one pandit starts, the rest follow because devotee tips spike 40% for video-proof pandits.

6/ Payouts must be weekly.

Pandits live booking-to-booking. Monthly payout cycles kill the network. We do weekly Razorpay batch every Friday. ₹100 minimum threshold.

7/ Disputes happen. Have a runbook.

Pandit no-show (1% rate), devotee dispute (2% rate), video proof missing (0.5% rate). We auto-refund pandit no-show. Other cases: founder mediates within 48hr.

8/ The 80/20 of trust is video proof + verified Aadhaar + clear pricing.

Everything else is detail. Get these three right, and the network self-organises.

9/ NRI customers pay 9x for verified video proof of family puja in India. That's the LTV unlock.

Domestic India pricing is volume play. NRI pricing is margin play.

10/ Final lesson: trust scales with founder presence.

I personally vetted the first 50 pandits. The next 50 came through their recommendations. Year 2 challenge: scale trust as we hit 1000+ pandits.

bhaktisetu.com/pandits
```
