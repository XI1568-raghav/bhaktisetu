## Content strategy

Per group, 3-4 posts/week:
- Monday: festival reminder (next Hindu festival w/ India tradition)
- Wednesday: aarti / shloka of the week (Sanskrit + English)
- Friday: pandit Q&A (community asks, Bhaktisetu pandit answers)
- Sunday: Bhaktisetu update (new feature / temple / promo)

NEVER hard-sell. 70% community value, 30% Bhaktisetu mentions.

## Admin discipline

- 2 admins/group (founder + community lead)
- Community guidelines pinned
- No spam, no politics, no caste
- Removed-user policy public
