**Outline + draft:**

The Kashi Vishwanath Temple in Varanasi is one of India's most revered Hindu pilgrimage sites and the first of the 12 Jyotirlingas listed in the Dwadasha Jyotirlinga Stotram by Adi Shankaracharya. Located on the western bank of the Ganga, the temple draws over 30,000 daily visitors and significantly more during Maha Shivaratri and Sawan month.

For Hindu families across India and the diaspora, booking pujas at Kashi Vishwanath has historically been complicated — physical visits during peak season require advance planning, and remote booking through informal channels (WhatsApp middlemen, temple office calls) often lacks verification or proof.

### What pujas can be booked online?

The following pujas are available through verified online platforms including Bhaktisetu:

1. **Rudrabhishek** — the foremost Shiva ritual involving milk, honey, ganga jal, bilva leaves
2. **Maha Mrityunjaya Japa** — 125,000 repetitions for serious illness or longevity
3. **Mangal Shiva Puja** — Tuesday-specific Mars affliction remedy
4. **Sandhya Aarti** — evening worship sponsorship in your name
5. **Akhanda Path** — continuous Shiva Sahasranama recitation
6. **Sawan Sankalp Puja** — month-long Sawan vrat dedications

### Pricing tiers

Verified pandit platforms (including Bhaktisetu) typically charge ₹2,500-15,000 depending on tier. Lower tiers cover basic ritual with verified pandit + video proof. Premium tiers include extended duration, specific shloka recitation, and personalised sankalp.

NRI pricing in USD typically ranges $30-200 per puja with same-day video proof delivery.

### How to verify pandit credibility

Before booking, verify:
- Aadhaar verification confirmation
- Years of experience (minimum 5 for major pujas)
- Two reference video proofs from prior bookings
- Sanskrit/Hindi proficiency
- Direct contact ability for clarifications

### What to expect after booking

A verified booking should provide:
- Booking confirmation w/ pandit name and contact
- 24-72 hour pandit assignment + sankalp ritual
- Video proof within 24 hours of completion
- 80G tax-deductible receipt (Section 8 verified platforms only)
- WhatsApp updates throughout

### Cultural context

Lord Shiva at Kashi is worshipped as Vishwanath — Lord of the Universe. The Jyotirlinga form represents the cosmic pillar of light. Per Skanda Purana, dying at Kashi grants moksha — Bhairava himself whispers the tarak-mantra at the moment of death. The current temple structure was rebuilt in 1780 by Ahilyabai Holkar after centuries of destruction and rebuilding.

### NRI considerations

For Hindu families in US, UK, Canada, and Australia:
- USD/GBP/CAD/AUD pricing avoids exchange rate friction
- 24-hour video proof works across all timezones
- Tax-deductible status varies by country (US no; Canada partial; UK gift aid)
- Many platforms offer multi-temple bundling (Kashi + Varanasi temples combo)

### Booking platforms

- Bhaktisetu (bhaktisetu.com/temples/kashi-vishwanath) — verified pandit, 24hr video proof
- Sri Mandir Direct (limited Kashi inventory)
- Official KV Temple Trust (call only, no online booking yet)

### Conclusion

Online Kashi Vishwanath puja booking has matured significantly in 2025-26. With verified platforms offering Aadhaar-KYC pandits, 24-hour video proof, and dual-currency NRI pricing, the trust gap that historically limited remote bookings has largely closed.

For families planning specific pujas, advance booking during Sawan month (July-August) is strongly recommended given peak demand.
