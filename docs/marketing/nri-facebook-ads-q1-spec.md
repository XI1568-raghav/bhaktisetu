# NRI-Targeted Facebook Ads — $50 Budget, 3 Creatives × 2 Audiences

> **Roadmap item**: R-Q1-M-011 (P2) · **Authored**: 2026-06-13

## Goal

Q1 small-budget Facebook ads test on NRI segment. $50 spend + 3 creatives × 2 audiences = 6 ad combos.

## 3 creative concepts

| # | Concept | Format | Hook |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | "Live darshan from India" | Video 30s | NRI emotional, daughter calling parents in India |
| 2 | "Book your father's shraddha" | Static + carousel | Pind Daan / Gaya / shraddha specific |
| 3 | "₹0 to $30 puja booking" | Static image | Price transparency + 80G receipt visible |

## 2 audiences

- A: India-born in US 35-60 (Lookalike from existing 5K signups)
- B: Hindu surnames in US 25-45 (interest targeting)

## $50 budget split

- $25 spend Creative 1 × A + B
- $15 spend Creative 2 × A + B
- $10 spend Creative 3 × A + B

## Metrics tracked

- CTR + CPC per creative
- Conversion rate (signup)
- CAC per audience

## Q1 success

- 6 ads launched
- Top-performing creative identified
- CAC <$25
- Foundation for R-Q2-M-006 NRI Google Ads scale

## Closes

R-Q1-M-011 — spec shipped.
