> **Roadmap item**: R-Q1-M-013 (P0) · **Authored**: 2026-06-12 · **Cadence**: 1 post/day for 14 days · **Owner**: founder (compose + post; review before scheduling)
>
> **Rule**: each tweet ≤ 280 chars including handles + hashtags. Voice = honest founder, no marketing fluff, no emoji walls. Every tweet has: HOOK + CONCRETE NUMBER + INSIGHT.

## Strategy

- **Topic anchors** rotate: solo-founder life · devotional super-app niche · free-tier discipline · autonomous orchestrator · temple supply struggle · NRI economics · open metrics · lessons learned · the actual product
- **Lift bias**: build-in-public posts that share a NUMBER + a LESSON outperform abstract motivation 8:1 (anecdotally on Indian founder Twitter)
- **Hashtag discipline**: 1-2 max per tweet. Overusing hashtags reads as bot
- **Engagement bait avoided**: no "comment 'X' to get my..." gimmicks. Honesty > virality
- **Hindi-English mix**: occasional Hindi line for India-resonance, but English primary (broader reach)

Target benchmark after 14 days:
- ≥1,000 impressions per tweet (avg)
- ≥2% engagement rate
- ≥10 new followers
- ≥3 DMs from warm leads
- 1+ inbound that converts to onboarded pandit/temple/astrologer

Lower than 1K impressions/tweet → either following is too small (boost via reply-to-other-builders strategy) OR content not resonating (rework topic mix for week 3).
