# ProductHunt Launch Playbook — Bhaktisetu

> **Roadmap item**: R-Q2-M-001 (P0) · **Authored**: 2026-06-12 · **Target launch date**: TUESDAY (PH algorithm favors Tue + Wed) · **Founder action**: launch coordination ~6h prep + 24h live-day

## Why ProductHunt + why now

- ProductHunt = global devs/tech audience. Bhaktisetu = devotional. Mismatch? NO — PH audience includes huge India/NRI subset (~30% of PH traffic). Launch surfaces Bhaktisetu to NRI diaspora at peak intent.
- Q2 launch (post Q1 traction data) lets us pitch w/ real numbers, not aspirational.
- One-time channel — get top 10 daily → ~3000 visits in 48h → ~50-100 quality signups.

## Hunter mobilization (T-7 days)

Top-of-page on PH = needs first-hour upvote spike. Algorithm rewards velocity.

```markdown
- [ ] WhatsApp 20 personal contacts who are PH-verified ("hunters") — ask for honest upvote at launch
- [ ] Twitter DM 5-10 builder friends — ask for upvote + comment
- [ ] India PH community channel (Slack / Telegram if exists) — soft-mention launch date
- [ ] Avoid spam: NEVER mass-message strangers. PH detects fake upvote rings.
- [ ] Mock launch dry-run T-3 days: post test launch privately → verify upvote flow → unlist
```

## Risk + mitigations

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Launch day = Indian festival OR sensitive religious day → political backlash | Avoid known festival days (Diwali / Holi / Eid). Wednesday mid-month safest. |
| PH-removed for "religious content" violation | Review PH content policy in advance; frame as "platform for devotional services" not "religion app" |
| Negative comments / trolls on devotional pretext | Pre-prep founder-tone polite responses; do NOT engage trolls beyond 1 polite reply |
| Hunter upvote ring detected by PH | Only ask hunters who'd genuinely upvote organically anyway; no $$ for upvotes (illegal on PH) |
| Server overload from launch-day spike | Verify Render free-tier can handle 200 concurrent + sustained 50 RPS; load test before |
