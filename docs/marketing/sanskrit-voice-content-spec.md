# Sanskrit Voice Content — Daily 60-sec Aarti/Shloka for Instagram + YouTube

> **Roadmap item**: R-Q2-M-004 (P1) · **Authored**: 2026-06-13

## Goal

Daily 60-sec Sanskrit aarti/shloka voice content posted to Instagram Reels + YouTube Shorts. Aim 30K monthly views by Q2-end.

## Content format (per 60-sec piece)

```
0-5 sec: Brand intro card "Bhaktisetu — Sanskrit shloka of the day"
5-50 sec: Senior pandit recites shloka (Sanskrit) + on-screen text:
  - Devanagari Sanskrit
  - Roman transliteration  
  - English meaning (1-line)
50-60 sec: CTA — "Daily shlokas + verified pandit booking — bhaktisetu.com"
```

## Production pipeline

Pre-recorded library: 60 shlokas (20 Vedic + 20 Stotras + 20 Aartis).

- Recording: senior pandit + audio engineer, ₹500/shloka × 60 = ₹30K one-time
- Video: founder edits in CapCut / Premiere (or hires editor ₹500/video)
- Total Q2 production: ₹60K one-time + ₹1K/month ongoing edits

## Cadence

- Daily 1 post/week M-F (5/week)
- Weekend: long-form 5-min explainer video (separate workstream)
- Total: ~22 posts/month per platform = 44 posts/month total

## Distribution

- Instagram Reels (primary)
- YouTube Shorts (cross-post)
- Twitter (60-sec video on Twitter)
- Optional: WhatsApp Status as broadcast

## Engagement target

- Per-Reel: 5K views, 50 likes, 5 shares
- Cumulative monthly: 100K views
- Follower growth: +50 Instagram followers/month from content alone
