## Organic flywheel

1. SEO long-tail captures intent search ("rameshwaram darshan how to book online")
2. Visitors signup → book first puja
3. Post-puja, asked to share w/ family (referral program)
4. Referrals book → adopt-a-temple subscription → recurring user
5. Repeat customers post on social → drives more organic

Loop math: 100 paid customers → 30 organic (3:1 viral coefficient at year-1 maturity).
