## 5. First comment (Maker comment, target ~200 words, hard cap 2200 chars)

Hi Hunters,

I built Bhaktisetu because my grandmother in Bengaluru kept asking me to "send a puja" to our family deity in Varanasi every time something important came up in our family. The existing apps either took her money and sent a one-line confirmation, or charged enterprise-tier dollars for a thirty-second video.

So I built the thing she actually wanted. Every puja you book on Bhaktisetu comes with mandatory video proof from a verified pandit. Every donation auto-generates an 80G receipt for tax. Live aarti darshan streams from temples that have opted in. Vedic tools — muhurat, tithi, kundli — are free.

What's free, always:
- Live darshan from participating temples
- Daily panchang, tithi, muhurat calculators
- Kundli generation and basic horoscope

What's paid:
- Pujas booked through verified pandits (starts at rupees 500)
- Temple adoption subscriptions (rupees 500 a month)
- Premium consultations with astrologers
- Yatra packages

NRI lane: dual currency (INR + USD), 9-10x domestic pricing because the cost of fulfilment and the willingness to pay are both higher.

Roadmap: gaushala seva, gurukul scholarships, B2B temple white-label, and a sangha layer for community puja groups.

I'd love feedback on the adopt-a-temple flow specifically — does the value prop land for you, and what's missing? Reply here, I'll respond to every comment.

-- Founder, Bhaktisetu
