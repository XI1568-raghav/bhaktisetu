## Content production process

### Weekly cadence (per content contractor)

- Monday: 1 long-form blog post (1500-2500 words)
- Tuesday: 1 short-form blog post (800-1200 words)
- Wednesday: 1 sankalp/devotional explainer (1000-1500 words)
- Friday: 1 NRI-focused content piece (1200 words)
- Sunday: 1 founder editorial OR investor update

Total per week: ~5 new pages.

### Quarterly capacity

- 13 weeks per quarter × 5 pages/week = 65 pages
- Founder review: 1 hour per page
- Content contractor: 2 hours per page production
- Editor (founder OR senior advisor): 30 min per page review

### Total annual production capacity

- 240 pages annually
- Per page cost: ₹2,000 content + ₹500 review + ₹100 publication = ₹2,600
- Annual content investment: ~₹6.2L

## SEO + distribution

### Per-page checklist (per existing SEO spec)
- H1 contains target keyword
- Meta description 150-160 chars
- URL slug includes keyword
- Schema.org JSON-LD
- Internal links (5+)
- External links (1-3)
- Image optimization
- Mobile responsive

### Distribution channels per published page
- Sitemap auto-update
- WhatsApp opt-in broadcast (per content cluster)
- Twitter thread w/ excerpt
- LinkedIn long-form OR carousel
- Reddit r/india + r/IndianAmerican (selective)
- Email newsletter (monthly digest)

## Quality gates

- Founder review: every page before publish
- Cultural authenticity: pandit liaison consultation for ritual content
- SEO optimization: keyword research + schema validation
- A/B testing: page-level title + meta tests for top 50 pages
