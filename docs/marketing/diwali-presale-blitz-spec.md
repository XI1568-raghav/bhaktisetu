## Capacity planning

5K pujas × 30-min each = 2,500 pandit-hours needed Nov 8.
At 8 hr/pandit/day × Nov 8 = 312 pandit-day-slots needed.
Bhaktisetu pandit count at Nov 8: target 200 (per S-001-Q3 cumulative + extras).
312/200 = 1.56 → need 50 additional pandits sourced specifically for Diwali OR cap at 4K pre-bookings.

Recommendation: cap pre-bookings at 4K to avoid pandit overflow. Reserve 1K capacity for walk-in (day-of) demand at higher prices.
