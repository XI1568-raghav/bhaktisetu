## Format

Each video:
- Drone shot of temple (if accessible)
- Founder/trustee interview (3 min)
- Devotee testimonial (1 min)
- Bhaktisetu integration call-out (15 sec)
- Bhaktisetu URL + QR code (5 sec)

## Production approach

- Hire freelance videographer per city (₹50K-1L per video)
- Founder remote-direct via Zoom for interview questions

- Total per-video budget: ₹70K-1.2L
- Series total: ₹10-15L (Q3-Q4 spend, Series-A funded)
