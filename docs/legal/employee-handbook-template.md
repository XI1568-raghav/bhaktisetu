## Welcome

You're joining Bhaktisetu Foundation — a Section 8 non-profit building verified-pandit puja booking + AI Pandit + live darshan platform for 1B+ Hindu families globally.

## Mission + Values

### Mission

To build the global trust + verification + transactional layer for Hindu devotional commerce.

1. **Devotional respect** — every interaction with pandits + customers honored

3. **NRI bridge** — global Hindu diaspora deserves first-class infrastructure
4. **Mission alignment** — Section 8 = mission-first, not profit-extraction
5. **Founder personally accountable** — direct WhatsApp + email always

## Compensation

### Contractor model (current)
- Monthly retainer per role (₹40K-120K depending on role)
- Performance bonus up to 20% of retainer (quarterly)
- ESOP grant per role (0.1-0.5% vest 1-year cliff)
- Razorpay payment 1st of each month
- TDS 10% deducted per Section 194J

### Full-time employee (post-Series A)
- Competitive salary per role + market
- ESOP grant (TBD based on role + tenure)
- Health insurance + meal vouchers + WFH stipend
- Standard Indian employee benefits per labor law

### Equity philosophy
- Share equity broadly with team (not just founders)
- Long-term vesting (4-year)
- Acceleration on acquisition / IPO

## Work culture

### Hours + flexibility

- Standard 9 AM - 6 PM IST
- Async communication preferred (Slack + Notion)
- Flexibility for Indian festivals + family time
- 25 days PTO/year (post-FT hire)

### Remote-first

- Bhaktisetu operates remote (no physical office)
- Quarterly in-person founder + team gathering (Bengaluru)
- Travel reimbursed for in-person meetings

### Communication discipline

- Slack #ops channel for all real-time team communication
- Notion for all documents + processes
- Email for external partners + investors only
- WhatsApp for customer + founder direct only

## Conflict resolution

- Direct conversation first (don't gossip)
- Escalate to founder if unresolved
- Advisory board mediation for serious conflicts
- Founder + advisor decision is final

## Performance + termination

### Performance philosophy
- Founder personally reviews all hires monthly
- 30/60/90/180-day formal reviews documented
- High-performers rewarded with bigger ESOP + responsibility
- Underperformers given 30-day improvement plan

### Termination process
- Direct conversation by founder (not delegation)
- 30-day notice OR severance per Indian labor law
- ESOP vesting per agreement
- Reference letter provided for next role
- Knowledge transfer + clean handoff prioritized

## Equity + ownership

### Vesting schedule
- 1-year cliff (no vesting before)
- 4-year linear vest after cliff
- Acceleration on acquisition (50% acceleration default)
- Termination for cause = forfeit unvested

### Strike price (option)
- Set at fair market value at grant date
- Re-priced after Series A + Series B based on round valuation
- Repriced reasonable to retain talent during downturns
