# Board + Advisory Governance Spec

> Foundation for Section 8 board management + Series-A board structure. Pre-seed close trigger.

## Current state (pre-pre-seed)

- Founder: Sunil Kumar (CEO)
- No formal board (Section 8 minimum 2 directors required by MCA)
- Advisory board: 3-5 informal advisors

## Pre-seed close requirements

Section 8 + Companies Act compliance:
- Minimum 2 directors required (post-pre-seed expand to 3-5)
- Annual general meeting (AGM) required
- Quarterly board meetings minimum
- All resolutions documented + filed with MCA

## Advisory Board (no fiduciary duty)

Separate from formal Board. Provides advice + introductions.

### Existing advisors (target 3-5)
1. Strategic advisor: senior India consumer founder
2. Technical advisor: engineering leader (Y Combinator alum preferred)
3. Sales advisor: enterprise B2B specialist
4. NRI advisor: diaspora community leader
5. Legal advisor: tier-2 firm partner

### Advisor compensation
- ESOP grant 0.25% per advisor (1-year vest)
- No cash compensation
- Reasonable expense reimbursement
- 12-month renewable engagement

## Board meeting cadence

### Quarterly board meetings
- 90-minute Zoom (default)
- Founder presents quarterly review
- Board approves: budget, hiring plans, strategic initiatives
- Independent vote on conflicts of interest

### Annual general meeting (AGM)
- All shareholders/stakeholders
- Annual report presented
- Director re-elections
- Year-ahead plan ratified

## Decision-making framework

### Founder unilateral decisions
- Day-to-day operations
- Hiring < ₹50K/month role
- Marketing spend < ₹50K
- Vendor agreements < ₹50K

### Board ratification required
- New product launches (major)
- Pricing model changes
- Hiring > ₹50K/month role
- Marketing spend > ₹50K (single campaign)
- Vendor agreements > ₹50K annual
- Investor relations decisions
- M&A / strategic partnerships

### Unanimous Board required
- Equity dilution (any round)
- Acquisition decisions
- Founder compensation changes
- Section 8 → Section 8A or Pvt Ltd subsidiary
- Trademark + IP licensing
- Litigation initiation

## Conflict of interest policy

- All directors disclose related-party interests annually
- Conflicting director recuses from related decisions
- Founder must disclose any personal interest in vendor/partner
- Quarterly disclosure update

## Board materials

Pre-board distributed 7 days before meeting:
1. Quarterly metrics dashboard (per /admin/customer-success)
2. Financial review (P&L + Burn rate + Cash position)
3. Strategic initiatives status (per OKRs)
4. Risk register update
5. Topics for board approval (specific resolutions)

## Documentation

- Board meeting minutes signed within 7 days
- All resolutions filed with MCA per Companies Act
- Annual report compliance per Section 8 obligations
- 3-year retention minimum (often 7-year for audit)

## Counsel engagement

- Tier-2 firm engaged for board governance (₹50K Q3 + ₹2L annual)
- Annual board governance audit
- Regulatory filing support

## Founder accountability

- Founder + CEO is fiduciary for shareholders
- Annual self-evaluation submitted to board
- Investor + board feedback solicited annually
- Performance review by independent director annually

## Series A board changes

When Series A closes:
- Independent director seat expands to 2
- Lead VC may negotiate observer rights (vs full director)
- Board increases from 3 to 5 members
- Pre-seed lead may convert to observer

## Series B board (Year 3)

- Total 5-7 members
- Lead Series B VC
- Lead Series A VC (continues)
- 2-3 Independent Directors
- Founder + CEO

## Founder protection strategies

- Founder-friendly vesting (acceleration on acquisition)
- Pro-rata rights for founder in future rounds
- Anti-dilution protection (weighted average)
- Single class of common stock + preferred stock for investors
- Founder doesn't sign personal guarantees

## ESOP pool

- Pre-seed: 10% ESOP pool reserved
- Series A: expand to 15%
- Future rounds: maintain 15-20%
- Founder approves all ESOP grants

## Successor planning

- Document handover procedures for crisis scenarios
- 1 designated successor in advisory board (CEO-elect)
- Annual leadership transition planning
- Insurance: Director + Officer (D&O) coverage

## Series A pitch + DD requirement

For Series A close:
- Board governance documents shared with VCs
- Independent director profile established
- Clean cap table + ESOP policy
- Quarterly board meeting history (post-pre-seed close)
- Annual report previous fiscal year
