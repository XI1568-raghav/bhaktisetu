# ASCI India Influencer Compliance — 3 Required Docs

> Mandatory for Bhaktisetu influencer partnerships per R-Q2-M-005 + R-Q4-M-009.

## Document 1 — Influencer Partnership Agreement Template

```
INFLUENCER PARTNERSHIP AGREEMENT — BHAKTISETU FOUNDATION

This Agreement dated [DATE] is between:

PARTY A: Bhaktisetu Foundation, Section 8 non-profit registered in India (CIN: [CIN])
PARTY B: [Influencer name], a content creator with handles [Instagram/YouTube/Twitter @handle]

1. SCOPE OF ENGAGEMENT
   1.1 Party B will create [N] pieces of sponsored content per month for [duration months]
   1.2 Content formats: [Instagram Reels / YouTube videos / Twitter threads / LinkedIn posts]
   1.3 Each content piece will mention or feature Bhaktisetu Foundation services

2. CONTENT GUIDELINES
   2.1 Party B retains full editorial control
   2.2 Bhaktisetu cannot dictate creative direction beyond brand mention
   2.3 Party B will not make medical, financial, or legal claims on Bhaktisetu's behalf
   2.4 Party B will not denigrate other devotional platforms or competitors

3. ASCI INDIA COMPLIANCE (MANDATORY)
   3.1 Every sponsored post MUST carry #ad OR #paidpartnership hashtag (Instagram + Twitter)
   3.2 YouTube content MUST display "Sponsored by Bhaktisetu" overlay or verbal mention
   3.3 Audio podcasts MUST include "In partnership with Bhaktisetu" verbal disclosure
   3.4 Failure to disclose results in immediate termination + no payment for that piece

4. COMPENSATION
   4.1 Cash compensation: ₹[X] per content piece via Razorpay/bank transfer
   4.2 In-kind compensation: [free puja / free yatra package / Bhaktisetu Pro subscription]
   4.3 Affiliate commission: 15% on every booking from Party B's unique discount code
   4.4 Payment within 30 days of content publication

5. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY
   5.1 Party B retains all rights to content created
   5.2 Bhaktisetu may re-post Party B's content with credit + tag
   5.3 Bhaktisetu cannot use Party B's image for unrelated marketing

6. TERM AND TERMINATION
   6.1 Initial term: [3/6 months]
   6.2 Either party may terminate with 30-day written notice
   6.3 Outstanding compensation for completed content paid within 30 days of termination
   6.4 Either party may terminate immediately for ASCI violation

7. CONFIDENTIALITY
   7.1 Party B will not disclose Bhaktisetu internal data, customer information, or unreleased products
   7.2 Bhaktisetu will not disclose Party B's audience metrics or commercial terms

8. INDEMNIFICATION
   8.1 Party B indemnifies Bhaktisetu for any claims arising from Party B's content
   8.2 Bhaktisetu indemnifies Party B for any claims arising from Bhaktisetu's services or products

9. GOVERNING LAW + JURISDICTION
   Bengaluru, Karnataka, India.

SIGNED:
Party A (Bhaktisetu Foundation): __________
Party B (Influencer): __________
Date: __________
```

## Document 2 — ASCI Disclosure Quick-Reference

Per ASCI India Code (Chapter VI):

| Platform | Required disclosure | Format |
|---|---|---|
| Instagram Reels | #ad or #paidpartnership | Must be in first 3 hashtags of caption |
| Instagram Stories | Sticker "Paid Partnership with Bhaktisetu" | Use Instagram native sticker |
| YouTube videos | "Sponsored" overlay first 30 seconds OR verbal mention in first minute | Both preferred |
| YouTube Shorts | "Paid promotion" label via YouTube native tools | Mandatory enabled |
| Twitter/X posts | #ad or #paidpartnership | First hashtag, before campaign tags |
| LinkedIn posts | "Sponsored content" prefix in first line | Visible before any content |
| Podcast audio | "In partnership with Bhaktisetu" verbal disclosure | Within first 60 seconds |
| WhatsApp broadcast | "Sponsored by Bhaktisetu" prefix | In every broadcast text |
