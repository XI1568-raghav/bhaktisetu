## 1. Scope and applicability

1.1 This policy governs every paid transaction effected through Bhaktisetu's website, mobile application, and partner channels, including (a) puja bookings, (b) adopt-a-temple subscriptions, (c) merchandise sold through the Bhaktisetu store, (d) astrology and wellness consultations, (e) gaushala seva contributions, (f) chadhava (virtual offerings), and (g) corporate CSR donation-match commitments. Live darshan is provided gratuitously and is outside the scope of refunds save where this Policy expressly says otherwise.

1.2 By tendering payment or executing a Memorandum of Understanding ("**MoU**") with Bhaktisetu the customer (each, a "**User**") agrees to be bound by this Policy. This Policy together with the Terms of Use and the Privacy Policy constitutes the entire agreement between Bhaktisetu and the User in respect of refunds, cancellations, and dispute resolution arising from a Bhaktisetu transaction. To the extent of any conflict, this Policy prevails on the subjects expressly addressed herein.

1.3 Nothing in this Policy operates to waive or limit a User's non-waivable statutory rights under the CPA, the DPDPA, or any other Indian statute. Where this Policy is silent or appears more restrictive than statute, the statute prevails.

## 2. Force Majeure

2.1 "**Force Majeure Event**" means any event beyond the reasonable control of Bhaktisetu, including without limitation: act of God, fire, flood, earthquake, cyclone, epidemic, pandemic, civil unrest, war, terrorism, riot, government order or notification (including any temple-closure order issued by a Devaswom Board, religious endowments commissioner, or competent authority), strike, lockout, failure of telecommunication or internet infrastructure not attributable to Bhaktisetu, or refusal of a temple authority or priest to perform the booked service for reasons of ritual purity, scriptural observance, or temple custom.

2.2 On the occurrence of a Force Majeure Event, Bhaktisetu shall (a) notify the User in-app and by registered email within forty-eight (48) hours of becoming aware, (b) suspend or cancel the affected service, and (c) credit a full refund of the principal transaction amount to the User's original mode of payment within fourteen (14) Business Days of cancellation, save for irrecoverable taxes and gateway fees handled per Clause 10.

2.3 Bhaktisetu owes no service credit, platform credit, compensation, or consequential damages in respect of a Force Majeure Event beyond the refund stipulated in Clause 2.2.

## 3. Puja bookings

3.1 The following grid applies to puja bookings made through the Bhaktisetu platform. "**Scheduled Time**" means the start time of the puja as confirmed at the time of booking, in Indian Standard Time.

| Cancellation window before Scheduled Time | Refund payable |
|---|---|
| > 24 hours | 100% |
| 6 to 24 hours | 50% |
| < 6 hours | Nil (samagri procured, priest mobilised) |
| Temple cancels (any cause) | 100% **plus** 10% Platform Credit (terms in Clause 3.5) |
| Video proof not delivered within 48 hours of Scheduled Time | At the User's election: 100% refund **or** free reschedule within 90 days |
| Video proof delivered but materially deficient (incorrect temple, incorrect puja, unidentifiable visual) | 100% refund **plus** 10% Platform Credit, on outcome of investigation per Clause 3.3 |
| Cancellation initiated by Bhaktisetu (suspected fraud, KYC failure, breach of Terms) | 100% refund of principal; investigation outcome shared in writing within 7 Business Days; gateway fees absorbed by Bhaktisetu |

3.2 Cancellations may be initiated via account dashboard (Booking History → Cancel) or by emailing support@darshya.online citing the booking reference (`BS-YYYYMMDD-XXXXX`). Disputes within the 6–24 hour or sub-6-hour windows may be granted full or partial refund at Bhaktisetu's discretion where the temple confirms in writing that samagri had not been procured and the priest had not been mobilised.

3.3 **Video-proof quality investigation**: User may raise a quality dispute within seven (7) days of receipt of video proof. Bhaktisetu shall complete investigation within five (5) Business Days from acknowledgement, inclusive of communication with the relevant temple and priest. Refund (where due) is credited within seven (7) Business Days of investigation conclusion.

3.4 **Platform-initiated cancellation**: where Bhaktisetu cancels a booking on suspicion of fraud, breach of these Terms, or sanctions-list match, the refund of principal is unconditional. Bhaktisetu may withhold gateway transaction fees only where the User is ultimately found by Bhaktisetu to have made a knowingly false representation; the User shall be informed of such finding in writing and may dispute it via Clauses 13 and 14.

3.5 **Platform Credit terms**: Platform Credit is non-transferable, non-encashable for cash, valid for twelve (12) months from issue, redeemable only against future paid services on the Bhaktisetu platform, and forfeitable on expiry. Platform Credit is a goodwill instrument and does not constitute a debt owed by Bhaktisetu to the User.

## 4. Subscriptions (adopt-a-temple, recurring devotional plans, CSR match)

4.1 Monthly subscriptions are cancellable by the User at any time via the account dashboard. Cancellation takes effect at the conclusion of the then-current billing cycle. No pro-rata refund is payable for the partial month already in progress, save in Force Majeure or where the temple ceases operations.

4.2 Annual subscriptions are cancellable within thirty (30) days of activation for a pro-rata refund of the unused months, less an administrative deduction of fifteen percent (15%) of the principal. After thirty (30) days the annual subscription is non-refundable save in Force Majeure.

4.3 Corporate CSR match commitments are governed by the executed MoU between Bhaktisetu and the corporate sponsor. This consumer-facing Policy does not modify any such MoU, and any conflict between this Policy and a duly executed MoU shall be resolved in favour of the MoU as between the corporate sponsor and Bhaktisetu.

## 5. Bhaktisetu Store (goods)

5.1 Returnable categories: puja items (murtis, diyas, thalis, rudraksha), books, and other non-consumable goods may be returned within seven (7) days of delivery provided the goods are unused, in original packaging, and accompanied by the order reference.

5.2 Non-returnable categories: consumables (incense, camphor, kumkum, haldi, prasad), perishables, custom-engraved goods, and opened books. This restriction is grounded in hygiene, sanctity, and customisation and does not derogate from the User's rights under Section 86 of the CPA in respect of defective goods.

5.3 Damaged or wrong goods: User must report within forty-eight (48) hours of delivery via support@darshya.online with photographic evidence. Bhaktisetu shall, at its election, replace the goods or refund in full including return shipping.

5.4 Address typographical error attributable to the User: re-shipment is at the User's cost, or the User may elect partial refund equal to (principal less actual courier charges incurred less ten percent (10%) restocking fee). Where the courier marks the consignment as delivered to the address printed on the consignment note, Bhaktisetu's liability is discharged on production of the courier proof-of-delivery.

5.5 **Product-liability claim under CPA 2019**: claims grounded in defect, deficiency, or unfair trade practice shall be dealt with on the merits in accordance with Sections 82 to 87 of the CPA. Without prejudice to the User's statutory rights, Bhaktisetu's aggregate contractual liability per single claim is capped at the higher of (a) the principal amount paid for the affected goods or (b) ₹1,00,000 (Rupees One Lakh). This cap does not apply to (i) death or personal injury caused by Bhaktisetu's wilful default or gross negligence, or (ii) fraud, in respect of which liability is unlimited as a matter of law.

## 6. Astrology and wellness consultations

6.1 Cancellable up to six (6) hours before the scheduled session for a full refund. Once the consultant has joined the video room or telephonic bridge, the session is non-refundable.

6.2 If the consultant fails to join within fifteen (15) minutes of the scheduled start, the User is entitled to a full refund or a free reschedule.

6.3 **Partial-delay window (five to fifteen minutes late)**: at Bhaktisetu's discretion, the User may be offered a fifty percent (50%) refund or a free reschedule, having regard to (a) session length, (b) consultant explanation, and (c) any prior pattern.

## 7. Gaushala seva and other charitable donations

7.1 Donations under this clause are made to Bhaktisetu Foundation (an 80G-certified Section 8 company) and are disbursed within twenty-four (24) hours of receipt to the designated gaushala or beneficiary. Upon disbursement an 80G receipt is generated.

7.2 **Reversal request — UI error or wrong beneficiary**: User may request reversal within twenty-four (24) hours of donation. Where Bhaktisetu confirms a platform-attributable error, the principal shall be refunded within seven (7) Business Days. Where the beneficiary has not yet been credited, reversal is unconditional; where the beneficiary has already been credited, Bhaktisetu shall use reasonable endeavours to recover and refund but may be unable to do so, in which case the User shall be offered an equivalent re-donation to the intended beneficiary funded by Bhaktisetu.

7.3 **80G receipt already issued and tax benefit claimed**: reversal is not possible. Where the User has claimed the 80G deduction in an income-tax return that has been processed, any subsequent reversal would constitute tax fraud. In such cases the User may instead request a donor-name correction or a transfer of the equivalent value to a different beneficiary, subject to Bhaktisetu's verification.

7.4 **Appeal mechanism**: a User aggrieved by a decision under this Clause 7 may, within seven (7) Business Days, escalate to the Grievance Officer named in Clause 13. The Grievance Officer shall dispose of the escalation within five (5) Business Days.

## 8. Chadhava (virtual offerings)

8.1 Virtual offerings (flowers, bhog, diya, ghee, tilak) are deemed performed at the time the offering is communicated to the temple system, typically in real time. They are non-refundable thereafter.

8.2 Where the offering is not performed because of a Bhaktisetu platform error, 100% refund is payable within seven (7) Business Days of confirmation. Where the temple priest performs an offering different from that booked because of a communication or coordination error, 50% refund is payable on outcome of investigation within five (5) Business Days.

## 9. Live darshan

Live darshan is offered without charge and no refund mechanism is applicable. Premium darshan features, if introduced in future, will carry their own refund terms communicated at the point of sale.

## 10. Refund mechanics, timelines, GST, and FX

10.1 Refunds are processed to the original instrument of payment. Bhaktisetu does not issue cash refunds and does not redirect refunds to alternate instruments save where the original instrument has been irrevocably closed and the User submits documentary proof.

10.2 Indicative timelines (subject to payment-aggregator and issuing-bank processing):

| Instrument | Timeline |
|---|---|
| UPI, mobile wallets (Razorpay) | 1 to 3 Business Days |
| Debit and credit cards (Razorpay) | 5 to 7 Business Days |
| Net banking | 5 to 10 Business Days |
| International cards (Stripe), USD or other foreign currency | 7 to 14 Business Days |
| Platform Credit | Instant credit; valid 12 months per Clause 3.5 |

10.3 **GST treatment**: where Bhaktisetu has charged GST on the original invoice and the supply is cancelled before consumption, Bhaktisetu shall issue a credit note under Section 34 of the CGST Act and the refund shall include the GST component, recovered by Bhaktisetu through its monthly GST reconciliation. Where the supply has already been consumed in part (for example a partially completed puja, or a consultation that commenced), the GST attributable to the consumed portion is non-refundable as a matter of law. Bhaktisetu does not pass through gateway fees levied by Razorpay or Stripe on the original transaction; such fees are absorbed by Bhaktisetu save in respect of platform-initiated cancellations under Clause 3.4 where Bhaktisetu may withhold proven gateway costs.

10.4 **Foreign-exchange risk on USD or other foreign-currency refunds**: refunds are processed at the exchange rate published by the payment aggregator (Stripe) on the date of refund execution. Bhaktisetu does not hedge intra-day exchange-rate movement and is not liable for any difference between the rate at original capture and the rate at refund execution. The User expressly assumes this exchange-rate risk as consideration for the convenience of dual-currency billing.

## 11. Chargebacks

11.1 Users are requested in good faith to contact support@darshya.online with the booking or order reference before initiating a chargeback through their card-issuing bank.

11.2 Where a User initiates a chargeback without prior contact and Bhaktisetu reasonably believes the underlying transaction was genuine and properly performed, Bhaktisetu may (a) suspend the User's account for the duration of the chargeback investigation up to thirty (30) days, and (b) seek recovery of the chargeback-handling fee levied by the payment aggregator (presently ₹500 or USD 10, subject to aggregator schedules) from any future credit balance accruing to the User. Such set-off is consensual under Section 60 of the ICA and is part of the consideration for these Terms.

11.3 Where the chargeback is ruled in favour of the User by the card-issuing bank, the chargeback-handling fee is waived and the User's account is reinstated without prejudice.

11.4 Three or more chargebacks initiated by the same User in any rolling twelve-month period may result in permanent account suspension. The User shall be afforded a written hearing through the Grievance Officer prior to permanent suspension.

## 12. Bereavement

12.1 Where a User is deceased prior to the rendering of a paid service, an immediate family member (spouse, parent, child, or sibling) may request a full refund or a reschedule by writing to grievance@darshya.online with (a) the booking reference if known, or the registered email of the deceased, (b) the death certificate, and (c) a government-issued identification document of the requester evidencing relationship to the deceased.

12.2 A full refund will be processed within fourteen (14) Business Days regardless of the standard cancellation grid. The Grievance Officer handles bereavement requests personally and treats them as priority cases.

## 14. Dispute resolution

14.1 The Parties shall first attempt in good faith to resolve any dispute, claim, or controversy arising out of or in connection with this Policy through the Grievance Officer process set out in Clause 13.

14.2 If a dispute remains unresolved thirty (30) days after the Grievance Officer issues a disposition, either Party may refer the dispute to arbitration administered by SAMA (Society for Alternative Mediation and Arbitration), or in the absence of SAMA acceptance, by a sole arbitrator appointed by the Bangalore International Mediation, Arbitration & Conciliation Centre ("**BIMACC**") in accordance with the Arbitration and Conciliation Act 1996. Seat and venue: Bengaluru. Language: English. The arbitral award is final and binding.

14.3 **Consumer carve-out**: notwithstanding Clause 14.2, where the User is a "consumer" within the meaning of Section 2(7) of the CPA, the User retains the absolute right to approach the appropriate District, State, or National Consumer Disputes Redressal Commission, and Clause 14.2 shall not be enforced against such User by Bhaktisetu. This carve-out is included in compliance with the Supreme Court of India's view that pre-dispute arbitration clauses are not enforceable against consumers as a class.
