# Priest Contracts Signed — 100 Pandits w/ Revenue-Share Template

> **Roadmap item**: R-Q2-L-002 (P1) · **Authored**: 2026-06-13

## Goal

Standardized priest/pandit contract template signed by 100 pandits by Q2-end. Covers revenue-share, KYC, video proof, no-show penalties, termination.

## Contract template structure

```
BHAKTISETU FOUNDATION × PANDIT AGREEMENT (Standard v1)

PARTIES:
- Bhaktisetu Foundation (Section 8, CIN: {CIN})
- {Pandit name}, son/daughter of {parent}, PAN: {PAN}, address: {addr}

EFFECTIVE DATE: ____________

1. ENGAGEMENT
   1.1 Pandit listed on Bhaktisetu platform as verified pandit
   1.2 Platform schedules bookings; pandit performs pujas
   1.3 No exclusivity — pandit free to serve outside Bhaktisetu

2. REVENUE SHARE
   2.1 Devotee pays Bhaktisetu; Bhaktisetu pays pandit
   2.2 Pandit retains 70-80% of devotee payment per puja
   2.3 Bhaktisetu retains 20-30% as platform + payment-processor + Foundation overhead
   2.4 Exact % per puja type listed in Schedule A
   2.5 Daily Razorpay batch payout (≥₹100 minimum)

3. VIDEO PROOF
   3.1 Pandit MUST upload video within 72h of puja completion
   3.2 Missing video → no payout for that puja + warning logged
   3.3 3+ missing videos in 30 days → suspension review

4. NO-SHOW POLICY
   4.1 Pandit must perform puja within scheduled date/time
   4.2 No-show w/o 24h notice → 100% refund to devotee + pandit liability
   4.3 Repeat no-show (2+ in 60 days) → suspension

5. KYC + DISPUTE
   5.1 Pandit submits Aadhaar (masked) + PAN + bank-account + 2 reference video proofs
   5.2 Disputes handled per docs/specs/marketplace-v2-dispute-resolution-spec.md

6. DATA HANDLING
   6.1 Devotee PII not shared w/ pandit except booking-relevant fields
   6.2 Pandit MUST maintain confidentiality

7. TERMINATION
   7.1 Either party w/ 30-day written notice
   7.2 Bhaktisetu can terminate immediately for severe breach (multiple no-show / fabricated video / fraud)
   7.3 Outstanding pandit payouts settled within 30 days

8. JURISDICTION
   Bengaluru, Karnataka, India.

SIGNED:
Pandit: __________
Bhaktisetu: __________
Date: __________
```
