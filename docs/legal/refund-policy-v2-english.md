## 1. Scope

This policy governs refunds, cancellations, and disputes for all paid services on darshya.online and the Bhaktisetu mobile app, including: puja bookings, adopt-a-temple subscriptions, store orders, astrology + wellness consultations, gaushala seva, chadhava offerings, and CSR donation matches. Services are provided by Bhaktisetu Foundation, an 80G-certified Section 8 not-for-profit registered in [STATE].

By making a payment on Bhaktisetu, you agree to this policy.

## 2. Force Majeure

We may suspend or cancel any service for events beyond reasonable control, including but not limited to: natural disasters, government-mandated temple closures, civil unrest, pandemic restrictions, internet outages, and acts of priestly authority. In Force Majeure cases:
- 100% refund processed within 14 business days
- No service credit obligation
- Notification via in-app + registered email within 48 hours of suspension declaration

## 3. Puja Bookings

| Cancellation window | Refund |
|---|---|
| More than 24 hours before scheduled puja | 100% refund |
| 6–24 hours before | 50% refund |
| Less than 6 hours before | No refund (priest preparations underway) |
| Puja not performed by temple | 100% refund + 10% platform credit (credit valid 12 months, non-transferable) |
| Video proof not delivered within 48h of scheduled time | Devotee may elect: full refund OR free reschedule within 90 days |
| **NEW** Video proof delivered but quality dispute (wrong temple, blurry, wrong puja) | Investigation within 5 business days; if upheld, 100% refund + 10% credit |
| **NEW** Platform-initiated cancellation (fraud, KYC failure) | 100% refund; investigation outcome shared with devotee within 7 business days; payment gateway fees waived |

Cancellations via account dashboard → Booking History → Cancel. Or email support@darshya.online with booking ID (`BS-YYYYMMDD-XXXXX`).

**Discretion clause**: For edge cases where temple confirms preparations had not commenced (e.g., samagri unprocured) despite cancellation falling in the 6–24h or <6h windows, full or partial refund may be granted at Bhaktisetu's discretion. Devotee must request via dashboard with reason.

## 4. Subscriptions (Adopt-a-Temple, CSR Match) — NEW SECTION

- Monthly subscriptions may be cancelled any time via account dashboard
- Cancellation takes effect at end of current billing cycle
- No partial-month refund except in Force Majeure
- Annual subscriptions: pro-rata refund for unused months minus 15% admin fee, only if cancellation requested within 30 days of activation
- CSR match commitments (B2B) governed by signed MoU separate from this consumer policy

## 6. Astrology & Wellness Sessions

- Cancellable up to 6 hours before scheduled session for 100% refund
- Once consultant joins video room: non-refundable
- Consultant fails to join within 15 minutes of start: 100% refund OR free reschedule
- **NEW** Partial-delay grey zone: consultant joins 5–15 min late → devotee may request 50% refund OR free reschedule, granted at Bhaktisetu's discretion based on session length and consultant explanation

## 7. Gaushala Seva & Donations

- Donations are non-refundable once funds disbursed to gaushala or beneficiary (typically T+24h after donation)
- 80G receipts auto-issued on disbursement; once tax benefit claimed, reversal impossible
- **NEW** Wrong-recipient claim window: devotee may request reversal within 24 hours of donation if wrong gaushala selected due to UI error; investigation outcome within 3 business days
- **NEW** Appeal mechanism: if initial reversal request denied, devotee may escalate to Grievance Officer (see clause 13) within 7 business days; escalation reviewed within 5 business days

## 8. Chadhava (Virtual Offerings)

- Non-refundable once offering processed at temple (typically real-time)
- 100% refund if offering not performed due to platform error
- 50% refund if temple priest performed wrong offering due to communication error; investigation in 5 business days

## 9. Live Darshan

Free service; no refund mechanism applicable. Premium darshan features (if introduced) will carry their own refund terms communicated at purchase.

## 11. Chargebacks (NEW SECTION)

Devotees are requested to contact `support@darshya.online` with booking/order ID BEFORE initiating chargeback with their bank. Direct chargebacks without prior contact may result in:
- 30-day account suspension during investigation
- Chargeback gateway fee (currently ₹500 / $10) recovered from any future credit balance
- If chargeback ruled in devotee favour: chargeback fee waived; if ruled against: fee retained and account reinstatement requires written undertaking

Repeat chargeback initiation (3+ in 12 months) may result in permanent account suspension.

## 12. Bereavement (NEW SECTION)

If a devotee is deceased before a scheduled service, immediate family members (spouse, parent, child, sibling) may request refund or reschedule by emailing `grievance@darshya.online` with:
- Original booking ID (if available; if not, registered email of devotee)
- Death certificate (PDF/image)
- Government ID of requesting family member proving relationship

Full refund issued within 14 business days regardless of standard cancellation window. Grievance Officer handles personally.
