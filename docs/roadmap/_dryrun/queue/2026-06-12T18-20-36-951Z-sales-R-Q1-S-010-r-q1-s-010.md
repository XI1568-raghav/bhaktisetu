## Vetting rubric (6 gates in vetting-rubric.md)

1. Verifiable credential: yoga (RYT-200+ or recognized Indian school cert) / Ayurveda (BAMS or recognized institute diploma)
2. Public teaching history ≥2 years (IG/YouTube/studio website evidence)
3. Sample class video review (15-min) — instructor sends or links existing
4. Background check declaration signed (self-declaration acceptable Q1)
5. English OR Hindi instruction fluency (audio check)
6. Liability MOU signed (waiver template for wellness — flag legal review needed but ship draft)
