## 12 questions (exact, include these in script)

**Why-now (Q1-Q3):**
1. What was happening in your life when you decided to book a puja online instead of in-person?
2. Walk me through the last 24h before you completed the booking — what triggered the decision?
3. Was there a specific date/event (shraddha, housewarming, festival) driving urgency?

**Alternatives (Q4-Q6):**
4. Before Bhaktisetu, how were you (or your family) arranging pujas?
5. Did you compare us to anyone else — local priest, ePuja, Utsav, family network? What made you pick us?
6. If Bhaktisetu disappeared tomorrow, what would you do for your next puja?

**NRI tier WTP (Q7-Q9):**
7. [If NRI] How much extra would you pay for guaranteed English-speaking pandit + Zoom join link + sankalp in your timezone? ₹0 / ₹500 / ₹1500 / ₹3000?
8. What's the single most painful thing about arranging puja from abroad today?
9. Would you pay a yearly membership (₹X) for priority booking + dedicated pandit relationship? What's "X"?

**Booking flow friction (Q10-Q12):**
10. From landing on the site to payment — where did you hesitate or get confused?
11. After payment, between confirmation and the puja day — what felt unclear or made you anxious?
12. The video proof — did you watch it? Did it match your expectation? What would make it better?
