## TDD — research-first

before writing copy, verify each candidate:
- follower count in 5K-50K range (screenshot URL in candidates.md)
- ≥3 devotional posts in last 30 days
- engagement rate ≥3% (likes/followers on last 5 posts, manual calc)
- audience indicator: bio mentions NRI/diaspora/USA/UK/Canada/Australia OR comments in English+devanagari mix
- reachable: public DM OR business email in bio

reject candidate if any fail. document rejection reason.

## Candidate sourcing (free methods)

- Instagram search: `#bhakti`, `#sanskritmantra`, `#templevisit`, `#hindudiaspora`, `#nritemple`, `#vedicwisdom`
- YouTube: search "puja vidhi NRI", "bhakti diaspora", "sanskrit lessons", filter <50K subs
- Twitter/X advanced: from:devotional accounts, min_faves:50, lang:en, has:images
- Reddit cross-ref: r/hinduism, r/sanskrit — note any creators mentioned
- Substack: bhakti/dharma newsletters with paid tier (proxy for serious creators)

## Outreach copy requirements

3 variants, each ≤120 words:

**V1 warm** — for creators who already mention puja booking pain
**V2 cold value-first** — lead with free service offer (1 free puja booking for them)
**V3 collab pitch** — propose joint content (creator script, we produce, both promote)

each variant: subject line, body, CTA, fallback follow-up after 5 days.
