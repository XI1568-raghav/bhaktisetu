## Deliverable structure (sections required)

1. **Mission** — one paragraph. Why this product exists. Death is non-negotiable; we serve, not sell.
2. **Non-goals** — explicit list: no upsells, no cross-sell to puja, no reviews prompt within 30 days, no photos in booking flow, no urgency banners, no discount codes.
3. **Personas** — (a) bereaved son/daughter coordinating, (b) extended family member in another city, (c) elderly spouse needing phone support.
4. **Booking flow — 4 steps** (ASCII wireframe each):
   - Step 1: Deceased details (name, age, gender, place of death, time of death) — single screen, large fonts, no progress gamification
   - Step 2: Service location + scheduled time (with "as soon as possible" default)
   - Step 3: Family contact + one secondary contact for coordination
   - Step 4: Confirm + assign — no payment screen. Show "We will be there" message with pandit ETA placeholder.
5. **Pandit dispatch SLA** — 4 hour from booking to pandit at location. Define:
   - Dispatch queue prioritization (FIFO, no surge pricing ever)
   - Pandit notification flow (SMS + call + WhatsApp; ack required within 10min or re-dispatch)
   - Escalation: if no ack in 10min → ops human picks up phone
   - Geo-radius: 25km primary, 50km fallback with 6h SLA disclosure to family
6. **Family coordination chat** — group chat auto-created with: primary contact, secondary contact, assigned pandit, Bhaktisetu ops. WhatsApp-based (use existing wa-bot infra — verify path `apps/wa-bot/` or similar before citing). Functions: share pandit ETA, samagri checklist, location pin, ops broadcasts.
7. **Payment model — deferred** — no card capture upfront. Service rendered → 24h grace → invoice via WhatsApp + email with UPI link. Define:
   - Pricing tiers (research existing puja pricing in DB; if unknown mark TBD-founder)
   - Refund/dispute path
   - Bad-debt acceptance: target <5% non-payment, treat as cost of dignity
8. **Grief-sensitive copy review checklist** — 12+ items minimum. Examples:
   - No exclamation marks anywhere in flow
   - No "Congratulations" / "Success!" / "Awesome!" — replace with "Confirmed" / "Received"
   - No countdown timers
   - No "limited slots" or scarcity language
   - No emoji except 🪔 (diya) in confirmation
   - All CTAs use verbs of service: "Request pandit" not "Book now"
   - Hindi + English bilingual; Hindi primary on confirmation screens
   - No images of deceased or pyres
   - Avoid words: "deal", "offer", "save", "discount", "premium", "VIP"
   - Confirmation message reviewed by senior pandit before ship
   - Tone test: read aloud to someone who lost parent in last year
   - Legal: include cremation ground partnership disclaimer
9. **Data model sketch** — `antim_sanskar_bookings` table fields (deceased_name, deceased_age, deceased_gender, death_time, death_place, service_location, service_time, primary_contact, secondary_contact, assigned_pandit_id, status enum, sla_deadline, invoice_status). This sketch feeds R-Q4-E-001 engineer.
10. **5 wireframes ASCII** — one per booking step + one for family chat. Show layout, font emphasis, button placement.
11. **Success criteria** (verbatim from roadmap):
    - Populate during first sub-agent emission cycle
    (Add operational criteria too: CSAT 90%, 4h dispatch SLA met 95%, zero upsell complaints)
12. **Open questions for founder** — pricing tiers, pandit network capacity, cremation ground partnerships, legal liability framing.
