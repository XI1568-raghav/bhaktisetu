## Pillar page specs

### Pillar 1: `/festivals`
- **Route**: `/festivals`
- **Target keyword**: "Hindu festivals"
- **Audience**: devotees searching for festival dates, rituals, stories
- **Required sections** (≥4 H2):
  - 2026 Hindu festival calendar (table — list ≥12 major festivals with date, deity, key ritual)
  - Major festivals overview (one paragraph each for Diwali, Holi, Navratri, Janmashtami, Ganesh Chaturthi, Shivaratri, Rama Navami, Krishna Janmashtami, Dussehra, Makar Sankranti, Onam, Pongal)
  - Regional variations (North vs South vs East vs West — brief)
  - Rituals & samagri summary (link forward to `/shop` pillar)
  - FAQ (≥6 Q&A)
- **Cluster hooks** (in `cluster_links` frontmatter + Related Resources section): `/festivals/diwali`, `/festivals/holi`, `/festivals/navratri`, `/festivals/janmashtami`, `/festivals/ganesh-chaturthi`

### Pillar 2: `/yatra`
- **Route**: `/yatra`
- **Target keyword**: "Hindu pilgrimage yatra"
- **Audience**: pilgrims planning tirtha yatra
- **Required sections**:
  - Char Dham Yatra overview (Yamunotri, Gangotri, Kedarnath, Badrinath — one paragraph each + season)
  - 12 Jyotirlingas (list with locations — use standard published list, no fabrication)
  - 51 Shakti Peethas summary (group by region, name major ones)
  - Preparation checklist (physical, document, ritual items)
  - Best seasons & registration notes (Char Dham Yatra registration via `uttarakhandtourism.gov.in` — link outbound)
  - FAQ (≥6 Q&A)
- **Cluster hooks**: `/yatra/kashi`, `/yatra/tirupati`, `/yatra/vaishno-devi`, `/yatra/amarnath`, `/yatra/rameswaram`

### Pillar 3: `/astrology`
- **Route**: `/astrology`
- **Target keyword**: "Vedic astrology Jyotisha"
- **Audience**: seekers of jyotisha guidance (devotional framing only)
- **Required sections**:
  - 9 Grahas overview (Surya, Chandra, Mangala, Budha, Guru, Shukra, Shani, Rahu, Ketu)
  - 12 Rashi list (Mesha through Meena — Sanskrit + English)

  - Panchang basics (Tithi, Vaar, Nakshatra, Yoga, Karana)
  - **Disclaimer section** (REQUIRED, in its own H2): "Bhaktisetu astrology content is devotional and cultural. It is NOT a substitute for professional consultation with a qualified Jyotishi. For personal decisions, consult a practitioner."
  - FAQ on horoscope matching, Mangal Dosha, Sade Sati (≥6 Q&A)
- **Cluster hooks**: `/astrology/panchang`, `/astrology/mangal-dosha`, `/astrology/sade-sati`, `/astrology/kundali-matching`
- **Hard constraint**: No predictive claims about individuals. No dosha-severity scoring. Frame all Jyotisha as cultural/devotional knowledge.

- **Route**: `/shop`
