# Why this matters

BAPS (Bochasanwasi Akshar Purushottam Swaminarayan Sanstha) operates the largest organized
Hindu temple network in North America (100+ mandirs across US/UK/Canada) with a deeply
engaged Gujarati-NRI diaspora that maps 1:1 to Darshya's NRI ICP (Anand persona — 41, SF,
USD checkout, gift puja to parents in India).

A single warm intro into BAPS senior leadership via the Hindu American Foundation (HAF)
unlocks co-marketing access to ~250K active NRI devotee households, pilot deployment
during major utsavs, and the single highest-credibility trust signal in the US Hindu
diaspora. Estimated TAM unlock: 50K-150K NRI active users within 12 months.

Cold outreach to BAPS leadership has near-zero response rate. HAF, as the leading US Hindu
civic advocacy org, has standing convening relationships with BAPS and routinely warm-intros
vetted Hindu-tech founders.
