## Why east expansion (Q4)

The east is the highest-density devotional region of India after the Gangetic plain, but is the lowest-platform-penetrated tier-2 stretch. Sri Mandir + Utsav focus on Mumbai/Delhi/Bengaluru/Hyderabad. Bhaktisetu Q4 east expansion is greenfield supply + a downstream NRI bridge (Bengali, Bihari, Odia, Assamese diaspora are dense in US/UK/Gulf).

## Onboarding pipeline (founder workflow)

1. **Source** (Week 1-4): Founder visits each city 1-2× OR hires local coordinators. Tap WhatsApp groups, Pathshala alumni networks, temple precinct families.
2. **Pre-screen** (Week 2-5): 15-min phone call per candidate. Confirm willingness, pricing tier, weekly availability, language fluency.
3. **KYC submission** (Week 3-6): Pandit fills onboarding form at `/pandit/onboard` (web). Uploads docs.
4. **Verification** (Week 4-7): Founder OR coordinator does in-person interview + reference checks. Verify Aadhaar via portal.
5. **Activation** (Week 5-8): Profile published. First test booking (founder or seeded devotee). Payout flow validated.
6. **First-month review** (Week 8-10): Rating + repeat-rate check. Re-validate if any issues.

## Sampradaya specifics — east region

### Bihar (Patna)
- Mithila + Magadh Brahmin lineage
- Pitra Paksh / Shraadh peak (Sept-Oct) — Gaya 100km is global tarpan capital
- Customs cadence: Chhath (massive, Oct-Nov), Sama Chakeva, Vivaha Panchami
- Pricing skew: Pitra Paksh tarpan packages spike 3x in Sept-Oct window
- Local language tilt: Hindi + Bhojpuri + Maithili (some east-Bihar pandits)

### Odisha (Bhubaneswar)
- Jagannath sampradaya (distinct from generic Smarta Vaishnava)
- Daitapatis + Pati Mahapatras + Bramha Pandas at Puri are hereditary sevayats
- Customs cadence: Rath Yatra (June-Jul, massive), Snana Yatra, Niladri Vije, Pana Sankranti, Ratha Yatra of subsidiary temples
- Pricing skew: Niti-based pujas (early-morning Mangala Aarti, Sakala Dhupa) are sacred-window specific
- Local language: Odia primary; Hindi secondary
- Note: Puri Jagannath puja by non-Daitapati is contested — clearly label "in-temple by Daitapati" vs "home/external puja by Bhaktisetu pandit"

### Assam (Guwahati)
- Shakta-tantric tradition (Kamakhya) + Vaishnava (Sankaradeva tradition Ekasarana Dharma)
- Customs cadence: Ambubachi Mela (June, Kamakhya — temple closes 3 days), Durga Puja (Oct, Bengali-style), Bihu (3× a year — Bohag/Magh/Kati, secular but pandit-blessing in some homes)
- Pricing skew: Ambubachi window is closed for Kamakhya — no on-site pujas; remote sankalp possible
- Local language: Assamese primary; Bengali secondary (significant Bengali Hindu community); Hindi tertiary
- Note: Tantric Shakta pandits should be opt-in segment, not default-listed
