## Per-vendor KYC

- Portfolio review (50+ wedding samples)
- Insurance verification (₹10L general liability min)
- GST registration
- 5-year industry experience min
- Reference check w/ 3 prior bride/groom families

## Per-vendor contract

- 60-70% revenue to vendor / 20-30% Bhaktisetu commission
- Cancellation: 50% refund up to 7d before, 0% within 48hr
- Quality SLA: pre-event consult + day-of execution + 30-day photo delivery
- Repeat-issue clause: 2+ complaints in 90 days → suspension

## Pricing tiers (vendor-side)

| Basic | 30K (4hr) | 500/pax |
| Standard | 75K (8hr + video) | 800/pax |
