# Varanasi Expansion — 5 Temples + 15 Pandits + Yatra Gateway

> **Roadmap item**: R-Q2-G-008 (P1) · **Authored**: 2026-06-13

## Goal

Q2 expand Bhaktisetu Varanasi from current Kashi Vishwanath solo footprint → 5 temples + 15 verified pandits + yatra packages from Mumbai/Delhi/Bengaluru.

## Why Varanasi

- Single most-searched Hindu pilgrimage destination (~1M+ monthly Google searches India)
- High NRI emotional resonance (every Hindu plans a Kashi visit lifetime)
- Gateway to Buddhist (Sarnath) + Sufi (Banaras Sufi shrines) cross-pollination
- Strong shraddha/pind-daan economy (Gaya proximity 200km)

## 5 target temples Varanasi

1. **Kashi Vishwanath Temple** (already shipped) — Maha Jyotirlinga
2. **Sankat Mochan Hanuman** — popular pre-darshan stop
3. **Annapurna Temple** — Mata seva
4. **Mrityunjaya Mahadev** — death-conqueror Shiva
5. **Tulsi Manas Mandir** — Tulsidas + Ramcharitmanas

## 15 pandit onboarding plan

- Source: Kashi pandit collective (Punditgan Sabha)
- Selection: 5+ years experience + Sanskrit proficiency + video proof willingness
- Comp: 70-80% per puja (per R-Q2-L-002 contract template)
- Bench: 15 pandits + 5 reserve for festival peak demand

## Yatra packages (3 SKUs)

1. **Mumbai → Varanasi 3D/2N** — pilgrimage + 5 temples + 2 puja
2. **Delhi → Varanasi 4D/3N** — pilgrimage + 5 temples + 4 pujas + Sarnath
3. **Bengaluru → Varanasi 5D/4N** — pilgrimage + 5 temples + 4 pujas + Sarnath + Gaya extension

Pricing: ₹15K-30K all-in per pilgrim (flights + hotel + transport + puja + guide)
