# City Focus Decision — Bengaluru (single-city deep)

> **Roadmap item**: R-Q1-G-008 (P0, founder strategic decision) · **Authored**: 2026-06-12 · **Status**: decision — Bengaluru chosen. Other cities deferred.

## Decision

**Bhaktisetu commits 100% of Q1+Q2 supply-side founder hours to Bengaluru.** Varanasi (R-Q1-G-002) and Mumbai (R-Q1-G-003) are paused at planned status. Re-evaluate only after Bengaluru hits success criteria below OR fails kill-criteria below.

| Factor | Bengaluru | Varanasi | Mumbai |

| **Tech-savvy devotee density** | HIGH (IT corridor + 13M pop) | LOW (pilgrim-economy, low online-booking habit) | HIGH (but Mumbai-Razorpay-saturated market) |
| **Founder personal network** | HIGH (founder Bengaluru-based per project context) | NONE | LOW |
| **Pandit supply density** | MED-HIGH (Iyengar/Smarta/Madhva clusters; documented in S-005) | VERY HIGH (Kashi traditional center) but harder to recruit (institutional) | MED |
| **Verified temple availability** | MED-HIGH (30 prospects researched in S-005) | HIGH but elder-trustee dominant, slower | MED |
| **NRI feedback loop** | STRONG (Bengaluru IT diaspora US/UK base) | WEAK (mostly inbound pilgrim, not diaspora-driven) | STRONG (Mumbai diaspora) |
| **Devotional spend per capita** | HIGH (IT salaries + brahmin community concentration) | MED (locals; pilgrims drive volume but not booking-platform usage) | HIGH |
| **Competitive intensity** | LOW (Sri Mandir focused Tier-1-Tier-2 elsewhere) | MED (multiple online-puja apps targeting Kashi) | HIGH (Razorpay-heavy fintech context, Mumbai PSP-pampered users) |
| **Operational complexity** | LOW (founder local, easy in-person temple visits) | HIGH (founder must travel, multi-day trips for site visits) | MED |
| **Customer-trust building** | FAST (founder = local face, weekly in-person follow-ups) | SLOW (founder = outsider, longer trust cycle) | MED |
| **Score (Bhaktisetu fit)** | **9/10** | 6/10 | 7/10 |

## Why NOT spread across 3 cities

**Spread thesis (rejected)**: "more cities = more revenue surface". Counter-arg per `docs/ceo-roadmap-2026-05-27.md` + R-Q1-G-008 spec:

1. **Founder hours are finite**. ~30 supply-side founder hours/week. Across 3 cities = 10 hr/city = no meaningful traction anywhere.
2. **Trust requires repetition**. Temple TPs need 3-5 in-person touches before signing. Travel between BLR-Mumbai-Varanasi consumes 60% of founder week. Net: 4 hr/city/wk = insufficient.
3. **Brand-via-density wedge**. 10 Bengaluru temples on Bhaktisetu beats 3 temples scattered across 3 cities. Devotee local-network effect (1 happy BLR family tells 5 more BLR families).
4. **Operational drag** at 3-city scale: per-city payment-method preferences, language nuances, festival calendar variations. Manage 1 city well first.
5. **Investor narrative**. Pre-seed pitch lands stronger with "20 Bengaluru temples, ₹3L MRR" than "5 temples each across BLR/Mumbai/Varanasi, ₹1L total". Depth > breadth.

Hit ALL 8 → unlock Varanasi (R-Q1-G-002) + Mumbai (R-Q1-G-003) sequentially.

## Kill criteria — switch trigger

If by end of Q2 ANY of the following:

| Condition | Action |
|---|---|
| <3 temples onboarded | Re-evaluate temple-side outreach strategy (founder pivot OR new agent for temple-side outreach research) |
| <10 pandits onboarded | Re-evaluate pandit acquisition channel (W2-style hire instead of marketplace?) |
| <500 BLR MAU | Re-evaluate marketing channel mix (founder build-in-public Twitter R-Q1-M-013 not landing → try Reddit/WA groups) |
| <10 paid bookings/month | Re-evaluate pricing OR puja types offered (maybe Bengaluru wants specific sampradaya pujas we don't have) |
| Aggregate Bengaluru-related cost > ₹50K/month w/o offsetting revenue | Pause Bengaluru deep-dive, re-think product-market-fit thesis |

If ANY 3 of 5 kill criteria fire by Q2-end → STOP Bengaluru deep-dive + write 1-pager RCA + open Varanasi-OR-Mumbai-as-alternative debate w/ board.
