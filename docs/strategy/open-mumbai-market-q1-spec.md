# Open Mumbai Market — Third City

> **Roadmap item**: R-Q1-G-003 (P0) · **Authored**: 2026-06-13

## Goal

Q1 open Mumbai as Bhaktisetu's third city (after Bengaluru + Varanasi). 8 verified temples + 30 verified pandits + 1K MAU.

## Why Mumbai third

- Largest India urban tier-1 by population
- High consumer-spend (puja avg ticket ₹3-5K vs ₹1-2K tier-2)
- Mahalakshmi + Siddhivinayak = #2 + #3 most-searched India temples
- Strong NRI emigrant pattern (Mumbai → US/UK heavy)
- Tech-savvy customer base (Razorpay penetration high)

## 8 target temples

1. **Siddhivinayak** (Mumbai's #1 temple)
2. **Mahalakshmi** (Mumbai's #2)
3. **Babulnath Mahadev**
4. **Walkeshwar (Banganga)**
5. **ISKCON Juhu** (per R-Q2-S-001 ISKCON partnership)
6. **Mumbadevi**
7. **Mount Mary (Bandra)** — cross-tradition, NRI-friendly
8. **Shree Krishna Mandir Andheri**
