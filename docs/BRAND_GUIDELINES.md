# Poojly Brand Guidelines

> Version 1.0 | April 2026

---

## 1. Brand Overview

### Brand Name
**Poojly** (Sanskrit: Bhakti = devotion, Setu = bridge)

### Tagline
**"Bridging You to the Divine"**

### Mission
To make authentic spiritual experiences accessible to every devotee, anywhere in the world, through technology that respects and preserves tradition.

### Brand Essence
Poojly stands at the intersection of ancient devotion and modern convenience. We are not replacing the temple visit -- we are extending the temple's reach. Every pixel, every interaction, every feature must honor the sacredness of the traditions we serve.

## 2. Logo

### Primary Logo
The Poojly logo consists of two elements:
1. **The Bridge Arc (Setu)** -- A clean, upward-curving arc representing the bridge between the devotee and the divine.
2. **The Diya Flame** -- A traditional oil lamp flame sitting at the apex of the bridge, symbolizing devotion, knowledge, and spiritual light.

### Logo Files
| File | Usage |
|------|-------|
| `logo.svg` | Standard logo for light backgrounds |
| `logo-dark.svg` | Logo for dark backgrounds |
| `favicon.svg` | Simplified icon for favicons and small contexts |

### Clear Space
Maintain a minimum clear space around the logo equal to the height of the flame element on all sides. No other graphic elements, text, or borders should enter this zone.

### Minimum Size
- **Digital**: Minimum 24px height for the icon mark, 120px width for the full lockup
- **Print**: Minimum 10mm height for the icon mark, 30mm width for the full lockup

### Logo Misuse -- Do Not:
- Rotate or skew the logo
- Change the logo colors outside the approved palette
- Add drop shadows, glows, or 3D effects
- Place the logo on busy or low-contrast backgrounds
- Stretch or compress the logo disproportionately
- Rearrange the logo elements
- Add a border or outline around the logo
- Use the logo as a pattern or repeating element

- Hindi text should use Noto Sans Devanagari, not the Latin Noto Sans

- Use sentence case for all UI text (not Title Case or ALL CAPS, except overlines)
- Avoid font sizes below 12px for accessibility

## 5. Voice and Tone

### Brand Voice
Poojly speaks with **warmth**, **reverence**, and **clarity**. We are a trusted companion on the spiritual journey, not a salesperson.

### Voice Attributes

| Attribute | What It Means | Example |
|-----------|---------------|---------|
| **Reverent** | We honor traditions and treat spiritual content with respect | "Begin your sacred journey with live darshan" (not "Watch temples live!") |
| **Warm** | We are welcoming, inclusive, and encouraging | "Welcome back, devotee" (not "Login successful") |
| **Clear** | We communicate simply and directly, no jargon barriers | "Your puja is confirmed for Thursday" (not "Booking ID #123 status: CONFIRMED") |
| **Trustworthy** | We are transparent about pricing, timings, and processes | "Includes prasad delivery (2-3 days)" (not hidden fees) |
| **Inclusive** | We welcome all devotees regardless of background | "Explore traditions" (not "For Hindus only") |

### Tone Variations by Context

| Context | Tone | Example |
|---------|------|---------|
| Home page / Marketing | Inspirational, uplifting | "Where devotion meets the digital age" |
| Booking flow | Clear, reassuring | "Your Rudrabhishek at Kashi Vishwanath is confirmed" |
| Error states | Gentle, helpful | "We could not process your payment. Please try again or choose another method." |
| Push notifications | Personal, timely | "Mangal Aarti at Kashi begins in 5 minutes" |
| Content pages | Educational, respectful | "The Hanuman Chalisa, composed by Tulsidas..." |
| Success states | Celebratory, warm | "Your booking is confirmed. May your prayers be answered." |

### Language Guidelines
- Use "devotee" or "you", never "user" or "customer" in devotee-facing UI
- Prefer Sanskrit/Hindi terms with English explanations: "Sankalp (prayer intention)"
- Never use casual slang or overly corporate language
- Avoid exclamation marks in spiritual/ritual contexts
- Dates should include both Gregorian and Panchang where relevant

## 6. Photography Style Guide

### Temple Photography
- Show the architecture and grandeur of temples
- Capture the warm, golden light of diyas and aarti
- Prefer shots during golden hour or with warm interior lighting
- Include human elements (devotees, priests) for scale and emotion
- Avoid overly processed or HDR-heavy editing

### Deity Images
- Use high-quality photographs of actual temple murtis (idols)
- Maintain respectful framing -- the full deity should be visible
- Warm color temperature preferred
- No AI-generated deity images

### Product Photography
- Clean, well-lit product shots on neutral backgrounds
- Show products in context where possible (a diya lit, a mala being worn)
- Consistent aspect ratios within product grids

### People Photography
- Show diverse devotees of all ages and backgrounds
- Capture genuine moments of devotion, not posed shots
- Priests should appear professional and approachable
- Respect privacy -- no identifiable faces without consent

### Image Technical Requirements
- Use Unsplash URLs with consistent parameters: `?w=WIDTH&h=HEIGHT&fit=crop&q=80`
- Standard aspect ratios: 4:3 for cards, 16:9 for banners, 1:1 for avatars
- All images must have meaningful alt text describing the content
- Lazy load all images below the fold
- Use next/image with proper width/height for Core Web Vitals

## 7. Iconography

### Icon System
- Primary icon set: **Lucide React** (consistent line-weight, clean aesthetic)
- Icon size: 16px for inline, 20px for navigation, 24px for feature icons
- Stroke width: 1.5px (Lucide default)
- Color: Inherit from text color, or use Saffron 600 for active/highlighted states

### Custom Icons
If Lucide does not have an appropriate icon for a Hindu/spiritual concept, create custom SVG icons following:
- 24x24 viewbox
- 1.5px stroke weight
- Rounded line caps and joins
- Same visual weight as Lucide icons

## 8. Component Patterns

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Saffron 600 background, white text, rounded-lg
- **Secondary**: Surface 100 background, Surface 800 text, rounded-lg
- **Ghost**: Transparent background, Surface 600 text, hover Surface 100
- **Danger**: Error red background, white text (for destructive actions only)

### Cards
- Background: White with subtle surface-200 border
- Border radius: 12px (rounded-xl)
- Shadow: Minimal, only on hover (shadow-sm to shadow-md transition)
- Padding: 16px-24px depending on content density

### Spacing
- Base unit: 4px
- Component padding: 12px-24px
- Section spacing: 48px-80px
- Use Tailwind's spacing scale consistently

---

## 9. Motion and Animation

### Principles
- **Purposeful**: Every animation should serve a purpose (feedback, orientation, delight)
- **Subtle**: Keep animations small and quick -- this is a spiritual app, not a game
- **Respectful**: No flashy animations on sacred content (deity images, mantra text)

### Timing
- Micro-interactions: 150ms (hover, focus, toggle)
- Transitions: 200-300ms (page transitions, modals)
- Entrance animations: 300-500ms (cards loading, content appearing)
- Easing: `ease-out` for entrances, `ease-in-out` for state changes

---

## 10. Accessibility

### Requirements
- WCAG 2.1 AA compliance minimum
- All images must have descriptive alt text
- All interactive elements must be keyboard accessible
- Focus indicators must be clearly visible
- Support screen readers with proper ARIA labels
- Touch targets minimum 44x44px on mobile
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` for all animations
- Support `prefers-color-scheme` for future dark mode

### Language
- Support bilingual UI (English + Hindi)
- Use `lang` attributes on Hindi text blocks
- Ensure Devanagari text renders correctly across devices

## 11. Brand Applications

### Favicon
Use `favicon.svg` -- the simplified bridge+flame mark. The SVG format ensures crisp rendering at all sizes.

### Open Graph / Social Media
- Image size: 1200x630px
- Include the logo mark and tagline
- Use warm, inviting temple imagery as background
- Text overlay should be readable on mobile

### App Icon (PWA/Mobile)
- Use the bridge+flame icon mark on a saffron-to-gold gradient background
- Ensure the icon is recognizable at 48x48px
- Rounded corners as per platform guidelines

### Email
- Use the logo in the header
- Stick to system fonts as fallback (Arial, sans-serif)
- Saffron 600 for CTAs
- Surface 50 background
