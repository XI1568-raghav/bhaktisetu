## Per-competitor deep dives

### 1. Sri Mandir (AppsForBharat)

**Positioning.** "Your Own Temple — India's Largest App for Hindu Devotees." Everyday devotee, not premium NRI.

**Scale.** 30M+ users, 30+ countries, 1L+ Play Store ratings at 4.5★, 3M+ services completed.

**Offering.** Puja at 100+ temples, chadhava, daily darshan, 5,000+ ad-free aarti/chalisa/mantra/bhajan, panchang, India-only astrologer consults, separate srimandir.store. Sister apps: Daily Mantra, Daily Darshan, SuperAstro.

**Pricing.** Homepage shows zero numbers — price reveals post-selection. Free for browsing, transactional for services.

**Trust signals.** Named city testimonials, Hindi-press logos (Dainik Bhaskar, Jagran, Amar Ujala, Hindustan, Navbharat Times), ISO 27001, Digital India badge, Razorpay. **No video proof claim.**

**Distribution edge.** Four-year head start (launched Feb 2021). Hindi-press credibility stack. Multi-app cross-install funnels.

**Match.** Their 5,000+ ad-free content library is table stakes. Their daily darshan habit loop is the stickiness driver — Darshya needs an equivalent daily trigger that isn't gated on a paid puja.

**Attack.** No mandatory video proof, no gaushala, no integrated wellness/Jitsi consults, no 80G automation, no NRI-premium tier. They treat global users as the same SKU as domestic — that's ARPU they're leaving on the table.

### 2. Vama

**Positioning.** "VAMA – Online Puja, Chadhava & Astrology Services." Premium-leaning, app-first.

**Scale.** Not disclosed publicly. The vama.app homepage renders as a JS loading shell — no marketing copy extractable without app hydration. Pitch deck cites ₹19.5 Cr revenue (third-party trackers) — directionally a small fraction of Sri Mandir.

**Offering (inferred).** Puja booking, chadhava, astrology. No public evidence of darshan streaming, content library, gaushala, wellness, or NRI tier.

**Pricing.** Not visible. Per-puja price points reportedly above Sri Mandir's — unverified.

**Trust signals.** None extractable from the loading-state page.

**Take.** Treat Vama as a brand-only competitor at the marketing layer until their site discloses real product copy. Pure transactional app, not a content threat. Darshya wins on content + gaushala + NRI tier + 80G without needing to out-execute on puja depth.

---

### 3. Utsav App

**Positioning.** "Experience Hindu Pujas & Rituals with Utsav." Tight puja-booking + prasad-to-home loop, less spread than Sri Mandir.

**Scale.** 10L+ devotees (1M+), 4.6★. About 1/30th of Sri Mandir's claimed base.

**Offering.** Puja booking with named priests, personalized puja photo/video (clip, not full video proof), prasad delivery, panchang, rashifal, "Dharmik Gyan" content, "Siddha Store" gifting. No gaushala, wellness, or NRI tier.

**Pricing.** Hidden on homepage; same anti-friction pattern as Sri Mandir.

**Trust signals.** "9/10 devotees rate Utsav 5★", named city testimonials (Nagpur, Mumbai, Lucknow, Rajasthan), per-event devotee counts ("11.8k+" at Hanuman Garhi, "5.6k+" at Akalipur Kali), "Authentic Prasad" badge. **WhatsApp puja video delivery** — closer to Darshya's video-proof posture than any other competitor.

**Distribution.** WhatsApp video distribution is clever — forwarded family videos = free CAC. 30-city Panchang SEO footprint.

**Take.** **Utsav is the closest threat to Darshya's video-proof wedge.** They already deliver puja clips via WhatsApp. Darshya has to make the distinction obvious: Utsav sends "a video"; Darshya gates priest payout on full video proof acceptance. Outside that overlap, Utsav has no live darshan, no AI guidance, no gaushala, no NRI tier, no 80G automation.

### 4. AppsForBharat (parent)

Not a direct app — corporate hub for Sri Mandir, Daily Mantra, Daily Darshan, SuperAstro.

**Why it matters.** Only multi-app faith-tech platform in the market. Public roadmap: App launch Feb 2021 → Chadhava Aug 2022 → Puja Dec 2022 → Global Seva Jan 2024 → Sri Yatra + Astrology Jan 2025. One new vertical per year. Within 24–36 months they'll have darshan + astrology + yatra + e-commerce — the exact surface Darshya is trying to occupy.

**Implication.** Darshya cannot win as "Sri Mandir with more verticals" three years out — AppsForBharat will be there too. The wedge must be (a) verification/trust (video proof + 80G + verified-temple gating), (b) NRI premium pricing rigour, (c) AI Pandit + sankalp layer. Probably all three.

---

### 5. VedicMeet

**Positioning.** "India's Most Trusted Problem-Solving App." Pure astrology / wellness consultation — not a religious-services aggregator.

**Scale.** No download counts, user figures, or ratings published.

**Offering.** Online astrologers (chat/call, 24/7), appointment booking, online kundali, vastu compass, tarot, numerology, palmistry, reiki, graphology, mantra meditation + calming music, anonymous community, daily horoscopes, compatibility. **No darshan, no puja booking, no temple count, no gaushala, no NRI services, no store.**

**Pricing.** No numbers on page. Claims "transparent pricing" without showing it (a yellow flag).

**Trust signals.** Generic ("secure payments," "helpful customer support," "years of experience"). No testimonials, no review count, no video proof.

**Match-or-counter.** Not really a competitor to Darshya's full surface. They're a competitor only to Darshya's astrology vertical. Treat them as benchmark for *how to package consultation flows* (anonymous community, vastu compass, tarot variety) — not as a threat to the super-app thesis.

**Where Darshya can attack.** Everywhere outside astrology. Within astrology, Darshya should match their breadth of modalities (numerology, palmistry, vastu) rather than only kundali + chat.

## Synthesis

### Darshya's defensible wedge

Five things, in order of how hard they are to copy:

1. **Video proof gated to priest payout.** Not "a video gets sent" (Utsav already does). The contractual primitive: priest doesn't receive commission-net payout until video proof is uploaded and accepted. Competitors would have to rebuild priest-onboarding contracts to match.
2. **80G receipt automation** with priest/temple PAN + donee 80G regn. No competitor surfaces this. NRI taxpayer segment cares deeply; compliance plumbing (DR-YYYYMMDD-XXXX numbering) is non-trivial.
3. **9–10× NRI pricing tier** with USD/GBP/AED rendering. Sri Mandir is global but doesn't price-discriminate. Darshya's $4.99 → $59.99 ladder is rational ARPU capture nobody else is doing.
4. **AI Pandit grounded in Bhagavad Gita RAG with citations.** SuperAstro is AI for prediction, not scripture. "Every answer cites a verse" is defensible at the trust layer.
5. **Gaushala vertical.** Not a huge revenue line on its own, but the only Hindu-charity primitive any competitor offers — pairs with 80G into a "punya + tax-deductible" loop NRIs will pay for.

### Three features competitors have that Darshya should add

1. **Chadhava (sacred offerings).** Sri Mandir's flagship, Utsav's hook. Low-ticket repeat-purchase that drives daily-active behaviour in a way puja booking (an event) cannot. Darshya has it on roadmap — pull into launch SKU, not Phase 2.
2. **WhatsApp video distribution.** Utsav forwards puja videos via WhatsApp, creating family-side virality (Bangalore daughter-in-law forwards to Indore in-laws). Darshya should auto-route video proof to up to 5 WhatsApp numbers per booking — viral coefficient > 1 from this feature alone.
3. **Daily-trigger habit loop.** Sri Mandir's daily darshan + Daily Mantra create a daily open reason. Darshya's launch plan leans on event triggers (booking, festival). Without a daily surface (sunrise aarti push, panchang notification, mantra of the day), retention collapses between bookings.

### Three things Darshya should NOT copy

1. **Hidden pricing on landing pages.** Incumbents do this because they have ASO and brand. Darshya is a challenger and should *lead with price* — the $14.99/mo NRI tier is itself a premium signal vs. Sri Mandir's mass-market posture.
2. **Multi-app portfolio (AppsForBharat model).** Four separate apps requires four maintenance surfaces. With $0 budget, the super-app thesis is the right answer — don't dilute it.
3. **Astrologer-marketplace race to the bottom.** AstroTalk, VedicMeet compete on astrologer count. 500 astrologers = 500 KYC + payout + dispute SLAs. Darshya's 15 verified + AI Pandit is the deliberately tighter SKU. Chase quality, not count.

### Pricing posture

**Hybrid, leaning premium.**

The market today: mass-market floor (Sri Mandir, Utsav — free browsing, transactional pujas, no public price anchor); premium-implied middle (Vama); astrology specialists on per-minute consult (VedicMeet). **No anchor exists for a $14.99/mo super-app subscription.** Darshya should set it.

- **Domestic**: free tier + ₹49/mo Bhakti Pass + per-puja transactional. Match Sri Mandir on the free tier so ASO conversions work; lead acquisition on the Bhakti Pass anchor — it's the recurring-revenue story for investors.
- **NRI**: aggressively premium ($14.99 / $29.99 / $59.99). Willingness-to-pay is well above the domestic floor, no competitor prices for it. Don't apologise for the gap — make it the headline ("authentic puja + 80G receipt + video proof = premium-priced").
- **Avoid**: per-minute astrology pricing. Race-to-bottom commodity. Bundle astrology into the subscription.

Honest read: Sri Mandir will likely launch a premium tier within 12–18 months once Darshya validates the willingness-to-pay. Darshya's window is the next 12 months to lock NRI users into annual subscriptions before the incumbent moves.
