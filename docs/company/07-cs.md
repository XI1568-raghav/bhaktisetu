## 1. Top 5 complaint types (frequency × severity)

### 1. "Priest never showed / late by 30+ min" (HIGH × HIGH)
- **Likely root cause**: no priest-side reminder, no booking-day check-in. `apps/api/src/routes/conversations.ts` shows chat is gated on `puja_booking` status not in `pending_payment`/`cancelled`/`refunded` (lines 89–98), but there is no scheduled "T-60min priest ping" job. Priest tap to confirm "I'm preparing" is missing.
- **Apology (Hi-En)**: "Pranam {name} ji, aapne is puja ke liye intezaar kiya — yeh humari taraf se galti hai. Pandit ji se baat ki gayi hai, aur aapko aaj hi dobara schedule kiya jaa raha hai (no extra charge). Iske saath ek 10% credit aapke wallet mein daal diya hai."
- **Refund rule**: 100% refund OR free reschedule + 10% wallet credit (matches `refunds/page.tsx` row 87 "puja not performed"). Devotee picks.

### 2. "Video proof never arrived" (HIGH × HIGH)
- **Likely root cause**: priest forgot to upload; no enforcement gate before payout claim. CLAUDE.md line "Video proof MANDATORY for puja bookings — priest uploads before payout" is the *policy*, but only as strong as the cron that nags the priest. Render free tier has no cron — this gap is real.
- **Apology**: "Aapki puja zaroor hui — humare paas pandit ji ki confirmation hai. Lekin video humein 48 ghante mein nahi mila, jo aapka haq tha. Hum aaj raat tak video bhejenge, ya full refund — aap batayein."
- **Refund rule**: per refunds/page.tsx row 88 — "Full refund or reschedule option." If video lands within 96h, offer 25% credit instead of refund (preserves priest payout).

### 3. "Payment deducted, booking not confirmed" (MEDIUM × HIGH)
- **Likely root cause**: Razorpay webhook race or Render cold-start dropping the callback. Standard PSP-integration failure.
- **Apology**: "Aapka paisa kat gaya lekin booking confirm nahi hui — hum samajh sakte hain yeh kitna pareshan karne wala hai. Within 2 hours, ya toh booking confirm hogi ya 100% refund — koi documentation nahi maangenge."
- **Refund rule**: auto-refund within 2h if no booking row exists matching the Razorpay order_id. No questions asked.

### 4. "Live darshan stream froze / pixelated during aarti" (MEDIUM × HIGH)
- **Likely root cause**: AWS IVS is web-only (CLAUDE.md), mobile users on poor 4G hit transcoding fallbacks. Aarti timing peaks load.
- **Apology**: "Aarti ke samay stream ruk gaya — yeh sabse zaroori moment tha aur hum nahi de paaye. Recording link bhej rahe hain, aur agle 7 din free Premium darshan."
- **Refund rule**: free service today (refunds/page.tsx §6), so issue 7-day premium credit + send recording.

### 5. "Prasad never delivered / arrived spoiled" (MEDIUM × MEDIUM)
- **Likely root cause**: courier failure on consumable items; refunds policy says prasad is non-returnable for sanctity (refunds/page.tsx §3 "Non-Returnable Items").
- **Apology**: "Prasad ka pahunchna utna hi zaroori hai jitna puja ka hona — hum maafi maangte hain. Photo bhejiye, hum 100% refund + agli puja par free prasad bhejenge."
- **Refund rule**: photo evidence → 100% refund of prasad portion + free prasad on next booking. Override the "non-returnable" line for hygiene/damage cases.

## 2. Tier-1 macro library (8 replies)

1. **Booking confirmed**: "Pranam {name} ji, aapki {puja} {date} ko confirm hai. Sankalp details aapko 24 ghante pehle WhatsApp par milengi. Booking ID: {BS-id}."
2. **Refund timeline**: "Aapka refund process ho gaya hai — UPI mein 1-3 din, card mein 5-7 din lagte hain (bank-side). Reference: {refund_id}. Agar 7 din mein nahi aaye, yahin reply karein."
3. **Priest no-show**: "Yeh humari taraf se sharm ki baat hai. Aaj hi reschedule kar dete hain (no charge) ya full refund + 10% wallet credit. Aap batayein."
4. **Payment stuck**: "Paisa kat gaya, booking nahi dikh rahi — hum check kar rahe hain. Agle 2 ghante mein ya toh booking active hogi ya refund. Aapko kuch bhi karne ki zaroorat nahi."
5. **Stream not loading**: "Stream check kar liya — abhi temple side se issue hai. 5 min mein wapas chalu ho jana chahiye. Aap is link par recording dekh sakte hain: {url}."
6. **Order tracking**: "Aapka order {id} {status} hai, courier: {courier} ({tracking}). Predicted delivery: {date}. Damage ho to 48 ghante mein photo bhejein, full replacement."
7. **Gotra / sankalp confusion**: "Sankalp ke liye aapka naam, gotra, aur nakshatra chahiye. Gotra na pata ho to 'Kashyap' likh dein — yeh standard hai jab gotra unknown ho. Pandit ji handle kar lenge."
8. **NRI pricing question**: "NRI pricing temple ke special arrangements aur dollar-INR conversion ke saath aata hai (pandit ji ko priority slot, English commentary, recorded video courier). Detailed breakup: {link}."

Tone rule: open with the devotee's name + "Pranam ji" or acknowledgement of the disruption *before* any policy quote. Never lead with "Per our policy."

## 3. Routing rules (solo founder)

**Immediately escalate (phone within 30 min, founder personally):**
Keywords — `priest didn't come`, `pandit nahi aaya`, `paisa kata`, `money deducted`, `legal`, `consumer court`, `Twitter`, `media`, `fraud`, `refund denied`, `death`, `shraddh`, `antyesti`, `bereavement`. Also: any 1-star rating submitted via the `RatingBar` (Inbox.tsx:443) on a high-value puja (>₹2000).

**Auto-respond (macro within 2 min):**
Keywords — `booking confirmed?`, `track`, `delivery`, `when refund`, `refund status`, `gotra`, `sankalp`, `nakshatra`. Acknowledge + macro 1/2/6/7.

**End-of-day queue (founder reviews 9pm IST):**
Everything else — feature requests, "can you add X temple," general feedback, NRI questions without urgency, stream quality complaints that resolved themselves.

Implementation: simple keyword filter on inbound `/messages` body in conversations.ts before the founder gets a push. The 60/min send rate limit (line 47) is fine; we don't need a separate CS rate limit.

## 4. NPS-equivalent for devotional service

Standard NPS ("recommend to a friend") is wrong here — devotion is personal, not viral. Three candidates:

1. **"Would you ask this priest to perform a puja for your own family next time?"** — measures trust, not promotion. The devotee's family is the ultimate stakeholder; this is sharper than recommendation.
2. **"Did this puja feel as meaningful as one performed in person?"** — measures whether we cleared the spiritual-equivalence bar, which is the actual product promise. 1-5 scale.
3. **"Will you light a diya at home today because of this experience?"** — behavioural proxy. If yes, we created a moment, not a transaction.

**Recommend #1 as primary.** It surfaces priest-level quality (which is what we can fix), it's emotionally loaded enough to filter polite 4s from real 5s, and "for my family" is the highest-trust frame in Indian households. Survey via `RatingBar` after `callEnded` (Inbox.tsx:209), so we capture it while the experience is fresh.

## 5. First 10 reviews — seeded authentically

1. **Founder calls every devotee personally after their first puja.** No script — actual conversation, ask what was missing. The ones who say "really? you called me?" leave 5-star reviews on their own. This works because puja booking volume is low (<5/day at launch); doesn't scale past ~30/day, but that's a problem for later.
2. **Send unrequested video footage extras** — not just the mandatory video proof, but a 30-sec clip of the priest *introducing himself* before sankalp. Costs the priest 30 seconds; devotees screenshot and share. Tied to existing video-proof pipeline.
3. **Free 80G receipt with explanation video** for every donation/seva — most platforms make you ask. Ours arrives by email same day with a 60-sec founder-recorded explainer of how the seva was used. Receipt format `DR-YYYYMMDD-XXXX` already exists; the explainer video is the unlock. Devotees post the receipt on family WhatsApp; review follows.

## 6. Churn signals (pre-emptive)

1. **Devotee opens a thread post-puja but sends no message** (Inbox.tsx:209 `messages.length === 0` after a closed booking). Means they're hesitating to complain — anger they swallowed. **Action**: founder DMs within 1 hour: "Aapne thread khola lekin kuch likha nahi — sab theek tha?"
2. **Rating submitted = 3 stars with no body text** (RatingBar onSubmit, Inbox.tsx:398). Three stars in Indian rating culture = "polite disappointment." **Action**: auto-trigger a follow-up macro asking one specific question: "Kya pandit ji ki audio clear thi?" — narrow it so they answer.
3. **Logged-in devotee browses a puja category 3+ times across 14 days but never books** (PostHog event). Means trust gap, not interest gap. **Action**: ₹100 first-puja credit by email, expires in 7 days. Don't make it a coupon; phrase it as "humein launch mein support karne ke liye dhanyavaad."

The cheap one is signal #1 — silence after engagement is the loudest churn signal in devotional commerce, and we can detect it for free with existing Prisma data.
