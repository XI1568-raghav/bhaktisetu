## 1. Purpose & Target

Founder calls or WhatsApps **20 temples per day in Varanasi** with a single goal: secure an introduction to **at least one pandit** at that temple (mahant, purohit, or temple-affiliated priest). Success rate target: **5 pandit-intros per 20 calls = 25% intro conversion**. With 50% intro→verified conversion (KYC complete + 1 paid puja in 30d), this yields **~25 verified pandits per week**, fully clearing the 50-pandit target by week 3.

Calls are not pitches to the temple — they are **referral asks**. The temple is the gatekeeper; the pandit is the customer.

## 2. Pre-call Checklist

Before dialing, fill these 5 fields in the call-log row (Section 9):

| Field | Source | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Temple name (official + colloquial) | Google Maps / `docs/sales/varanasi-temples-10-prospects.md` | Open with correct name = signals research |
| Mahant / priest-in-charge name | Temple website, Justdial, Google reviews | "Speak to Sharma-ji" beats "speak to someone" |
| Presiding deity | Temple page, Wikipedia | Lets you reference the right aarti/puja in pitch |
| Peak puja days | Local panchang, devotee reviews | Don't call during peak hours; respect their bandwidth |
| Prior context (if any) | WhatsApp tracker, past intros | "Last week Pankaj-ji from Sankat Mochan suggested I call you" |

If any field is blank, **do not dial**. Spend 3 minutes researching first.

## 3. Hindi Phone Script (~90 seconds)

> Devanagari below. Use polite Banarasi-Hindi register. Honorifics: **पंडित जी / महाराज जी / गुरुजी**. [PAUSE] = wait 1.5–2 s. [SOFT] = lower volume, slower cadence.

### Opening (10 s)

> "नमस्ते पंडित जी 🙏 [PAUSE] मैं रघव बोल रहा हूँ, **दर्शय** से — हम वाराणसी के मंदिरों के साथ काम कर रहे हैं ताकि दूर बैठे भक्त ऑनलाइन पूजा करवा सकें। [PAUSE] क्या आपके पास दो मिनट हैं?"

(If "अभी व्यस्त हूँ" — "ठीक है महाराज जी, मैं शाम को 6 बजे के बाद फिर से संपर्क करूँ?" — log callback, end call.)

### Value prop (30 s)

> [SOFT] "महाराज जी, हमारी एक छोटी सी request है। हम पंडित जी को **बिना किसी upfront cost के अपनी services list करने का मौका** दे रहे हैं। [PAUSE]
>
> भक्त ऐप पर पूजा book करता है, पंडित जी अपने temple में पूजा करते हैं, हम **video proof** record करके भक्त को भेजते हैं — और booking का जो amount आता है, उसका बड़ा हिस्सा सीधे पंडित जी के bank account में जाता है। [PAUSE]
>
> कोई joining fee नहीं, कोई monthly charge नहीं — सिर्फ booking होने पर revenue share। पंडित जी की reputation और bookings दोनों बढ़ती हैं।"

### Ask (20 s)

> "महाराज जी, क्या आप अपने मंदिर के **एक या दो पंडितों से मेरी बात करवा सकते हैं**? [PAUSE] बस 10 मिनट का intro call — अगर interest हो तो आगे बढ़ें, नहीं तो भी कोई दिक्कत नहीं।
>
> क्या मैं उनका WhatsApp number ले सकता हूँ — या आप मेरा number उन्हें दे देंगे?"

### Close (10 s)

> "बहुत-बहुत धन्यवाद पंडित जी 🙏 [PAUSE] मैं अभी आपको WhatsApp पर एक short message भेज रहा हूँ — जिसमें हमारी website का link है। आप उसे पंडित जी को forward कर दीजिए। [PAUSE]
>
> आपका दिन शुभ हो। हर हर महादेव।"

(Hang up. **Send WhatsApp within 5 minutes** — script in Section 5.)

## 4. English Phone Script (~90 seconds)

> For younger temple administrators (urban temples, English-comfortable). Same structure as Hindi.

### Opening (10 s)

> "Namaste, am I speaking with [Name]-ji? My name is Raghav, I'm calling from **Darshya** — we work with temples in Varanasi to help devotees abroad book authentic pujas online. Do you have two minutes?"

### Value prop (30 s)

> "We're reaching out because we'd love to give the pandits at your temple a chance to list their services on our platform — with **zero upfront cost, zero monthly fee**. Devotees book a puja through our app, the pandit performs it at the temple, we record video proof, and the majority of the booking revenue goes directly to the pandit's bank account.
>
> No joining fee. No monthly charge. Just a small revenue share per completed booking. It increases visibility and adds a verified-pandit badge to their profile."

### Ask (20 s)

> "Would you be willing to **connect us with one or two pandits at your temple**? Just a 10-minute introductory conversation — if they're interested, we proceed; if not, no obligation.
>
> Could you share their WhatsApp number, or pass mine along to them?"

### Close (10 s)

> "Thank you so much — I really appreciate your time. I'll send you a short WhatsApp message right after this with our website link, which you can forward. Have a wonderful day. Hari Om."

## 8. Objection Handlers (3)

### O1 — "Hum already manually book karte hain, app ki zaroorat nahi"

> "We already book pujas manually, no need for an app."

- **Root concern**: status quo bias + perceived disintermediation risk.
- **Hindi rebuttal**: "बिलकुल सही महाराज जी — हम आपकी existing bookings में कोई दखल नहीं देते। हमारा app सिर्फ उन भक्तों तक पहुँचता है जो विदेश में हैं या जो अभी आपको नहीं जानते — यानी ये **additional revenue** है, replacement नहीं।"
- **English rebuttal**: "Absolutely — we don't interfere with your existing bookings at all. Our app reaches only devotees who are abroad or who don't know you yet. So this is **additional revenue**, not a replacement for what you already do."
- **Escalation if fails**: "ठीक है महाराज जी, अगर future में interest हो तो हमारा number आपके पास है। एक favor — क्या आप किसी और पंडित जी को जानते हैं जो interested हो सकते हैं?" / "If interest changes in the future, please reach out. One small favor — do you know any other pandit who might be interested?"

### O2 — "Commission kyon doon? Devotee sidha aata hai"

> "Why pay commission? The devotee comes to me directly."

- **Root concern**: revenue leakage perception.
- **Hindi rebuttal**: "महाराज जी, **जो भक्त आपके पास सीधा आता है — उस पर हम कोई commission नहीं लेते।** हम सिर्फ **उन bookings पर** revenue share लेते हैं जो हम आपको ला कर देते हैं। यानी हम पूरा cost-of-acquisition उठाते हैं — marketing, app, payment, video proof — और बदले में {REV_SHARE_PCT}% share। ये same जैसा OYO hotels करता है।"
- **English rebuttal**: "For devotees who come to you directly, we charge nothing. We only take a share on bookings **we bring to you** — and in return we cover the entire cost of acquisition: marketing, app, payments, video proof. It's the same model as OYO or Swiggy for restaurants."
- **Escalation if fails**: Offer a 90-day trial with 0% commission to demonstrate net-new bookings. "Pehle 90 din bina commission ke — agar net-new bookings नहीं आईं, तो आप मुफ्त में बाहर निकल सकते हैं।" Log + escalate to founder for final approval.

### O3 — "Aadhaar verification mein time lagega, pandit ji ko pasand nahi"

> "Aadhaar verification will take time, pandits won't like it."

- **Root concern**: KYC friction + privacy worry (especially with elderly pandits).
- **Hindi rebuttal**: "महाराज जी, ये Aadhaar verification सिर्फ **payment security के लिए** है — ताकि पंडित जी का पैसा सीधे उन्हीं के account में जाए, बीच में कोई fraud न हो। पूरी process **2 मिनट** की है — सिर्फ Aadhaar number और एक OTP, बस। हम Aadhaar number कहीं store नहीं करते, सिर्फ verify करते हैं। और जब तक Aadhaar verify नहीं, **listing free है — कोई rush नहीं**।"
- **English rebuttal**: "Aadhaar verification is purely for **payment security** — to ensure the pandit's earnings go directly to his own account, with no fraud in between. The whole process takes **2 minutes** — just Aadhaar number plus one OTP. We don't store the number; we only verify. And listing is free until Aadhaar is verified — no rush."
- **Escalation if fails**: Offer an in-person Aadhaar verification visit — founder or local volunteer comes to the temple, completes verification in 10 minutes. "Hum khud aa kar verify kar denge — pandit ji ko kuch nahi karna padega." Log + assign to local volunteer (or founder if Varanasi).

## 10. Daily Target & Tripwire

**Daily**: 20 calls/day → **5 pandit intros/day** (25% conversion) → 1 verified pandit/day after KYC + first paid puja (50% intro→verified) → **~25 verified pandits/week**.

**Weekly review** (every Sunday evening): count call-log rows, compute conversion. If conversion drops below 20% intros or below 40% intro→verified for 2 consecutive weeks, **revise script v3**.

**Parent-item tripwire** (R-Q1-S-001 / supply onboard-50-pandits-varanasi):

> If `verified_pandits_count_varanasi < 15 by 2026-08-01`, founder escalates for pivot review. Options: (a) hire local Varanasi sales rep, (b) switch to Bengaluru/Mumbai first, (c) revisit incentive (waive commission for first 30 days), (d) drop temple-channel and go direct-to-pandit via Justdial.
