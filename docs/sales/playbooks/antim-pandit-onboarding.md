# Antim Sanskar Pandit Onboarding Playbook

> Spec ref: R-Q4-S-007 (Q4 supply, P1, target=20 verified by month 10 close)
> Scope: Kashi (Varanasi) + Haridwar + Prayagraj ghats. Antim sanskar (last-rites) specialists only.

## Why antim sanskar is its own lane

- Different ritual literacy than puja pandits (Garuda Purana, Pretkalpa, gotra-vansh recital, asthi-visarjan).
- Different distribution: ghat permits + dom-raja network in Kashi, Prayagwal sabha in Prayagraj, ganga sabha in Haridwar.
- Different buyer urgency: bereaved family books within 6-24h, not weeks ahead. Price sensitivity low, trust sensitivity extreme.
- Different revenue model: single-event ticket Rs 8k-25k, plus optional 10/11/13th-day kriya extension Rs 15k-40k.

## Cold-call script (Hindi)

> "Namaskar pandit ji. Main Darshya se baat kar raha hoon. Hum ek devotional super-app chala rahe hain jahan parivar online se antim sanskar pandit ji book kar sakte hain — Kashi, Haridwar, Prayagraj teen jagah ke ghat ke liye. Aap kashi vishwanath / har ki pauri / sangam pe regular antim kriya karwate hain? Hum aap ki profile add karna chahte hain — koi fees nahi, har booking pe aap ko 70 percent payout, baki 30 percent platform commission. Kya 2 minute baat kar sakte hain?"

| Response | Counter |

| Pehle se Sri Mandir / Utsav pe hai | "Wahaan puja booking hota hai, antim sanskar slot nahi hai. Hum specifically antim ke liye dedicated app bana rahe hain — peak demand hota hai aur supply kam hai." |
| Commission zyada hai | "Commission tier-based hai — first 5 bookings pe 20% only, agar verified hain aur ratings 4.5+ hain to 18%. Cash collection bhi allowed hai, hum sirf invoice + 80G handle karte hain." |
| Mujhe app nahi aati | "Koi baat nahi, hum WhatsApp pe booking forward karenge, aap simply confirm karenge. App ki zaroorat nahi shuru mein." |
| Mere paas time nahi | "Theek hai pandit ji. Ek kaam karte hain — kya aap ka WhatsApp number share kar sakte hain? Main detail bhej deta hoon, fursat mein dekh lijiyega." |

## Verification checklist

- [ ] Aadhaar verified (DigiLocker or eKYC)
- [ ] Ghat permit / sabha membership (Kashi Vishwanath Trust / Ganga Sabha Haridwar / Prayagwal Sabha)
- [ ] Yajurveda + Garuda Purana recitation literacy — verify via 2-min audio sample (Garuda Purana adhyay 5 shlok recital)
- [ ] Gotra-vansh + samkalp recital fluency check (in person)
- [ ] 2 family references — call to confirm prior antim kriya conducted in last 12 months
- [ ] Identity proof — Aadhaar + PAN cross-match
- [ ] Bank/UPI for payouts — name match with Aadhaar
- [ ] In-person handshake (founder visit) — non-negotiable for antim lane
