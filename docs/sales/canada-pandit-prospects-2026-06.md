## Why Canada first

- CAN-IN diaspora ~1.8M (StatsCan 2023); densest in GTA + Vancouver Lower Mainland + Calgary + Edmonton + Ottawa + Montreal.
- Hindu temples in Canada: ~250 active per CHF (Council of Hindu Temples of Canada) `[VERIFY-FOUNDER]`.
- Diaspora-trained pandits: typically Vedic-college (Banaras / Kerala / Karnataka) graduates who emigrated post-1990 OR Canadian-born w/ India parampara training.
- USD/CAD pricing: Bhaktisetu NRI tier is INR equivalent 9-10x domestic. CAN puja market: $50-$500 CAD per puja typically (vs $9-100 INR equivalent in India).
- Unlock: NRI customer demand exists; supply is the bottleneck for diaspora-pandit-led pujas.

**Subject**: Bhaktisetu Foundation — NRI puja partnership for {Temple name} pandits (revenue + India diaspora reach)

**Body** (190 words):

```
Hare Krishna / Namaste {Recipient name} ji,

I am {founder name}, founder of Bhaktisetu Foundation
(https://darshya.online) — a verified-temple devotional platform that
serves both India devotees and the global Hindu diaspora.

I am writing because our NRI users in Canada (~1.8M Indian-origin) book
remote pujas in India BUT increasingly ask for pujas performed by
Canada-based pandits who understand both their diaspora context and
sastra rigour. {Temple name} comes up frequently in our user research as
a trusted referral source for Toronto / GTA / Lower Mainland / Calgary /
etc — depending on city.

Specific ask: a 30-minute call to explore whether one or two pandits
associated with {Temple name} would be open to listing on Bhaktisetu's
Canada-NRI puja roster. Indicative terms: pandit retains 70-80% of
devotee payment (CAD $40-$400 per puja), no upfront fee, video proof of
puja completion mandatory, devotee match-making by Bhaktisetu.

Bhaktisetu Foundation holds active 80G + CSR-1 in India; for CAN-side
operations we are exploring CRA-registered partnerships. Happy to share
our deck + Foundation registration on request.

Hare Krishna,
{Founder name}
Founder, Bhaktisetu Foundation
{phone-IN} · {email}
```

**Customisations per recipient**:

- **BAPS temples (Toronto, Vancouver, Edmonton)** — open w/ "Hare Krishna ji" (community uses "Jai Swaminarayan" but Hare Krishna is universally respected); reference Swaminarayan sampradaya specifically.
- **Tamil Saiva temples (Thiru Murugan Montréal, Sri Mahalakshmi Mississauga)** — open w/ "Vanakkam"; mention Tamil sampradaya respect.
- **ISKCON temples** — same Hare Krishna salutation; reference Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada lineage.
- **Federation orgs (Hindu Federation Canada #9, Sanatan Dharm Cultural Society BC #17)** — different ask: "umbrella partnership to refer pandits across N member temples" rather than single-temple pilot.

## Risk + sensitivity notes

1. **Don't blast all 30 in 1 day** — Canada inboxes have stricter spam filters; pace at 3/day across 10 days.
2. **CRA partnership ambiguity** — Bhaktisetu Foundation is India 80G; doesn't translate to Canadian tax-deductible. Be transparent: pilot operates as commercial pandit-listing, NOT as Canadian-tax-deductible donation channel (yet).
3. **BAPS temples**: institutionally cautious about external partnerships. Expect 30-day evaluation cycle minimum.
4. **ISKCON temples**: cross-reference w/ `docs/sales/iskcon-org-map-2026-06.md` — Canada ISKCON falls under separate jurisdiction from India ISKCON, but worth coordinating outreach so we don't approach both in conflicting ways.
5. **Federation orgs**: most leveraged path. One umbrella deal = 20-50 temple intros. But slower decision cycle (3-6 months).
6. **Native language matters**: Tamil Saiva pandits respond better to Tamil-language follow-ups. Founder may need translator help for Tamil/Bengali initial calls.
