## Per-partnership success metrics

- Initial meeting scheduled (1-2 months from outreach)
- 2nd meeting (technical scoping)
- LoI / MoU draft
- Final contract signed
- 90-day post-sign performance review
- Renewal conversation (12 months out)

## Risk + mitigation

- **Risk**: Single partnership > 30% of revenue
- **Mitigation**: Diversification across 10+ accounts; cap any single account
- **Risk**: Founder bandwidth bottleneck
- **Mitigation**: CS-ops + Business Development hire Q3 2027
- **Risk**: Partnership fails operationally
- **Mitigation**: Pilot before full execution; quarterly reviews
