## Founder discipline

- Maximum 7 cold-email investor outreach per week (preserve personalization)
- Decline pitch meetings if no genuine fit (saves your + their time)
- Follow-up within 24-48 hours of any pitch meeting
- Annual rotation of cold list (reach passed angels with new traction)
