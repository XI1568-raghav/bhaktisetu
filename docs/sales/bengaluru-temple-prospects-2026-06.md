## Why Bengaluru first

- Bengaluru is **densest tech-savvy devotee** city in India: 13M population, ~30% Hindu temple-going active (per Karnataka 2021 census proxy), high English+Hindi+Kannada digital fluency, NRI-family base.
- IT salaries + diaspora remittance = highest puja-per-capita spend in any major Indian city.
- Temple density: ~3,500 listed temples in BBMP + Bengaluru Urban district (per Karnataka Endowment Dept registry `[VERIFY-FOUNDER]`).
- Bhaktisetu has 0 Bengaluru temples onboarded. Even 5 verified temples in Bengaluru = ~₹50-100K MRR.

## Outreach approach per tier

### Tier A approach: Foundation-level + heritage angle

Cold email subject: "Bhaktisetu Foundation — partnership for {temple name}'s digital devotee reach"

Body angle: emphasize heritage preservation + 80G donation rails + audited Foundation rather than commercial puja booking. Tier-A trustees value institutional respectability over revenue.

### Tier B approach: Institutional pitch + revenue + tech-fit

Cold email subject: "Bhaktisetu × {institution name} — verified-temple platform partnership for diaspora reach"

Body angle: clear commercial terms (60-80% commission), institutional reach (NRI base), tech-friendly integration. Tier-B management is professionalized and responds to clear ROI math.

### Tier C approach: Founder-direct WhatsApp

Cold WA: "Pranam {trustee/manager name} ji, {founder name} from Bhaktisetu Foundation. Aapke {temple} ko digital puja booking + NRI reach offer karna chahta hoon. 15-min call?"

Tier C is fastest decision cycle (single trustee/manager, no committee). Reach via direct phone numbers obtained from neighborhood walks or community references.

## Risk + sensitivity

1. **Bangalore ISKCON Bureau dispute**: must decide founder-side which camp to engage. Cross-engaging both = damages relationships. Recommend Tier-B path with Bangalore-ISKCON-specific (Madhu Pandit das camp) as SEPARATE pilot, transparent to both sides.
2. **Heritage temples (Tier A)**: trustees often elderly, prefer phone over email. Build 30-day expectation window minimum.
3. **Mutt/matha-adjacent temples** (Sringeri, Ahobila, Pejawar, Raghavendra): decisions sit with parent matha, not local chapter. Slower.
4. **Karnataka Endowment Dept temples**: some are technically state-managed (Bhoga Nandeeshwara, Banashankari Devi possibly). Pilot here requires Endowment Dept clearance — slower, more bureaucratic. Confirm before engaging.
5. **Sampradaya respect**: never call a Sri Vaishnava temple "Smarta" or vice versa. Verify sampradaya per row above before salutation.
6. **No mass outreach**: 30 cold emails in 1 day reads as spam. Pace strictly 3/day over 10 days.
