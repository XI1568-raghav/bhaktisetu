# ISKCON Central Partnership — LoI Signed + 5 Temple Onboard

> **Roadmap item**: R-Q2-S-001 (P0) · **Authored**: 2026-06-13

## Goal

Q2 sign Letter of Intent (LoI) w/ ISKCON Central + onboard 5 ISKCON temples (Bengaluru / Mumbai / Vrindavan / Mayapur / Delhi). Targets 50+ pujas + 10K MAU contribution.

## Approach

### Phase 1: Cold outreach (Month 4)
- Founder direct LinkedIn + email to ISKCON Central administrator (Vrindavan or Mumbai HQ)
- Cold pitch: "Bhaktisetu — verified pandit + video proof platform. Partnership for NRI puja booking commission share."
- 4-touch sequence: LinkedIn → email → WhatsApp follow-up → meeting request

### Phase 2: Pilot meeting (Month 5)
- Founder visits 1 ISKCON temple (Bengaluru) for in-person pitch
- Demo platform on iPad: book live puja → video proof flow
- Pitch: 70-80% revenue share to temple, Bhaktisetu handles tech + payment + customer support
- Ask: 5-temple LoI + onboard 1 pilot temple Q2

### Phase 3: Onboard (Month 6)
- Pilot temple Q2-end: 5 verified pandits + 3 puja SKUs (Govinda darshan / Janmashtami / Vaishnav vivah)
- 1 month performance review w/ booking + revenue + satisfaction
- Roll-forward 4 more temples Q3 if pilot green

## Commercial terms

| Variable | Pilot offer |
|---|---|
| Revenue share | 70% to temple, 20% to pandit, 10% platform |
| Min monthly revenue guarantee | None (pilot) |
| Marketing co-branding | "Powered by Bhaktisetu" footer on temple website (optional) |
| Tech requirements | iPad / phone for video recording, internet (covered by Bhaktisetu) |
| Contract duration | 6 months auto-renew + 30d termination |

## Success metric

- LoI signed by ISKCON Central
- 1 pilot temple onboarded + 5+ pujas booked
- ≥1 video proof completed end-to-end
