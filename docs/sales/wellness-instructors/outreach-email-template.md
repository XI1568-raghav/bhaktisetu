Founder writes from `founder@darshya.online`. English (instructors typically online-fluent).

## Subject lines (A/B test)

- **Subject A**: Teach global students via Darshya — keep 70%
- **Subject B**: Invitation: founding-cohort instructor

Track CTR per subject in CRM `notes` column. Switch to winner after 30 sends.

## Body (≤200 words)

```
Hi {firstName},

I run Darshya, an India-rooted devotional + wellness platform aimed at the global Hindu diaspora and India-domestic seekers. We're putting together a founding cohort of 5 yoga + Ayurveda instructors for Q1 and your work on {sample_referenced — IG post / podcast / studio} stood out.

- No platform fee in Q1 — you keep 100% of class revenue (we charge 20-30% post-Q1, instructor-set price)
- You set your own price and weekly availability
- We handle the tech (booking, payments INR + USD, Jitsi/Zoom room, recordings) so you teach
- Global student funnel — diaspora in US/UK/CA + India domestic

We're vetting carefully — credentials, sample class, language fluency, signed liability MOU. About 10 min of admin from your side total.

Would a 20-min intro call work this or next week? Happy to send a Calendly link.

Looking forward to it.

— {founderFirstName}
Darshya · darshya.online
```

## 3 objection responses

### Objection 1 — "I'm already on YogaGlo / Glo / Insight Timer."

> Totally fine — we're not asking for exclusivity. Lots of our founding cohort teach across 2-3 platforms. The pitch is that Darshya gets you the India-rooted student segment (NRI + domestic) that those US-first platforms don't reach as well. You decide which classes go where.

### Objection 2 — "I don't want to be locked in / give up exclusivity."

> No exclusivity, ever. Our terms-of-service are explicit about that. You can pause, leave, or move classes whenever. The MOU is mostly about safety/liability standards on the platform, not about restricting where you teach.

### Objection 3 — "The tech sounds overwhelming."

> Founder personally onboards each instructor — 1-hour Zoom walkthrough. You don't touch the booking page, the payment links, or the video room setup. You just show up at the class time and teach. We've designed this for instructors who'd rather not think about software.
