# Wellness Instructor Vetting Rubric (R-Q1-S-010)

Six-gate vetting protocol. All 6 must pass before an instructor goes live on Darshya. Gates evaluated in order — fail-fast on any gate skips subsequent checks.

Owner: founder (Q1). Move to ops lead in Q2.

## Gate 1 — Verifiable credential

**Yoga**: RYT-200 or higher with Yoga Alliance verifiable registration number, OR a recognized Indian school certificate (Bihar School of Yoga diploma, Sivananda TTC, Kaivalyadhama, S-VYASA, KPJAYI Authorized Level 1, RIMYI Junior Intermediate I+, Krishnamacharya Yoga Mandiram).

**Ayurveda**: BAMS degree (Bachelor of Ayurvedic Medicine and Surgery) with state council registration number, OR a recognized institute diploma (Kaivalyadhama, Patanjali, Vaidyaratnam P S Varier, Kerala AYUSH-recognized 2+ year programs).

Verification: founder runs CCH (Yoga Alliance) lookup or state-AYUSH council number lookup. Self-uploaded scan kept on file.

## Gate 2 — Public teaching history ≥ 2 years

Evidence required: Instagram/YouTube/studio website showing weekly classes or scheduled sessions for at least 24 months. Acceptable signals: dated class posts, recurring studio listings, podcast guest appearances, published articles.

Hard reject: <12 months. Soft escalate (founder review): 12-24 months with strong testimonials.

## Gate 3 — Sample class video review (15 minutes)

Instructor either sends a 15-minute teaching clip (unlisted YouTube/Drive link) or points to an existing published full class. Founder reviews for: cueing clarity, sequencing competence, safety (contraindications mentioned where relevant), camera/audio quality fit for online delivery.

Score 1-5 on each axis. Pass = 3+ on every axis.

## Gate 4 — Background check declaration signed

Q1 protocol: self-declaration form covering — no prior consumer complaints, no active legal proceedings related to teaching/clinical practice, no Sexual Misconduct allegations. Form linked from intake.

Q2 escalation path: paid 3rd-party background check (₹800-1500 INR per instructor) once volume justifies.

## Gate 5 — Instruction-language fluency (English OR Hindi)

10-minute live audio/video call with founder. Assess: instruction clarity in chosen language, accent neutral enough for pan-India + diaspora audience, ability to explain Sanskrit terms in English where needed.

Both English and Hindi fluency = bonus tier (broader reach). Either alone = pass.

## Gate 6 — Liability MOU signed (waiver template)

Wellness-specific draft MOU: instructor warranties around credential accuracy, agreement on data use, recording/IP terms, indemnity for participant injury claims arising from instructor negligence vs platform.

**Legal review flag**: Q1 ships founder-drafted template (this rubric); formal external legal review required before scaling past 10 instructors. Owner action: queue legal review by end of month 2.

## Scoring summary

All 6 gates pass → APPROVED → onboarding intake form sent.
Any gate fail → REJECTED with specific reason + remediation path (where applicable).
Gates 3 or 5 borderline → REQUEUED for re-evaluation in 30 days.
