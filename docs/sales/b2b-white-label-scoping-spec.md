# B2B White-Label Scoping — 2 Corporate Gifting Pilots

> **Roadmap item**: R-Q2-S-004 (P2) · **Authored**: 2026-06-13

## Goal

Q2 scope B2B white-label corporate gifting pilots. Targets 2 pilot signed contracts Q3.

## Use case

Indian companies want to send "puja gifts" or "yatra packages" to senior employees on Diwali / Akshaya Tritiya / birthdays. Current option: random vendor. Better: branded Bhaktisetu portal.

## Target shortlist (10 companies)

| Tier | Company type | Sample list |
|---|---|---|
| Tier 1 | IT services 10K+ employees | TCS, Infosys, Wipro, HCLTech |
| Tier 2 | Banking 5K+ | HDFC, ICICI, SBI Cards |
| Tier 3 | E-commerce | Flipkart, Snapdeal, Meesho |
| Tier 4 | NRI-centric | Y-Combinator startups w/ NRI founders (gift to employees in India) |

## Pilot offer

- 1-month free white-label portal
- Bhaktisetu-managed puja + yatra catalogue
- Branded co-marketing (company logo on portal)
- Net pricing 20% lower than direct retail (corporate discount)
- 30-day refund period

## Sales cycle

- Founder cold outreach (LinkedIn + email)
- Tier-1 likely requires CIO + HR procurement approval (3-month cycle)
- Tier-3/4 likely faster (1-month cycle)
- Lower-priority Q2 — focus on 2-3 high-prob signups

## Revenue model

- Setup fee: ₹50K one-time per company
- Per-transaction fee: 10% on gross gift value
- Min annual commit: ₹2L
