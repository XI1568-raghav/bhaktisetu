## 3. Temple presidents (TP) autonomy

**Rule of thumb** (validated against [ISKCON Bylaws excerpts and Bureau practice — `[UNVERIFIED for current version, cite Bureau 2025 bylaws when sourced]`]):

| Deal type | Authority |
|---|---|
| Single-temple booking pilot (Bhaktisetu lists ONE temple, ONE puja type, revenue ≤₹10K/mo) | TP can sign solo |
| Single-temple branded co-marketing (logo on Bhaktisetu temple page) | TP signs but should inform regional sec |
| Multi-temple bundle (2-5 ISKCON temples) | Regional sec signoff required |
| Brand-level central deal ("Bhaktisetu × ISKCON India") | GBC + Bureau both must sign |
| Foundation-level fundraising partnership (e.g. matching donations to ISKCON Food for Life) | GBC + Bureau + Foundation board |

**Implication**: pilot WITHOUT central deal is feasible — start with 1-2 TPs willing to experiment, prove model, then escalate to multi-temple via regional sec.

## 4. ISKCON Bureau (India)

The Bureau is the **separate India-specific legal/operational body** that oversees ISKCON registered entities in India (typically Section 8 companies / trusts). [Source: iskconbureau.com `[VERIFY URL alive]`].

**Why Bureau matters separately from GBC**:
- GBC = doctrinal authority (worldwide).
- Bureau = legal/operational authority (India-specific).
- A central commercial deal needs BOTH signoffs to be enforceable in Indian courts.
- Bureau handles all FCRA, 80G, Section 8 compliance — relevant for our 80G donation rails partnership.

**Outreach path for Bureau**: official `secretary@iskconbureau.com` `[VERIFY]` channel. Slower than direct TP but more durable.

## 8. Risk section — political factions + litigation

**MUST READ before any outreach** — getting these wrong damages relationships permanently.

### 8.1 Bangalore-ISKCON vs ISKCON Bureau (Madhu Pandit das case)

Long-running dispute (~25 years) between ISKCON Bangalore (Hare Krishna Hill) under Madhu Pandit das and the central ISKCON Bureau over temple ownership + management authority. **Status `[VERIFY-FOUNDER current legal status — Supreme Court rulings in 2020+ may have closed]`**.

**Implication for Bhaktisetu**:
- Bangalore ISKCON operates as a SEPARATE legal entity from ISKCON India in many practical ways.
- Approaching Madhu Pandit das directly does NOT count as approaching "ISKCON" via the Bureau path.
- Don't simultaneously approach both Bangalore-MPd-camp AND Bureau without disclosing — this reads as playing factions.
- Safer for v1: pick ONE camp explicitly, transparent about scope ("we're piloting with Bangalore ISKCON specifically").

### 8.2 Generational disciple-line dynamics

ISKCON has several disciple lines (Prabhupada direct → 11 zonal acaryas → next-gen). Some leaders are more aligned with reforming GBC; others with traditional camps. A naïve email blast to all 35 GBC members triggers political resistance.

**Implication**: founder must pick 2-3 names BASED ON known alignment, not "blast all GBC".

### 8.3 Recent splits / departures `[VERIFY-FOUNDER]`

Periodic high-profile departures of senior leaders from ISKCON (e.g. some 1990s zonal acarya departures). Reference any individual carefully — they may no longer be ISKCON-affiliated.

### 8.4 Bhakti Marga / similar adjacent orgs

DO NOT confuse ISKCON with Bhakti Marga (different lineage, Paramahamsa Vishwananda) or with non-Gaudiya Vaishnava bodies. Outreach must reference Prabhupada + Gaudiya Vaishnava tradition correctly.
