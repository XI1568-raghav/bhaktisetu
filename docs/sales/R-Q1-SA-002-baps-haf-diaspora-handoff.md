## Why this matters

BAPS (Bochasanwasi Akshar Purushottam Swaminarayan Sanstha) operates the largest organized Hindu temple network in North America (100+ mandirs across US/UK/Canada) with a deeply engaged Gujarati-NRI diaspora that maps 1:1 to Darshya's NRI ICP (Anand persona — 41, SF, USD checkout, gift puja to parents in India).

A single warm intro into BAPS senior leadership would unlock:
- Co-marketing access to ~250K active NRI devotee households across BAPS satsang groups
- Pilot deployment of Darshya's live darshan + 80G receipt + gift-puja flows during major utsavs (Janmashtami, Diwali, Annakut)
- Trust amplifier — BAPS endorsement is the single highest-credibility signal in the US Hindu diaspora
- Estimated TAM unlock: 50K-150K NRI active users within 12 months of partnership

Cold outreach to BAPS leadership has near-zero response rate (institutional gate). The Hindu American Foundation (HAF), as the leading US-based Hindu civic advocacy org, has standing convening relationships with BAPS and routinely warm-intros vetted Hindu-tech founders.

## Plan (3-step warm-intro funnel)

### Step 1 — HAF inbound (week 1)

Founder reaches out to HAF leadership via these channels:
- **Suhag Shukla** (Co-founder + Executive Director, HAF) — primary anchor; thought leader on Hindu civic engagement
- **Samir Kalra, Esq** (Managing Director, HAF) — policy + partnerships portfolio
- **Mat McDermott** (Senior Director of Communications, HAF) — diaspora comms

Channel: LinkedIn DM + HAF general contact form (https://www.hinduamerican.org/contact). Note: replace contact details below with currently-verified info from hinduamerican.org before sending — institutional contacts rotate.

### Step 2 — HAF → BAPS warm intro (week 2-3)

After establishing rapport with HAF (donate a small founder-level contribution if appropriate, attend a virtual HAF event), founder asks for warm intro to BAPS NA leadership:
- **Yogi Trivedi, PhD** (Columbia University, BAPS scholar-volunteer, public-facing BAPS spokesperson) — academic anchor
- **BAPS Public Affairs Office NA** — institutional gate, lower-priority than scholar-volunteers
- **Local BAPS mandir trustees** (Robbinsville NJ, Chino Hills CA, Atlanta GA — three largest US mandirs) — operational decision-makers for pilot

### Step 3 — BAPS pilot proposal (week 4-6)

Pitch a low-commitment pilot for Janmashtami 2026 (August) or Diwali 2026 (October):
- Free live darshan stream embed on BAPS satsang WhatsApp groups (zero infra ask from BAPS)
- Co-branded 80G receipt flow for NRI devotee donations to BAPS-affiliated India seva programs (Akshardham temple maintenance, BAPS Charities flood relief, etc)
- Founder personally manages first 100 transactions to guarantee zero failure
