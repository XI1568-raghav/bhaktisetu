## Goal

Sign 5-temple commercial agreement w/ BAPS Swaminarayan Sansthan covering 5 BAPS temples for puja booking + adopt-a-temple subscription.

## BAPS context (verify before outreach)

- BAPS = Bochasanwasi Akshar Purushottam Swaminarayan Sanstha
- Centralised institutional structure (unlike ISKCON's federation+bureau split)
- Decisions sit with Sant Mandal + Trust at HQ Akshardham Gandhinagar
- Famously professional + well-organized (Akshardham temples globally are showcase examples)
- Bhaktisetu fit: structured, scalable, brand-safe partnership
- 5 priority temples: Akshardham Delhi, Akshardham Gandhinagar, Swaminarayan Mandir Mumbai (Dadar), Swaminarayan Toronto, Swaminarayan Houston (already in Canada pandit research)

## Outreach approach

### Channel: founder-direct via BAPS public-affairs office

BAPS doesn't do unsolicited partnership well. Best path:
1. Foundation-letterhead written letter to BAPS Public Affairs (Ahmedabad HQ)
2. Followed by phone call 7 days post-letter
3. In-person meeting at Akshardham Gandhinagar if positive

### LOI template

Use `docs/sales/iskcon-loi-template-v1.md` as base. BAPS-specific tweaks:
- Reference Sant Mandal authority structure
- Emphasize centralized partnership (not per-temple)
- Higher commercial terms (BAPS more sophisticated negotiator) — propose 75% to BAPS (vs 60-80% range)
- Explicit India + diaspora (US/UK/CA) clause
- 12-month pilot vs 90-day (BAPS slower decision cycle)
