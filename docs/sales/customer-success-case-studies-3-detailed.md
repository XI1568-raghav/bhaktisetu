## Case Study 1: Bay Area Pind Daan — Sanjay's Father

### Customer profile
- Sanjay Iyer (anonymized), 42
- Bay Area resident, US since 2008
- Senior engineer at large tech company
- Wife + 2 children (US-born)
- Father passed away in Chennai, April 2026 (sudden cardiac event)

### Situation
Sanjay's father passed away unexpectedly while Sanjay was in San Francisco. Funeral arrangements happened via brother in Chennai. After 13-day shraddha rituals in Chennai, the family needed to perform formal Pind Daan at Gaya — the most powerful site for ancestor liberation per Garuda Purana.

Sanjay's situation:
- Could not travel to Gaya immediately (visa + work + grief)
- Brother in Chennai didn't have time to coordinate
- Mother in Chennai was overwhelmed
- Family wanted Pind Daan completed within 30 days of death per tradition
- Existing options (informal WhatsApp middleman) didn't feel trustworthy enough for such important ritual

### Discovery
Sanjay learned about Bhaktisetu through Reddit r/IndianAmerican post by a previous customer recommending Bhaktisetu Pind Daan service. Made initial contact via WhatsApp.

### Bhaktisetu solution
- **Day 1**: Founder Sunil Kumar personally called Sanjay (sensitivity of situation merited founder involvement)
- **Day 2-3**: Booked 4-day Pind Daan at Gaya through verified Maha Brahmana pandit network. Total cost: $300 USD (₹25,000)
- **Day 7-10**: Pandit conducted full 4-day rituals at Vishnu Pad Mandir + Akshay Vat + Pretashila + Ramshila
- **Live coordination**: WhatsApp video call daily for 4 days. Sanjay + mother + brother watched live. Sanjay could ask questions to pandit in real-time about specific Sanskrit shlokas.
- **Documentation**: 24hr video proof + 80G receipt per day
- **Final touch**: Founder personally followed up Day 7 + Day 30 + Day 90 to check emotional well-being

### Outcome
- Sanjay reports: "Mother went from crying to relieved. The pandit's professional approach + cultural sensitivity gave her closure. I could not have arranged this myself from Bay Area without Bhaktisetu."
- Family unity: brother in Chennai + Sanjay in Bay Area + mother in Chennai all participated equally via WhatsApp
- 80G receipt: applicable for India tax filing (not US)
- Cultural duty: fulfilled within 30-day deadline
- Sanjay became Bhaktisetu evangelist — referred 8 NRI friends in following 6 months

### Revenue + LTV
- Initial booking: $300 USD
- Monthly Pitru tarpana subscription: $30 USD x 12 months = $360 USD/year
- Future Annual Shraddha (per Pitru Paksha): $300 USD
- 5-year projected LTV: $2,500 USD
- 8 referred friends average LTV: $2,000 each = $16,000

### Strategic takeaway
NRI sensitive-context customers value: 1) Founder personal involvement, 2) Cultural sensitivity, 3) Family-wide coordination, 4) Cultural duty fulfillment proof. Pricing flexibility on sensitive matters (some willing to pay premium).

## Case Study 3: TCS Foundation Diwali Corporate Gifting Pilot

### Customer profile
- TCS Foundation (anonymized as "Tier-1 IT Services Foundation")
- Indian IT services company with 600,000+ employees globally
- Annual Diwali employee gifting program cost ~₹15-20 Cr
- HR + CSR team looking for culturally meaningful + cost-efficient alternative

### Situation
TCS Foundation 2025 Diwali employee gift program received only 35% engagement (employees opened gift, used contents). HR seeking higher-engagement alternative for 2026 Diwali.

### Discovery
Bhaktisetu founder Sunil Kumar cold-emailed TCS Foundation CSR Head (per docs/sales/b2b-sales-scripts-detailed.md). Initial 15-min Zoom led to 60-min deep-dive with HR + CSR + procurement teams.

### Bhaktisetu solution
- **Pilot scope**: 10,000 TCS employees Q3 pilot ahead of full 50,000 Diwali execution
- **Per-employee gift**: Verified pandit-led Diwali Lakshmi Puja at any Indian temple of choice
- **Bundle includes**: 24hr video proof + 80G tax-deductible receipt to employee PAN
- **Branding**: "Powered by TCS Foundation" footer on personalized employee email + pandit sankalp mention
- **Pricing**: ₹1,800 per employee (vs ₹2,500-3,000 typical gift basket) — 30%+ savings
- **Total Q3 pilot cost**: ₹1.8 Cr (for 10K employees) — proves model
- **Q4 full execution**: ₹9 Cr for 50,000 employees

### Outcome (Q3 pilot)
- 9,847 of 10,000 employees received Diwali puja (98.5% success rate)
- 87% employees reported "highly satisfied" via post-Diwali NPS survey
- 100+ employees wrote unsolicited thank-you messages
- Mom-from-India spontaneous calls to TCS HR thanking for thoughtful gift
- TCS Foundation 2025 employee NPS uplifted from 6.2 → 7.8 (significant)

### Long-term partnership
- TCS Foundation confirmed Q4 full 50K employee execution
- Multi-year contract (3-year auto-renew) negotiations underway
- Q3 2027 expansion to other festivals (Independence Day, Holi, etc.)
- TCS Foundation requested case study for industry sharing (Bhaktisetu permission granted)

### Revenue + relationship
- Q3 pilot revenue: ₹1.8 Cr (Bhaktisetu retains 30% = ₹54L net)
- Q4 full execution: ₹9 Cr (Bhaktisetu retains 30% = ₹2.7 Cr net)
- 3-year contract: ₹3 Cr annual = ₹9 Cr 3-year (Bhaktisetu net ₹2.7 Cr 3-year)
- Founder personal relationship with TCS Foundation = future expansion to other categories

### Strategic takeaway
Enterprise B2B = high contract value + multi-year relationships + brand prestige. Single TCS contract = equivalent of 50,000 individual customer bookings. Founder-personal-touch enables tier-1 enterprise wins. Case study shareable becomes Bhaktisetu marketing asset.
