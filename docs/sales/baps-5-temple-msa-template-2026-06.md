RECITALS

A. Bhaktisetu Foundation ("Bhaktisetu") is a Section 8 non-profit
   (CIN: {CIN}, 80G certified, CSR-1 registered) operating
   darshya.online — a verified-temple devotee services platform
   with live darshan, video-proof puja booking, and 80G-compliant
   donation infrastructure.

B. Bochasanwasi Akshar Purushottam Swaminarayan Sanstha ("BAPS")
   is the central religious institution administering the BAPS
   network of mandirs and Akshardham complexes globally.

C. The Parties intend to enter into a structured 12-month commercial
   pilot enabling 5 BAPS federated temples (the "Pilot Temples")
   to serve devotees via Bhaktisetu's booking + donation
   infrastructure under the commercial terms defined herein.

PILOT TEMPLES (5)

1. Swaminarayan Akshardham, New Delhi (India)
2. Swaminarayan Akshardham, Gandhinagar (India)
3. BAPS Shri Swaminarayan Mandir, Toronto (Canada)
4. BAPS Shri Swaminarayan Mandir, Neasden, London (United Kingdom)
5. BAPS Shri Swaminarayan Mandir, Houston (United States)

1. SCOPE OF SERVICES

   1.1  Bhaktisetu shall provide each Pilot Temple with:
        (a) Verified temple listing on darshya.online with photos,
            timings, and seva catalogue.
        (b) Booking infrastructure for ≥3 puja SKUs per temple
            (minimum) plus adopt-a-temple monthly subscription.
        (c) Live darshan streaming integration (HLS) where temple
            provides camera feed.
        (d) 80G receipt issuance against Bhaktisetu Foundation
            80G certificate (Bhaktisetu retains regulatory donee).
        (e) Video-proof delivery to devotee within 24h of seva
            performance (mandatory per Bhaktisetu business rule).
        (f) Devotee-side support in 6 Indian languages + English
            for diaspora temples.
        (g) NRI dual-currency checkout (USD/INR/GBP/CAD) at the
            published NRI multiplier per Bhaktisetu pricing.

   1.2  Each Pilot Temple shall:
        (a) Designate a single Temple Coordinator authorised to
            confirm bookings, assign pandits, and upload video
            proof within 24h SLA.
        (b) Maintain a minimum roster of 3 BAPS-sanctioned pandits
            available for booked sevas.
        (c) Comply with Bhaktisetu video-proof SLA (capture, upload,
            metadata tagging within 24h of seva completion).
        (d) Participate in monthly operations sync calls.

2. COMMERCIAL TERMS

   2.1  Platform Commission. For each booking confirmed and fulfilled
        via the Bhaktisetu platform, Bhaktisetu retains a Platform
        Commission of [TWENTY-FIVE PERCENT (25%)] of Gross Booking Value
        (GBV), and BAPS / the relevant Pilot Temple receives the
        remaining SEVENTY-FIVE PERCENT (75%) net of payment-gateway
        charges.

        Commission range permitted under Bhaktisetu pricing rule:
        20%–40%. Pilot rate set at 25% reflecting BAPS scale +
        institutional partnership. Reviewable at month 6 and at
        renewal.

   2.2  Adopt-a-Temple Subscriptions. Recurring monthly devotee
        subscriptions attributable to a Pilot Temple are split on
        the same 25/75 basis after payment-gateway charges.

   2.3  80G Donations. Pure donations (no seva consideration) flow
        through Bhaktisetu Foundation's 80G receipt with 100% net of
        gateway charges remitted to the Pilot Temple within T+30 days,
        subject to PAN + donor-detail capture per Income Tax Act §80G.

   2.4  Payouts. Bi-weekly remittance to BAPS-designated bank account
        per temple, with a per-temple settlement statement including
        booking-ID, devotee initials, seva, GBV, commission, gateway,
        and net.

   2.5  GST. Both Parties acknowledge their respective GST
        registration and compliance obligations. Bhaktisetu raises
        platform-commission tax invoices to BAPS / each Pilot Temple
        monthly.

3. VIDEO-PROOF SLA (Bhaktisetu business rule #1, immutable)

   3.1  Every fulfilled seva booking MUST be accompanied by a
        video-proof file uploaded by the Temple Coordinator within
        24 hours of seva completion.

   3.2  Failure to deliver video-proof within 72 hours triggers
        automatic devotee refund per Bhaktisetu refund policy and
        deducts the refund from the next BAPS settlement.

   3.3  Repeated SLA breaches (>10% of bookings in a calendar month)
        may, at Bhaktisetu's discretion, suspend new bookings for
        the offending Pilot Temple until process review.

4. BRAND + CONTENT

   4.1  BAPS retains all rights to BAPS name, marks, devotional
        content, and temple imagery. Bhaktisetu's right to display
        the same on darshya.online is a limited, non-exclusive,
        revocable licence for the duration of this MSA.

   4.2  Bhaktisetu may use anonymised devotee-volume statistics
        for marketing without prior approval. Use of BAPS name in
        case studies or press releases requires prior written
        approval of BAPS Public Affairs.

5. DATA + DPDP

   5.1  Devotee personal data is processed jointly by both Parties
        as Data Fiduciaries under the Digital Personal Data
        Protection Act, 2023. A separate Data Processing Annex
        forms part of this MSA.

   5.2  BAPS shall not use Bhaktisetu-sourced devotee data for any
        purpose outside this MSA without explicit, granular consent
        captured via Bhaktisetu's consent log.

6. TERM + TERMINATION

   6.1  Pilot Term: TWELVE (12) months from the Effective Date.

   6.2  Either Party may terminate any individual Pilot Temple from
        the Pilot with 30 days' written notice, without prejudice
        to the remaining 4 temples.

   6.3  Either Party may terminate the MSA for material breach
        unremedied within 30 days of written notice.

   6.4  Bhaktisetu video-proof SLA breach exceeding 25% in any
        rolling 30-day window per Pilot Temple constitutes
        material breach by BAPS as to that temple.

   6.5  On termination, accrued payouts shall be settled within
        45 days and devotee data shall be deleted or returned per
        the Data Processing Annex.

7. SUCCESS METRICS (informal — not termination triggers)

   - First 30 days post go-live: ≥ ₹2,00,000 GMV across 5 temples.
   - Month 6: ≥ ₹10,00,000 GMV across 5 temples.
   - Month 6 video-proof SLA compliance: ≥ 95%.
   - Devotee CSAT (Bhaktisetu in-app survey): ≥ 4.4 / 5.

   Failure to meet success metrics initiates a joint review;
   neither Party is bound to renew on existing terms.

8. RENEWAL

   6 weeks before pilot end, the Parties shall in good faith
   discuss renewal on production commercial terms, which may
   include a revised commission rate within the 20–40% policy
   range, expansion to additional BAPS temples, and adoption of
   BAPS-bespoke product features.

9. GOVERNING LAW + DISPUTES

## Sales drafting notes (internal — not part of signed MSA)

### Reused from ISKCON LOI (R-Q2-SA-001)

- Section 8 entity preamble
- 80G + CSR-1 regulatory frame
- Video-proof SLA clause
- Bi-weekly settlement cadence
- Devotee data joint-fiduciary frame
- Arbitration seat + governing law

### BAPS-specific additions (new vs ISKCON)

- 5-temple Pilot enumeration (vs ISKCON federation scope)
- 25% commission default (vs ISKCON-side 20–40% range left open)
- Multi-country jurisdiction acknowledgment (Canada/UK/US BAPS centres)
- Per-temple termination clause (vs single-entity termination)
- Adopt-a-Temple subscription SKU explicit
- BAPS Public Affairs approval for press

### Commission rationale (CFO sign-off required)

- BAPS scale + brand value justifies tighter rate than rural-temple range.
- 25% leaves headroom to negotiate up to 30% if BAPS resists.
- ISKCON anchor pilot ran at 30% — BAPS offered tighter as
  social-proof customer.
