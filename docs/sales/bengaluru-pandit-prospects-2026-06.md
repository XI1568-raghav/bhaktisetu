## Why Bengaluru pandits separately from temples

- Temple-affiliated pandits: bundled w/ temple onboarding via S-005
- **Independent / freelance pandits**: NOT bundled. Need own outreach. ~70% of Bengaluru's puja-officiating capacity is INDEPENDENT (priest-at-home pujas, vehicle pujas, griha-pravesh, mundan, etc).
- This kit targets the freelance/independent pandit segment.

## Onboarding pipeline (per pandit)

```markdown
- [ ] Initial call — 15 min (founder confirms Vedic knowledge depth via 3 prompted questions per sampradaya)
- [ ] KYC docs — Aadhaar + PAN + 2 video proofs of completed pujas
- [ ] Reference check — 1 community elder OR temple trustee
- [ ] Bhaktisetu trial — first 2 bookings under founder direct observation
- [ ] Onboard — live listing on platform w/ profile, photo (consent), service catalog
- [ ] Payout setup — UPI/IFSC; 70-80% per puja, monthly disbursement batch
```

## Economics

Per onboarded BLR pandit:
- 5-10 bookings/month × ₹501 avg × 70% to pandit = ₹1.7K-3.5K monthly to pandit
- Bhaktisetu 30% × ~₹2.5K avg booking volume = ₹750-1.5K MRR per pandit
- **30 pandits × ₹1K avg = ₹30K MRR direct** (+ cross-sell to adopt-a-temple, NRI 9x markup, etc.)

## Risk + sensitivity

1. **Sampradaya misclassification = instant lost relationship**. Verify before WA.
2. **Tamil pandits may prefer Tamil over Hindi**. Have Tamil follow-up ready (cross-ref macros-v1-ta.md).
3. **Tier D Kannada community**: prefer in-person initial meet over WhatsApp.
4. **Madhva mutt-chapter pandits**: decisions sit with mutt swami, not local pandit. Slower (30-day cycle).
5. **Trial puja quality control**: 2 trial bookings under founder observation BEFORE listing. Catches knowledge gaps before customer-facing.
