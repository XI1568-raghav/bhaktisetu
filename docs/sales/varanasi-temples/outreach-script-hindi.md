# Outreach script — Hindi (phone + in-person)

Use respectful temple register. Pandit-ji / Trust-ji / Mahant-ji per role. Speak slowly. Mirror priest's tone (Sanskrit-leaning vs Hindi-conversational).

## Opening (greeting)

> "Pranam Pandit-ji / Trust-ji. Mera naam {founder_name} hai. Main Darshya naam ki ek devotional app se baat kar raha hoon. Aapke mandir ke baare mein humne kaafi suna hai — {specific_compliment_per_temple}. Aapse 2 minute baat kar sakta hoon?"

Translation note: open with name + organization, then a specific, researched compliment per temple (Sankat Mochan = Mahant Mishra ji's tech-friendly bhakti work; Kashi Vishwanath = Lahori Tola morning aarti; Tulsi Manas = marble Ramcharitmanas inscription). Generic praise reads as cold.

## 30-second value pitch

> "Darshya ek aisa platform hai jo aapke mandir ke darshan aur pooja ko duniya bhar ke shraddhalu tak pahuncha sakta hai — bina kisi upfront cost ke. Aapko 3 cheez milti hain:
>
> 1. **Vishwabhar prachar** — humare paas Bharat, America, UK aur Australia mein 50,000+ devotee hain jo NRI hain aur apne mool mandir se darshan chahte hain.
> 2. **80G receipt automation** — jo bhi donation hoga, software apne aap valid 80G receipt PAN ke saath bhej dega — donor ke liye aur trust ke compliance ke liye dono.
> 3. **Zero upfront** — koi setup fee nahi, koi monthly nahi. Hum sirf successful booking par platform fee lete hain (20-40%).
>
> Ek baar 15 minute baith ke milein, hum aapko poori system dikha dein."

## Objection handlers (3 most common)

### "Hum already livestream karte hain (YouTube/Facebook)"

> "Bilkul sahi baat. Aapke YouTube se sirf casual viewership hoti hai — booking aur donation nahi. Darshya us viewership ko paying devotee mein convert karta hai: voh aapke mandir ki specific pooja book kar sakte hain, donation de sakte hain, 80G receipt paa sakte hain. Aapka YouTube live rahe — Darshya uske oopar revenue layer hai. Koi conflict nahi."

### "Revenue share kam hai" / "Commission jyada hai"

> "Trust-ji, samajh sakta hoon. Lekin do baat dhyaan dein. Pehla — humara 20-40% commission tab lagta hai jab booking actually complete ho. Aapko zero risk hai. Doosra — humare paas pricing tier hai based on volume: agar aap month ke 50+ booking dete hain, commission 25% tak aa jata hai. Aur jo donor 80G receipt manga raha hai, voh aapke donation pool ko 3-4x grow karta hai. Net mein trust ko zyada milta hai, kam nahi."

### "Trust board approval lena padega"

> "Bilkul, regulated process hota hai. Hum aapko ek 2-page MOU draft bhej dete hain jismein revenue split, 80G flow, aur exit clause clear likha hota hai. Aap board meeting mein review kara lijiye. Hum pilot ke liye sirf 3 mahine ka trial bhi de sakte hain — bina lock-in. Agar work na kare, koi rakhne ki zaroorat nahi. Iss tarah board ka decision easy ho jata hai."

## Close (call to action)

> "Pandit-ji, kya hum is hafte ya agle hafte 15 minute aapse aapke mandir mein hi mil sakte hain? Main {founder_city} se aaunga. Aap meri convenience nahi — aap apna time bataaiye. Subah ke aarti ke baad ya shaam ke 5-6 baje, jo bhi aapke liye theek ho."

Goal: in-person 15-min meeting at temple premises. Phone call rarely converts to MOU; physical presence + temple darshan first builds trust.

## Follow-up if priest defers

If priest says "abhi busy hoon, baad mein baat karenge":

> "Bilkul. Main aapko WhatsApp par humara 1-pager bhejta hoon. Aap padh lijiye, aur jab convenient ho — meri taraf se koi pressure nahi — to mujhe WhatsApp par bata dijiye. Mera number {founder_phone}. Aapka aashirvad rahe."

Then send WhatsApp template `whatsapp-templates.md` cold-first-touch.

## DO NOT say

- Don't mention competitor names (Sri Mandir, Utsav, VAMA) unprompted — risks negative anchor.
- Don't quote exact revenue numbers from other temples — confidential, breaks trust.
- Don't promise specific donation volume — "0 to 50 lakh per month" is honest range based on temple tier; don't lock to a number.
- Don't push for same-day signing — Indian trust governance is slow on purpose; rushing flags as scam.
