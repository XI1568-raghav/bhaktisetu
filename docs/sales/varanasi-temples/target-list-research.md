## Tier 1 (8 priority targets — famous temples with existing online presence)

### VNS-001 Kashi Vishwanath Dharma Karya
- **Source**: shrikashivishwanath.org (official), Government of UP Dharma Karya Vibhag
- **Governance**: government-administered through Shri Kashi Vishwanath Special Area Development Board (constituted 1983). Partnership needs Mandir Prashasan + Divisional Commissioner approval — slower but most prestigious anchor.
- **Tech footprint**: official site + YouTube live aarti (3 slots: Mangal/Bhog/Sandhya). NO existing booking platform.
- **Contact discovery**: phone via 0542-2392629 (general); priest network introduction needed for darshan-coordinator.
- **Risk**: complex governance + multiple stakeholders. May take 6-9 months from first call to MOU.
- **Opportunity**: anchor partner — if Kashi signs, all 24 others sign within Q1.

### VNS-002 Sankat Mochan Hanuman Mandir
- **Source**: sankatmochanvaranasi.org, Wikipedia, Mahant Prof Vishwambhar Nath Mishra public profile (BHU electrical engg professor)
- **Governance**: Sankat Mochan Foundation; Mishra family hereditary mahant tradition.
- **Tech footprint**: tech-friendly mahant (electrical engg PhD, runs Ganga clean-up tech initiative). Most likely Tier-1 early signer.
- **Contact discovery**: foundation office email on website; physical office at temple.
- **Opportunity**: Mishra ji is a natural champion. Get this signed first; reference for Kashi.

### VNS-003 Durga Kund Mandir
- **Source**: Google Maps, Varanasi tourism portal, Wikipedia
- **Governance**: 18th-century trust founded by Bengali Maharani; Bengali community board.
- **Tech footprint**: minimal — no website, no streaming.
- **Contact discovery**: field visit + ask for pradhan pujari.
- **Risk**: limited camera setup capacity — Gate 6 may fail.

### VNS-004 Tulsi Manas Mandir
- **Source**: Wikipedia, Varanasi tourism, vrindavanchandrodaya.org reference (similar marble-engraving Ramayan temple)
- **Governance**: private trust; Birla family historic association.
- **Tech footprint**: tourist-friendly; some YouTube presence via tour guides.
- **Contact discovery**: trust office at temple; Birla family corporate liaison option.
- **Opportunity**: marble Ramcharitmanas inscription is unique global draw.

### VNS-005 New Vishwanath Temple BHU
- **Source**: bhu.ac.in, Wikipedia
- **Governance**: Banaras Hindu University trust; founded by Madan Mohan Malaviya 1916.
- **Tech footprint**: university-administered; institutional partnership angle.
- **Contact discovery**: BHU PRO office, formal university process.
- **Opportunity**: institutional brand association lifts Darshya credibility.

### VNS-006 Annapurna Devi Mandir
- **Source**: field visit + Varanasi temple registry
- **Governance**: trust adjacent to Kashi Vishwanath; shared pilgrim flow.
- **Tech footprint**: no website. Recently restored Annapurna idol returned from Canada (2021) — high media profile.
- **Contact discovery**: field visit via Vishwanath Gali; ask Vishwanath admin for intro.

### VNS-007 Kal Bhairav Mandir
- **Source**: field visit, Wikipedia
- **Governance**: Kal Bhairav Trust; Kotwal of Kashi traditional role.
- **Tech footprint**: zero digital presence.
- **Opportunity**: daily aarti slot ideal for live darshan pilot. Bhairav devotees underserved by mainstream platforms.

### VNS-008 Mrityunjaya Mahadev Mandir
- **Source**: phone directory + field visit
- **Governance**: private trust; ancient site.
- **Tech footprint**: zero.
- **Opportunity**: Mahamrityunjaya jaap is high-ticket pooja booking (₹5,000–25,000 per slot).

## Research-method discipline (do not violate)

- Never fabricate phone numbers. Use `TBD-discover` if not findable from public source.
- Email addresses: only list verified org-level emails (sankatmochanvaranasi.org office email is verified; never invent `pandit@temple-name.com`).
- Research source URL or explicit method MUST be cited per row.
- When field visit is needed, mark `research_source: field visit needed` rather than leaving blank.
- Cross-faith inclusion (e.g. Sarnath Buddhist): flag clearly. Don't pitch without confirming the temple's leadership accepts cross-faith platform listing.
