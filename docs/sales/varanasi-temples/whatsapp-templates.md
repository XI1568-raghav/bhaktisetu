# WhatsApp templates — Varanasi temple outreach

3 variants. Per WhatsApp Business spam rules: no links in cold first-touch, no images, keep ≤300 chars, no all-caps.

## 1. Cold first-touch (Day 0 or Day 3 post-email)

```
Pranam Pandit-ji 🙏

Mera naam {founder_name} hai, Darshya app se. Aapke {temple_name} ke baare mein humne suna — aapke mandir ke devotees ko global reach + 80G receipt automation offer kar sakte hain, bina kisi upfront cost ke.

Kya 15 min ki call ya in-person milne ka time mil sakta hai is hafte?

Aapka aashirvad rahe.
{founder_name}
```

Character count: ~275. Within WA 1024 limit, well within 300-char cold rule.

## 2. Follow-up (Day 3 after no response)

```
Pranam Pandit-ji 🙏

Pichli baar message bheja tha Darshya ki taraf se. Samajh sakta hoon ki aap kaafi vyast hain.

Sirf 2 cheez: (1) hum 50,000+ NRI devotees ke saath kaam karte hain (2) 80G receipt apne aap generate hota hai aapke trust ke liye.

Agar ek 5-min phone call possible ho, mujhe convenient time bata dijiye. Subah ke aarti ke baad ya shaam ke 6 baje?

{founder_name}, +91-{founder_phone}
```

Character count: ~340. Slightly over cold limit — acceptable for follow-up (recipient has prior context).

## 3. Post-meeting recap

```
Pranam Pandit-ji 🙏

Aaj ke meeting ke liye dhanyavad. Aapne jo cheez share kee voh sab note kar liya hai.

Next steps:
1. Hum aapko 2-page MOU draft kal subah bhej denge
2. Aap board review kara lijiye (2-3 hafta lagega usually)
3. Approval ke baad pilot 3 mahine ka — bina lock-in

Koi bhi sawaal ho, please call ya WhatsApp karein.

Aashirvad rahe.
{founder_name}
```

Character count: ~310.

## Sending rules

- Send only between 09:00 IST and 19:00 IST.
- Never send on major festival days (Mahashivratri / Janmashtami / Diwali / Holi) — disrespectful + low response.
- Sunday OK in Varanasi (temple operates 7 days).
- After 2 follow-ups without reply, move to status `dropped` and revisit in Q2.
- Keep WA spam rate <0.1% (≤1 complaint per 1000 sends) to keep WA Business account active.

## Personalization tokens

Always replace ALL braces — `{founder_name}`, `{temple_name}`, `{founder_phone}`. Sending a templated message with a literal `{...}` token is the fastest path to lose credibility.
