## Why Varanasi (when unlocked)

- Highest pandit-density city in India (Kashi tradition center)
- ~5000+ registered pandits across community matha + akhara network
- Strong NRI pilgrim base (US/UK/CA diaspora regularly book Kashi pujas remotely)
- Major puja types: Pind Daan, Rudra Abhishek, Mahamrityunjaya, Asthi Visarjan, Pitru Paksha rituals
