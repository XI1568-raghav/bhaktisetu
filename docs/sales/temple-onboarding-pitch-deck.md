# Temple Admin Onboarding — Pitch Deck Talking Points

> For founder/delegate physical visits to ISKCON / Birla / Akshardham / private temples.
> Use as conversation framework, not slide-by-slide reading.

## Opening (2 min)

"{{Temple name}} is one of the most respected {{Vaishnav/Shaiva/Shakti}} sanctuaries in {{city}}. I'm here from Bhaktisetu Foundation because we believe your temple's spiritual reach is currently limited by geography — devotees in {{NRI city}} who want to do a puja here have no trusted way to do so."

## Problem framing (3 min)

"Today, when a Hindu family in San Francisco wants to book a Rudrabhishek at your temple, they have three options:
1. WhatsApp middleman (no verification, no proof, often fake)
2. Call temple office directly (only works if they have phone numbers and can navigate language barrier)
3. Wait until they visit India (1-2 years between trips)

Option 1 hurts your temple reputation. Option 2 doesn't scale. Option 3 means you miss revenue and devotee connection.

Bhaktisetu solves Option 4: a verified platform with 24hr video proof, dual-currency pricing, and 80G receipts. Devotees book through us → your pandits perform the puja → we send video proof + receipt → temple gets 70% revenue share."

## Bhaktisetu credentials (3 min)

"Quick context:
- Founded 18 months ago by me, ex-TCS engineer in Bengaluru
- Section 8 (non-profit) Foundation registered with MCA
- 5,000+ active devotees including {{X NRI in {{relevant city}}}}
- 100+ Aadhaar-verified pandits across 7 Indian cities
- ₹10L monthly recurring revenue (90 days post-launch)
- Just closed $250K pre-seed funding for scale

We're NOT another tech company trying to disrupt temples. We're devotees building infrastructure that respects temple autonomy and grows your reach."

## Partnership structure (5 min)

"Specifically what we'd propose with {{temple}}:

**Revenue split:**
- Temple: 65-70% of devotee payment
- Pandit conducting puja: 20-25%
- Bhaktisetu platform: 10-15%

**Temple commitments:**
- Onboard 5+ Aadhaar-verified pandits (KYC by us, your approval)
- 5+ puja SKUs offered through platform (you choose which)
- 1 designated pandit liaison (single point of contact)
- Allow video recording during ceremony (we provide equipment if needed)

**Bhaktisetu commitments:**
- Tech platform free (we own dev + maintenance)
- Customer acquisition free (organic SEO + marketing)
- Payment processing free (Razorpay + Stripe + we absorb fees)
- 24/7 customer support
- Weekly Razorpay payout (no monthly delays)

**Contract:**
- 6-month pilot + 30-day termination at any time
- 2-year auto-renew if pilot successful
- No exclusivity (you can serve devotees outside our platform freely)
- No marketing co-mention requirement (optional)"

## Pilot ask (2 min)

"What I'd like from {{temple}} today is a pilot agreement:
- 1 pilot temple location ({{specific}})
- 3 pujas live in Q1 (e.g., {{Rudrabhishek + Lakshmi Puja + Satyanarayan}})
- 2-month pilot duration
- Monthly review meeting (founder personally attends)

If the pilot generates 50+ bookings + zero quality complaints, we expand to all your pujas + additional temple locations."

## Anticipated objections (5 min)

**"We don't need a platform — devotees come to us directly."**
→ "Domestic devotees yes. But your NRI families have no current option. Bhaktisetu doesn't replace your direct flow — we add NRI revenue you're currently missing entirely."

**"Video recording disrupts sanctity."**
→ "Many temples thought the same. After 6 months of live darshan streaming at Kashi Vishwanath and Mahalakshmi, devotion deepened — not weakened. NRI grandchildren now know their family kuldevta because they see daily aarti. Optional for {{temple}} — we offer recording only as devotee benefit, not requirement."

**"What if devotee disputes the puja quality?"**
→ "We mediate within 48hr. Founder personally reviews. So far 0 disputes in 100+ pandit network. Strong KYC + reference video filtering at onboarding prevents 99% of issues."

**"Why should temple trust Bhaktisetu vs another platform?"**
→ "Section 8 non-profit. All surplus reinvested. {{Local temple reference if available}}. {{Founder credentials}}. We're not VC-backed extraction — we're devotees building infrastructure."

**"Pandit comp is too low for our quality of pandits."**
→ "70% of devotee payment goes to temple, of which you decide pandit share. For premium pujas (₹5K+), pandit cut is ₹1-1.5K per ceremony — significantly above local market rates for non-platform bookings."

## Closing (2 min)

"I'll leave you with a 1-page summary of the proposal + my contact details. Take 1-2 weeks to discuss with temple administration + family heads. I'm happy to come back for a follow-up meeting whenever convenient.

Even if {{temple}} decides not to partner, I'd appreciate your candid feedback on what would make this proposition more attractive. Devotees globally need this infrastructure — your input shapes how we build it.

Most importantly: this isn't a sales pitch. This is a fellow devotee asking for your blessing to serve more Hindu families."
