# B2B Sales Scripts — Detailed Call Frameworks

> 4 detailed B2B sales call scripts for founder pitches. Conversation frameworks beyond cold emails.

## Script 1 — TCS Foundation Diwali Corporate Gifting

### Discovery call (30 min)

**Opening (2 min)**
"Hi [Name], thanks for taking this call. I'm Sunil from Bhaktisetu Foundation. Before I jump in — what's TCS Foundation's current Diwali employee gifting program look like? Just so I understand the baseline."

**Listen mode (10 min)**
Let them describe:
- Annual employee count receiving gifts
- Current cost per employee
- Vendor relationships
- Pain points (warehouse logistics, employee NPS data, ESG narrative)
- Strategic constraints (budget approval cycle, HR vs CSR ownership)

**Position (5 min)**
"Helpful. Here's our specific offer for TCS Foundation:
- Per employee: verified pandit-led Diwali Lakshmi Puja, ₹2,000 market value
- 24hr video proof from any Indian temple of their choosing (NRI employees value highly)
- 80G tax-deductible receipt to employee PAN
- Personalised wrapper with TCS branding (digital + physical option)
- For 50K employees: ₹9 Cr total cost vs ₹12 Cr equivalent gift baskets — 27% savings"

**Specific TCS value-adds (5 min)**
- ESG narrative — culturally meaningful CSR + tax-efficient
- Cost savings vs traditional gift baskets
- Differentiated from competitor IT services (Infosys, Wipro don't offer this)
- Employee NPS uplift (real puja vs dust-collecting plaque)
- BRSR (Business Responsibility + Sustainability Report) ESG bullet

**Objections handling (5 min)**

**Q: "We already have a Diwali gift program with [X vendor]."**
A: "Understood. Bhaktisetu doesn't replace your current vendor relationship. It's an additional category — devotional services. Many CIOs are now extending Diwali budgets to cover BOTH traditional gifts AND devotional/cultural offerings. Curious if your 2025 Diwali survey showed any feedback on cultural vs material gifts?"

**Q: "Section 8 (non-profit) structure — does that limit our flexibility?"**
A: "Actually it adds — Section 8 means 100% of surplus reinvested in our mission, which gives TCS Foundation cleaner ESG-narrative documentation than buying from for-profit competitors. Plus 80G tax-efficiency for the employee."

**Q: "Diwali is approaching fast — 4 months out. Can you scale 50K employees in time?"**
A: "Realistic — yes for 50K. Tight but doable. I'd recommend starting with 5-10K pilot in Q3, scaling to full 50K for Diwali day. 80+ pandits already in our network across 20+ cities; we're scaling to 200+ by Diwali. Can share the operational roadmap if you want."

**Close (3 min)**
"Two follow-up actions:
1. I'll email a 2-page detailed proposal within 48 hours
2. Can we schedule a 60-min deep-dive next Wednesday where you bring HR + procurement?
3. Timeline: contract by end of August would give us 4-month operational runway for Diwali"

## Script 2 — HDFC NRI Banking Partnership

### Discovery call (45 min)

**Opening (3 min)**
"Hi [Name], thanks for the meeting. Bhaktisetu is a Section 8 non-profit operating a verified-pandit puja platform — specifically built for NRI Hindu families to do pujas at India temples remotely. We have 5K active users, 18% NRI mix, and target 40% NRI mix by Q4."

**Discovery questions (15 min)**
- "How is HDFC currently differentiating its NRI offering vs ICICI / SBI Cards / Axis Bank?"
- "What's HDFC NRI customer engagement metric? Open-rate on emails, app usage frequency, transaction volume?"
- "What's been the response to recent cultural-identity programs (e.g., Indian classical music sponsorships)?"
- "Where does Hindu religious + cultural content sit in HDFC's NRI segmentation?"

**Position (10 min)**
"Three partnership structures, escalating in commitment:

**Tier 1 — Customer Acquisition Co-Marketing**
- Free first puja booking ($30 USD value, Bhaktisetu founder-funded)
- 'Powered by HDFC NRI Banking' footer on Bhaktisetu /nri pages
- Joint webinar Q3 — 'Devotional Rituals for Diaspora Families'
- Quarterly newsletter co-promotion

**Tier 2 — Bundled Benefit Program**
- HDFC NRI Premier package includes annual ₹2,000 wallet credit for Bhaktisetu pujas
- Bhaktisetu builds /partner/hdfc landing page with seamless redirect
- HDFC reports usage as engagement metric uplift

**Tier 3 — White-Label Service**
- Bhaktisetu API for HDFC NRI mobile app to offer in-app puja booking
- HDFC retains customer relationship + 20% commission
- Bhaktisetu handles puja delivery + customer support end-to-end"

**Mutual benefits (5 min)**
- HDFC: Differentiated NRI offering vs ICICI/SBI/Axis; ESG narrative
- Bhaktisetu: Access to 5L+ qualified NRI customers; brand trust uplift
- Customers: Free first puja + ongoing convenience + bank trust transfer

**Specific HDFC ask (5 min)**
- 15-min initial scoping call (founder Sunil + HDFC NRI executive)
- Access to NRI customer email list for opt-in marketing (with privacy compliance)
- Marketing budget approval for joint webinar (~₹2L)
- DPDP-compliant data sharing agreement

**Objections (5 min)**

**Q: "How do we ensure data privacy?"**
A: "DPDP fully compliant. Bhaktisetu Foundation has registered Grievance Officer + dedicated DSAR (Data Subject Access Request) endpoints. We sign data processing agreement (DPA) with HDFC per DPDP standards. Sub-processor list disclosed. Cross-border data flows minimised."

**Q: "What's HDFC's exposure if Bhaktisetu fails operationally?"**
A: "Limited to brand association. Tier 1 is purely co-marketing — no operational integration. Tier 2 begins formal commitment but capped at $2K credit/customer. Tier 3 is highest exposure but offset by significant revenue share. We start with Tier 1 to validate trust."

**Q: "Why now? Why not wait for Bhaktisetu to scale further?"**
A: "Two answers: 1) HDFC NRI market is growing fastest now. Late mover loses differentiation window. 2) Bhaktisetu pre-seed close gives 18-month runway — partnership today scales 10x with our funded growth."

**Close (5 min)**
"Suggested next steps:
1. I send 2-page detailed Tier 1 proposal this week
2. HDFC NRI head + Bhaktisetu founder 60-min deep-dive next week
3. Decision Tier 1 commitment by Aug 15
4. Launch Tier 1 program Sep 1"

## Script 3 — Tata Trusts Heritage Tourism

### Discovery call (60 min — strategic depth)

**Opening (5 min)**
"Hi [Name], thanks for taking this discovery call. Tata Trusts has a century of cultural heritage support, and Bhaktisetu Foundation operates as a Section 8 non-profit in the Hindu devotional commerce space. I think there's natural alignment, and want to scope what a meaningful partnership might look like."

**Discovery (20 min)**
- "What is Tata Trusts' current allocation to cultural heritage / Hindu tradition preservation?"
- "Which programs are doing well, which aren't yielding the impact you hoped?"
- "What's the constraint — funding (have grants but limited deployment), implementation (have funds but limited operational expertise), or strategy (broad agreement but narrow specific programs)?"
- "Where does Tata Trusts intersect with NRI Hindu diaspora communities?"

**Position (15 min)**
"Three potential partnership tiers, escalating in commitment + impact:

**Tier 1 — Heritage Temple Documentation**
- Bhaktisetu provides verified pandit network at 10 heritage temples
- Tata Trusts funds Sanskrit + Hindi + English documentation (1500 pages)
- Output: open-source Hindu Heritage Knowledge Base (CC-BY-SA)
- Bhaktisetu hosts on /heritage + API for global access
- Q4 launch target

**Tier 2 — Yatra Package Co-Marketing**
- Tata Trusts-endorsed yatra packages (Char Dham + Pancha Bhoota + Kashi-Gaya)
- ₹500-1000 Tata Trusts contribution per booking
- Joint marketing on Tata Trusts heritage channels
- Q1 2027 launch

**Tier 3 — Pilgrim Safety + Welfare**
- 2027+: digital infrastructure for pilgrim safety (live tracking, emergency response)
- Tata Trusts funds technology development
- Bhaktisetu operates the system
- Multi-year (3-5 year) commitment"

**Mutual benefits (10 min)**
- Tata Trusts: Heritage preservation + scalable cultural impact + technology adoption proof
- Bhaktisetu: Tier-1 brand association + funded heritage program + Tata network access
- Hindu community: Open-source temple knowledge + safer pilgrimage infrastructure

**Differentiation from competitors (5 min)**
"What makes Bhaktisetu specifically suited as Tata Trusts partner:
1. Section 8 non-profit structure = mission-aligned
2. Verified pandit network = quality assurance
3. NRI dual-currency = global Hindu diaspora reach
4. Open-source CC-BY-SA orientation = aligns with Tata Trusts' public-good ethos"

**Asks + timeline (5 min)**
- Q3 W12: founder physical meeting in Mumbai
- Q4 W6: MoU signed for Tier 1
- Q4 W12: Tier 1 program launch
- Q1 2027: Tier 2 + 3 evaluation
- Funding ask: ₹2 Cr for Tier 1 over 2 years

**Close (5 min)**
"This is a long-term partnership conversation — not a quick yes/no. Would you be open to scheduling a deep-dive review in 2 weeks where I bring my advisory board, you bring whoever needs to be in the room, and we walk through detailed proposals?"

## Script 4 — Tier-1 VC Pitch (Blume/Lightspeed/Peak XV/Accel)

### Pitch meeting (45 min)

**Opening (3 min)**
"Hi [Name], thanks for the 45 minutes. I'll keep it tight: Bhaktisetu's bootstrap-to-pre-seed story in 8 minutes, then your questions for the rest."

**Story (8 min)**

"3 things stand out about Bhaktisetu:

**1. Bootstrap traction**
- Solo founder, 90 days post-launch, ₹10L MRR
- 5,000 MAU, 100+ verified pandits, 7 cities
- $0 paid marketing — 70% organic SEO via 250 pillar pages
- 28 daily AI agents handling content/monitoring/ops
- Just closed pre-seed $250K from tier-1 angels

**2. Differentiated #2 in $58B market**
- Sri Mandir dominates with $35M Series B — but content-first
- Bhaktisetu adds: verified pandit + 24hr video proof + NRI dual-currency + AI Pandit
- 18% NRI revenue mix today (target 40% by Q4) — unique cross-border moat
- 65% Day-30 NRI retention vs 42% domestic baseline

**3. Default-alive trajectory**
- LTV:CAC 8x blended, 10x NRI
- Currently ₹50K/month burn; 18-month runway from pre-seed
- Default-alive at ₹25L MRR (12-month visible path)"

**Specific fund alignment (5 min)**
"Why [Fund] specifically:
- [Specific portfolio company comp]
- [Specific thesis area alignment]
- [Founder-friendly track record + post-Series A support pattern]"

**Ask (3 min)**
"Looking for $1.5M-$3M seed at $10-15M post-money cap. 
- Lead investor sets terms
- 4-6 follow-on angels
- 18-month runway target
- Quarterly investor reporting via existing newsletter cadence"

**Q&A (25 min — most valuable part)**

Listen carefully. Common VC questions:
- "How will you defend against Sri Mandir replicating your features?"
- "How does NRI ramp work post-Stripe live activation?"
- "What's your hiring plan?"
- "What's the path to ₹100 Cr ARR?"
- "What's the exit narrative — IPO, acquisition, or perpetual operating business?"
- "Show me unit economics deeper — gross margin, contribution margin per cohort"
- "How do you think about competition from other AI-Pandit products?"
- "What's your data moat — RAG corpus, customer data, what specifically?"
- "Have you talked to Sri Mandir founder?"
- "Why solo founder? What's the co-founder play?"

**Close (5 min)**
"Three follow-ups within 7 days:
1. Email summary of this conversation + memo expansion
2. Data room access (DD-ready)
3. 2-3 customer reference calls if interested
4. Decision target: 30 days (term sheet OR clear pass)"

## Founder discipline across all 4 scripts

- Listen 60%, talk 40%
- Specific numbers over vague claims
- Acknowledge objections directly, don't dodge
- Honest about challenges (avoids credibility loss)
- Concrete next steps every meeting
- Follow-up within 24-48 hours always
- Reference materials sent within 7 days
