- "Powered by HDFC NRI Banking" footer on Bhaktisetu /nri landing pages
- Joint webinar Q3: "Devotional Rituals for the Global Hindu Family"

**Tier 2 — Bundled Benefit Program** 
- HDFC NRI Premier package includes annual ₹2,000 wallet credit for Bhaktisetu puja bookings
- Bhaktisetu builds /partner/hdfc landing page with seamless redirect

**Tier 3 — White-Label Service**
- Bhaktisetu provides custom API for HDFC NRI mobile app to offer in-app puja booking

### Mutual benefits

- HDFC: Differentiated NRI offering vs ICICI/SBI Cards/Axis Bank; ESG narrative

## SCOPE 2 — TCS Diwali Corporate Gifting

**Title:** "Bhaktisetu × TCS Foundation — Diwali Employee Gifting Program 2026"

### Strategic context

- TCS has ~6L employees globally, ~5L in India
- 2024 Diwali gift program cost ~₹2.5 Cr (industry estimate)
- TCS Foundation interested in culturally meaningful programs vs generic gifts
- Bhaktisetu Section 8 status enables 80G tax-efficiency

### Proposed scope

**Per-employee Diwali Gift Package**
- Verified pandit-led Diwali Lakshmi Puja (₹2,000 market value)
- 24hr video proof from any Indian temple of their choosing (NRI employees value highly)
- 80G tax-deductible receipt to employee PAN
- Personalised wrapper w/ TCS branding (digital + physical option)
- Pandit confirmation message includes TCS sponsor mention

**Pricing**
- 50,000 employees pilot: ₹9 Cr total (vs ₹12 Cr equivalent gift baskets)
- 10% corporate discount applied
- Net-30 invoicing terms
- Bhaktisetu absorbs payment processing fees

**Operations**
- 4-week lead time required (Diwali Oct 2026 = TCS commit by mid-Sept)
- TCS provides employee email + PAN list (privacy-compliant)
- Bhaktisetu coordinates 80+ pandits across 20+ cities
- Daily progress dashboard for TCS HR

### Mutual benefits

- TCS: 27% cost savings vs generic gifts + 30% NPS uplift + ESG narrative
- TCS Foundation: First major corporate Diwali partnership with devotional platform
- Bhaktisetu: ₹9 Cr Q4 revenue + 50K customer acquisition + landmark partnership reference

### Risks + mitigation

- Risk: TCS Foundation may have existing devotional programs
- Mitigation: Position as complement, not replacement
- Risk: 4-week timeline tight for 50K-employee execution
- Mitigation: Pilot 10K employees Q3, scale Q4 if successful

**Title:** "Bhaktisetu × Bharti Foundation — Hindu Education Curriculum Co-Development"

### Strategic context

- Bharti Foundation focus on education (Satya Bharti School network)
- Bhaktisetu builds Gurukul recurring tuition product (per R-Q3-P-004)

### Proposed scope

**Tier 1 — Vedic Studies Curriculum**
- Bharti Foundation funds development of free Vedic studies curriculum (40 hours of content)
- Bhaktisetu coordinates pandit-author network
- Output: free educational resource available globally
- License: CC-BY-SA (open-source education)

**Tier 2 — Sanskrit Tutor Subsidy**
- Bharti Foundation subsidizes Bhaktisetu Sanskrit gurukul subscriptions for 1000 underprivileged students
- Cost: ₹999/mo × 1000 students × 12 months = ₹1.2 Cr/year
- Bhaktisetu maintains operations + tracks student progress

**Tier 3 — Gurukul Network Support**
- Bhaktisetu identifies 10 underfunded Vedic gurukuls
- Bharti Foundation provides ₹5L/yr per gurukul × 10 = ₹50L/year
- Bhaktisetu maintains coordination + reporting

### Mutual benefits

- Bharti Foundation: Cultural heritage education at scale + measurable impact + tier-1 partner brand
- Bhaktisetu: Gurukul product validation + 1000+ student base + heritage credibility
- Society: 1000 underprivileged students access Vedic education
