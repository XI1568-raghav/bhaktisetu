## Goal

Direct DM 5 ISKCON Bengaluru priests via WhatsApp, NOT via temple-trustee path. Faster cycle, priest-direct outreach. Useful when temple TP path is bureaucratic.

## Why priest-direct DM (vs TP)

| Path | Cycle time | Conversion | Trust depth |
|---|---|---|---|
| Temple Trustee | 30-60 days | 20% (needs MoU + Bureau OK) | HIGH after sign |
| **Priest direct DM** | 5-14 days | 35-50% (priest decides own bookings) | MED (per-priest, no temple-level coverage) |

Priest-direct = best for pilot-validation. Temple-trustee = best for scaling.
