## Per-fund customisation principles

1. **Open with their thesis fit** — research their public investments
2. **Specific portfolio reference** — show familiarity with their existing wins
3. **Specific differentiation from competitor** — pre-empt the "Sri Mandir already exists" objection
4. **Their value-add beyond money** — distribution network, hiring, follow-on
5. **Clear ask + valuation** — anchor at fund's typical pre-seed check size
6. **18-month milestone** — concrete metric that matters to them

## Founder discipline

- Each variant pre-personalised w/ partner name + their stated thesis
- Maximum 25 slides (15 slides + 10 appendix)
- Live demo readiness regardless of variant
- Reference check 2 portfolio founders BEFORE meeting (mention in pitch)
- Follow-up email within 4 hours of meeting
