## Goal

After Q2 BLR ISKCON pilot proves model (1-2 BLR ISKCON temples live + happy customer feedback), escalate to central deal via Bureau path. Sign LOI covering 5 ISKCON temple onboarding pipeline.

## Path

1. Q1-Q2: pilot 2 BLR ISKCON temples (per S-011 — Bangalore-camp + 1 standard ISKCON)
2. Pilot proves model → Bhaktisetu has data (devotee count + GMV + video proof) to show Bureau
3. Q2-end (Aug): founder send formal LOI to Bureau Secretary (per iskcon-loi-template-v1.md)
4. Sept-Oct: 30-day Bureau review
5. Oct-Nov: in-person meeting Mayapur OR Mumbai
6. Dec: signed LOI committing to MoU within 90 days

## 5 target temples (post-LOI MoU)

Per iskcon-org-map.md §7 (5 distributed-deal entry points):
1. ISKCON Chowpatty Mumbai
2. ISKCON Juhu (Hare Krishna Land) Mumbai
3. ISKCON Delhi East of Kailash
4. ISKCON Mayapur
5. ISKCON Vrindavan

## Revenue impact

5 ISKCON temples × ~30 pujas/mo × ₹501 × 20-40% Bhaktisetu fee = ₹15K-30K MRR per temple = ₹75K-150K MRR aggregate from ISKCON alone.

Plus adopt-a-temple subscriptions layered on top.

Bangalore-camp dispute (per iskcon-org-map.md §8.1) means we may need to choose. Recommend: explicitly carve out Bangalore as separate via separate LOI w/ Bangalore camp; central deal covers ONLY other 5+ temples.
