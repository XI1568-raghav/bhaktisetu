## 1. Kavita Iyer — The NRI Mother

**Tagline:** "I left India, but I never left my parents' puja calendar."

### Demographics
- Age 42. Lives in Edison, New Jersey (also representative of comparable users in Leicester UK and Sharjah UAE).
- Tamil Brahmin (Smarta tradition); married into a Gujarati Vaishnav household. Two school-age children.
- Software product manager; household income $180K. Husband works in finance.
- Indian passport holder, OCI route. Visits India once every 18-24 months.

### Goals
- Honor her father's annual shraadh at Kashi and her mother-in-law's vrat calendar without taking PTO.
- Keep her US-born children connected to ritual identity through visible, narratable acts (a video of *their* puja at *their* family temple).
- Send authentic prasad and 80G-eligible donations as gifts to in-laws on birthdays and anniversaries.

### Pain points
- 9.5-hour timezone gap; Indian temple aarti happens while she sleeps.
- Has been burned by WhatsApp-only "puja agents" who took payment and sent a generic photo.
- Cannot verify whether a priest in Varanasi actually performed the sankalp in her father's gotra and nakshatra.
- US tax filings need clean donation receipts; informal Indian temple receipts are unusable.

### Buying triggers
- Death anniversaries (tithi-based, not Gregorian) — recurring, high-emotion.
- Pitru Paksha, Diwali, Akshaya Tritiya, Ganga Dussehra.
- Children's milestones: first day of school, US college admission results, birthdays.
- Parents' health scares back home (Mahamrityunjaya, Rudrabhishek).

### Channels
- Facebook NRI groups ("Indians in NJ", "Tamil Sangam"), Instagram reels, WhatsApp family broadcasts, YouTube T-Series Bhakti and Sadhguru pre-roll, BAPS Edison community board.

### Objections and the Darshya answer
- *"How do I know the puja actually happened?"* — Mandatory video proof uploaded by the priest before payout (CLAUDE.md business rule 1).
- *"Is the temple real?"* — Only verified temples go live (rule 6); verification badge visible on listing.
- *"Can I claim this on taxes?"* — 80G receipt with PAN + DR-YYYYMMDD-XXXX number, downloadable PDF.
- *"Why is this 10x what my cousin in Pune pays?"* — NRI tier pricing is transparent and funds full video production, international prasad shipping, and timezone-aware scheduling.

### Willingness to pay
Sweet spot is **Bhakti Premium ($14.99/mo)** as habitual baseline, escalating to **Bhakti Patron ($59.99/mo or $599.99/yr)** around major tithis when she wants VIP priest access and quarterly temple visit videos. Per-puja gifting: comfortable at $99-$299 for a Rudrabhishek with prasad shipped to in-laws.

### Sample converting message
"Amma's tithi is March 14. We will live-stream the puja from Kashi at your local time, send the full video the same day, and ship blessed prasad to her doorstep in Coimbatore. 80G receipt included."

## 2. Rajesh Deshmukh — The India Devotee

**Tagline:** "My ancestral temple is 700 km away. My EMI is due Friday."

### Demographics
- Age 38. Lives in Pune; ancestral village temple in Nashik district.
- Maharashtrian, Deshastha; family deity (kuldevta) at a heritage temple in Trimbakeshwar.
- Senior manager at an IT services firm; household income ₹28-35 LPA. One child, aging parents in the same city.
- Smartphone-first, comfortable with UPI, Razorpay, OTP flows. Reads in Marathi and English.

### Goals
- Maintain unbroken kuldevta obligations (annual abhishek, satyanarayan puja on key tithis) despite a 60-hour work week.
- Perform timely shanti pujas around life events without negotiating with a local pandit network he does not trust.
- Show respect to parents by booking pujas in their name during festivals.

### Pain points
- Cannot take Friday-Monday off four times a year to drive to Trimbakeshwar.
- Local pandit referrals are opaque: rates vary 3x between two priests for the same puja.
- WhatsApp-forwarded "online puja" sites look like phishing pages and have no GST invoice.
- Aging parents ask "did the puja really happen?" and he has no answer.

### Buying triggers
- Kuldevta annual obligation (fixed tithi).
- Festivals on the LAUNCH_STRATEGY calendar: Akshaya Tritiya, Ganga Dussehra, Jagannath Rath Yatra, Diwali Lakshmi Puja.
- Life events: child's board exam result, new home (vastu shanti), car purchase, parent's surgery (Mahamrityunjaya), promotion announcement.
- Death anniversary of grandfather (he is the eldest son).

### Channels
- WhatsApp family groups, Instagram reels (Marathi devotional creators), YouTube aarti channels, Google search ("kuldevta puja online", "trimbakeshwar abhishek booking"), Sharechat regional spiritual content, Reddit r/india occasionally.

### Objections and the Darshya answer
- *"How is this different from the WhatsApp scam I almost fell for?"* — Verified temples only, video proof mandatory, BS-YYYYMMDD-XXXXX booking number traceable in-app.
- *"Will the priest take my money and disappear?"* — Priest payout is gated on Aadhaar verification and video upload; commission is taken upfront by Darshya, not the priest.
- *"What if I need to cancel?"* — 24-hour cancellation window per business rule 5.
- *"Is the priest qualified for our parampara?"* — Filter by sampradaya, language, and gotra-aware sankalp generation.

### Willingness to pay
Domestic INR pricing (₹501-₹5,100 per puja depending on type). Will not subscribe initially; converts on transactional bookings. Likely upgrades to **Bhakti Pass at ₹49/month** (per pitch deck) once he has used the platform 2-3 times. Annual spend ₹8,000-₹20,000 across kuldevta + festivals + family events.

### Sample converting message
"Your kuldevta abhishek at Trimbakeshwar, Saturday 6 AM. Verified priest (Aadhaar + ratings), full video sent to your WhatsApp, GST invoice and 80G receipt for the donation portion. Cancel free until Friday 6 AM."

## 3. Pandit Anand Sharma — The Provider (with Temple Manager overlay)

**Tagline:** "I know the shastra. I do not know how to find devotees on the internet."

### Demographics
- Age 47. Lives in Varanasi; performs pujas at a mid-tier ghat temple and at devotee homes.
- Kanyakubja Brahmin, fourth-generation purohit. Trained in Karmakand at a traditional pathshala plus a Sanskrit MA.
- Household income ₹18,000-₹40,000/month, highly seasonal (festival-heavy). Wife, two children, ailing father at home.
- Owns an Android phone (₹12,000 range). Uses WhatsApp daily, UPI for tips, no email habit. Hindi-first, functional English.

**Temple Manager overlay:** A 35-year-old trustee or appointed manager at a partner temple (per Phase 2 onboarding) who handles MoU signing, hardware install, schedule entry, and analytics review. Same trust profile, but more administrative, less ritual.

### Goals
- A predictable monthly booking flow that smooths out the post-festival income trough.
- Reach NRI devotees without building a website or running ads he does not understand.
- Get paid on time, in full, into his own bank account — not routed through a middleman who deducts unexplained fees.

### Pain points
- Local aggregators take 50-60% and pay 30-45 days late (per pitch deck Slide 2).
- Cannot prove to a Mumbai or US devotee that the puja happened the way they wanted.
- Fears digital onboarding will demand documents he does not have organized (PAN, Aadhaar linkage, bank IFSC).
- Worries about being held responsible if a stream cuts out or prasad delivery is delayed.

### Buying triggers (i.e., reasons to onboard)
- Festival season approaching with empty calendar slots.
- A peer priest tells him about a successful payout from Darshya.
- A temple liaison coordinator (Phase 2) shows up in person with a camera kit and an MoU.
- Direct outreach quoting his expected monthly earning at 60-80% revenue share (Phase 2 pitch).

### Channels
- WhatsApp (priest peer groups, temple committee groups), in-person pitches by liaison coordinators, vernacular YouTube, missed-call lead-gen, panchayat-level word of mouth. Will NOT be reached on LinkedIn, Twitter, or Product Hunt.

### Objections and the Darshya answer
- *"Will I actually get paid?"* — Priest payout = booking price minus commission, gated only on Aadhaar verification (rule 4). Transparent in-app ledger, payout per booking, not batched.
- *"What if the devotee complains after the puja?"* — Video proof is the contractual completion artifact; once uploaded and accepted, payout is committed.
- *"How much do you take?"* — 20-40% configurable per temple (rule 3), disclosed upfront in the MoU.
- *"I do not have time to learn an app."* — Priest Portal is built for one job: see today's bookings, perform, upload video, get paid. Hindi UI per brand guidelines.
- *"Will I be locked in exclusively?"* — No exclusivity required; he can keep walk-in devotees on the side.

### Willingness to pay
He does not pay a subscription. He gives up commission. Acceptable range: **20-30% commission** for new priests (sweetened with hardware support and guaranteed booking volume in the first quarter), **30-40%** for high-demand priests at flagship temples once they see the volume. Temple Manager overlay: same — temple keeps 60-80% of booking value plus full donation pass-through.

### Sample converting message
"Pandit ji, three NRI devotees in New Jersey want a Rudrabhishek at your ghat next Saturday. We bring the camera, we handle payment, you receive ₹4,200 in your bank within 24 hours of uploading the puja video. Aadhaar + bank details, ten minutes setup."
