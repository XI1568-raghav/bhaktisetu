3. **Lead investor mandatory**: Yes (cleaner cap table, signals validation). If no lead by M3, party round acceptable.
4. **Closing schedule**: hard close end-Q3 2026 vs rolling close. Default = rolling close once first ₹1 Cr committed.
5. **Founder-friendly clauses**: weighted-average anti-dilution, super pro-rata, board observer (not member). Confirm with legal.
