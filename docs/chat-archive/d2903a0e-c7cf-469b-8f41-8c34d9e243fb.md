### Risk #2 — Apple IAP vs. Razorpay (Guideline 3.1.1) — HIGH if mishandled

This is the single most common reason job-board apps get rejected on iOS.

**The rule:**
- Razorpay is allowed for **"physical goods or services consumed outside the app"** — e.g., an employer paying for a recruitment service that produces real-world hires. ✅
- Razorpay is **NOT allowed** for **"digital content or services consumed within the app"** — e.g., unlocking premium features, removing ads, boosting your own profile. ❌ Apple requires **In-App Purchase (IAP)** with their 15–30% cut for those.

**What this means for Kirti Job:**

| Premium feature type | Payment allowed |
|---|---|
| Employer pays to post a job (recruitment service → real hire) | ✅ Razorpay OK |
| Employer pays to "feature" a listing (digital boost in-app) | ❌ Must use Apple IAP on iOS |
| Job seeker pays to unlock premium profile (digital benefit in-app) | ❌ Must use Apple IAP on iOS |
| Subscription to "Pro Recruiter" tier giving in-app digital perks | ❌ Must use Apple IAP on iOS |

✅ **Action — confirm with your team which premium features (if any) launch at v1.0**, then either:
- (a) Remove iOS premium features at launch and ship Razorpay-only on Android. Add IAP later. **Safest path.**
- (b) Implement Apple IAP for digital features on iOS *and* Razorpay on Android.
- (c) Launch with no paid features at all — both stores accept a free app.

If you're unsure, **launch free on iOS for v1.0**. Don't risk a rejection on day one.
