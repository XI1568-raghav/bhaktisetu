## 🏛️ SECTION B: THE SHANTI ACT — GAME CHANGER (Dec 2025)

### What is SHANTI?
**"Sustainable Harnessing and Advanced Nuclear Technology for India"** — Passed by Parliament in **December 2025**, replacing the 1962 Atomic Energy Act. The single most important nuclear policy reform in India's history.

### Key Changes

| Before SHANTI (1962 Act) | After SHANTI (Dec 2025) |
|--------------------------|-------------------------|
| Only NPCIL could build/own/operate nuclear plants | **Private companies can BUILD, OWN & OPERATE** |
| Nuclear = government monopoly | Licensed private nuclear operators allowed |
| Foreign investment near-zero | Minority foreign stakes possible |
| Restrictive liability made suppliers wary | New liability framework + commercial insurance pathway |
| SMR concept only theoretical | SMR RFP issued to 6 private companies |
| No captive nuclear power possible | Industrial companies can use captive nuclear |

### The "16 States" — Clarification
> ⚠️ **Important:** SHANTI is a **Central/Union law** — states do NOT vote on it separately.
