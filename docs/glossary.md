# Glossary

Domain terms used throughout the codebase. Read before naming new entities.

## Devotional

- **Darshan** — live video stream from a temple's sanctum sanctorum. The user "takes darshan" by viewing the deity.
- **Puja** — ritual prayer performed by a priest on behalf of a devotee. Booking flow includes time slot, sankalp, and video proof.
- **Sankalp** — formal prayer intention spoken at the start of a puja. Includes devotee's name, gotra, and stated wish/purpose. Generated by AI in our flow (Llama 3.3).
- **Prasad** — sacred food offering blessed during a puja, delivered to the devotee.
- **Chadhava** — virtual offering at a temple (flowers, bhog, diya, garland). Lower price point than full puja.
- **Aarti** — ritual of light, performed at fixed times. Streamed live for popular temples.
- **Mantra** — sacred chant. Audio content tier in our app.
- **Bhakti** — devotion. The product was briefly named "BhaktiSetu" (devotion bridge).

## Astrology / Vedic

- **Panchang** — Hindu almanac with five elements: tithi, nakshatra, yoga, karana, vaar (weekday). Plus festivals. We compute real values via `mhah-panchang`, not mocked.
- **Tithi** — lunar day (1-30 in a lunar month).
- **Nakshatra** — birth-star constellation (27 total). Important for puja muhurat.
- **Yoga** — astronomical combination of sun + moon longitudes.
- **Karana** — half-tithi.
- **Rashi** — Vedic zodiac sign (12 total).
- **Gotra** — patrilineal lineage identifier. Required field on every puja booking.
- **Muhurat** — auspicious time window for an action.

## Identity / lineage

- **Devotee** — end user receiving services.
- **Priest / Pandit / Pujari** — service provider performing pujas.
- **Acharya** — senior teacher/priest.
- **Gaushala** — cow shelter; cow feeding/adoption service.

## Tax / financial (India)

- **80G** — Indian Income Tax Act section permitting deduction for charitable donations. Receipts must include donee 80G registration number + PAN. Our format: `DR-YYYYMMDD-XXXX`.
- **PAN** — Permanent Account Number. India tax ID. Required on 80G receipts.
- **NRI** — Non-Resident Indian. Pricing tier (9-10× domestic).
- **GST** — Goods and Services Tax. Not currently applied to puja services in our flow.
