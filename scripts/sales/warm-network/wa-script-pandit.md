# WhatsApp script — Pandit referral ask

> **Purpose**: ask warm contact to introduce a verified pandit / pujari (last-rites OR general puja).
> **Send from**: founder's personal WhatsApp. Manual paste per contact.
> **Target**: 100 T1 pandits is current 90-day goal; only 3 onboarded.

## Variant A — close friend / family

```
{Name} bhai/ji, ek choti si ask hai.

Aap ke {family / community / city} mein koi pandit ji hain jo:
  (a) shastra ke knowledge ke saath puja karte hain
  (b) WhatsApp comfortable hain (basic tech)
  (c) khud ki income badhana chahte hain — hum pandit ko 70-80% payout dete hain per puja

Bhaktisetu pe pandit ji ko monthly income predictable ho jata hai (1 puja per week = ₹4-8K depending on type). Verification simple — Aadhaar + 2 video proof of completed pujas + reference.

Agar mind mein koi aate hain, intro kara dein. Phone ya WA enough hai.

Aapke time ke liye dhanyavad.

— {founder name}
```

**When to use**: close personal network, family pandit memory.
**Reply tracking**: log to `warm-network-tracker.csv` → `script_variant: pandit-A`.

## Variant B — distant connection / LinkedIn 1st-degree

```
Hi {Name},

Founder of Bhaktisetu here. Quick ask — we're growing our verified-pandit network across India and currently have a supply gap (3 onboarded vs 100 Q1 target).

Knowing your connection to {their context}, do you know a pandit, pujari, or priest who:
  - performs traditional pujas (any sampradaya — we cover Smarta/Vaishnava/Arya Samaj/regional)
  - has WhatsApp + Aadhaar (basic onboarding requirements)
  - wants additional booking flow on top of their existing family clients

We pay pandits 70-80% per puja completion. Average ₹4-8K per puja depending on type. Verification is light (Aadhaar + 2 reference video pujas).

Even one name + phone helps a lot. No pressure.

Best,
{founder name}
Bhaktisetu Foundation
```

**When to use**: < 2 years relationship, professional context.
**Reply tracking**: log → `script_variant: pandit-B`.

## Variant C — current Bhaktisetu user / devotee

```
{Name} ji, aap Bhaktisetu use karte hain — ek baat puchhni thi.

Aapke perception mein agar Bhaktisetu ke liye 5 pandit chahiye {city / region} mein, toh aap kis pandit ji ko recommend karenge?

Maine ye soch ke pucha — devotees jo pandit ji ko personally jaante hain, woh sabse better filter hote hain. Aap recommend karenge toh main aapka reference le kar contact karunga.

— {founder name}
```

**When to use**: existing devotee, social-proof leverage.
**Reply tracking**: log → `script_variant: pandit-C`.

## Post-reply (positive)

```
Dhanyavad {Name} ji. Aaj hi {referee name} se baat karta hoon — aapke reference ke saath. Onboarding 3-5 din mein complete ho jaayegi agar woh haan kahe.

— {founder name}
```

## Post-reply (silent)

No re-ping after 7 days. Pandit referrals are sensitive (community trust). Don't damage the bridge.
