# WhatsApp script — Gaushala referral ask

> **Purpose**: ask warm contact to introduce a registered gaushala manager (target 25 onboarded, currently 0 per R-Q4-S-005 baseline).
> **Send from**: founder's personal WhatsApp. Manual paste.
> **Bias**: gaushalas are typically run by older managers, less tech-comfortable. Phone call may be needed after initial WA.

## Variant A — close friend / family

```
{Name} bhai/ji, ek question hai gauseva ke baare mein.

Aapke {village / city / native place} mein koi gaushala hai jo:
  (a) registered hai (80G or 12A status)
  (b) cow-count 30+ hai
  (c) WhatsApp-comfortable manager hai (ya beta/beti jo modern dekhte hain)

Hum Bhaktisetu pe ek "Adopt a Gaushala" + "Gauseva donation" feature ship kar rahe hain — NRI + India donors gauseva ke liye recurring donate kar sakenge, 80G receipt + monthly cow-care impact report.

Gaushala ko 85% disbursement, hum payment + receipt + reporting handle karte hain. Pure transparent.

Agar koi pata hai, ek intro kara dein. Phone number bhi enough hai.

Gau-mata ka aashirvad,
{founder name}
```

**When to use**: family / personal network w/ village connection.
**Reply tracking**: log → `script_variant: gaushala-A`.

## Variant B — distant / LinkedIn

```
Hi {Name},

Bhaktisetu founder here. We're launching a recurring-donation feature for verified gaushalas (NRI + India donors fund gauseva on subscription model, 85% disbursed monthly, 80G receipts auto-issued).

We need 25 verified gaushala partners to launch. Currently zero.

Knowing your {context — region / community / interest}, do you know any gaushala manager or trustee who might be open to a 15-min intro call? Specifically:
  - registered 80G or 12A status
  - cow count 30+
  - trustworthy operational track record (no scams)

Even one name helps a lot. Happy to share our deck if useful.

Best,
{founder name}
Bhaktisetu Foundation
```

**When to use**: < 2 year relationship OR professional context.
**Reply tracking**: log → `script_variant: gaushala-B`.

## Variant C — gauseva donor / interested party

```
{Name} ji, aap gauseva mein interested hain.

Hum platform bana rahe hain jahaan donors aur registered gaushalas ko transparent connect kar sakein — recurring donation, monthly cow-care impact report, 80G eligible.

Aapko personally koi gaushala pata hai jo partner ban sakti hai? Ya koi referral chain (community elder / temple connection / etc)?

Aap khud bhi adopt karna chahein toh waitlist mein add kar dein — launch hone par first invite aapko milega.

— {founder name}
```

**When to use**: existing donor / gauseva-interested contact.
**Reply tracking**: log → `script_variant: gaushala-C`.

## Important notes for gaushala outreach

- Many gaushalas are RUN BY ELDERLY MEN who do NOT use WhatsApp regularly. After warm intro, expect a phone call (NOT WA). Block 15-30 min per follow-up.
- Verify 80G/12A status before adding to platform — request scanned copy. Foundation legal will validate.
- Avoid "donate now" CTA in initial messaging — gauseva trust is built over multiple touches. First message = curiosity invite, not transaction.

```
— {founder name}
```
