# WhatsApp script — Donor / first-customer ask

> **Purpose**: ask warm contact to BE the first customer (book a puja OR adopt-a-temple subscription). Different from supply-side asks (temple/pandit/gaushala) — this is demand-side activation.
> **Send from**: founder's personal WhatsApp. Manual paste.
> **Bias**: ask for action, not money. Most uncomfortable script in the kit — handle with respect.

## Variant A — close friend / family

```
{Name} bhai/ji, Bhaktisetu launch ke baad pehli baar puchh raha hoon.

Aap kabhi bhi koi puja booking, online darshan, ya temple-donation karna chahein, toh Bhaktisetu pe try karein — feedback meri sabse badi help hogi.

Available services:
  • Live darshan ({famous temple list — Tirupati / Kashi Vishwanath / Vaishno Devi) — free
  • Remote puja w/ video proof — ₹501 to ₹5000+ depending on type
  • Adopt-a-Temple subscription — ₹500/month onwards, monthly impact PDF + 80G receipt
  • Sankalp puja for a specific occasion (birth / anniversary / father's punyatithi)

Even one ₹501 booking se humein learn karne ko milta hai — payment flow, video delivery, satisfaction. Aapka feedback paid customer ka, organic ka nahi.

Link: https://darshya.online ya direct WA me se: {your code if exists}

Koi force nahi — agar mind kare toh karein.

Bhakti ke saath,
{founder name}
```

**When to use**: close personal network, willing to be alpha-tester.
**Reply tracking**: log → `script_variant: donor-A`.

## Variant B — NRI friend / family

```
Hi {Name},

You're abroad, festivals are far, family in India often books pujas without you knowing.

Bhaktisetu lets you:
  • book remote pujas at India temples on your behalf (video proof returned within 24-72h)
  • adopt a temple monthly with 80G + USD billing (Stripe rails for NRI)
  • send sankalp on punyatithi / birthday / festival without flying back

I'd be honored if you booked something — even ₹500 ($10) — to test the flow. Your feedback as a paying NRI user is what we need most.

Link: https://darshya.online

Pranam to family.
{founder name}
```

**When to use**: NRI close contact (USA / Gulf / UK / SG / AU).
**Reply tracking**: log → `script_variant: donor-B`.

## Variant C — current donor (other org) shifting to Bhaktisetu

```
{Name} ji, aap {Sai Baba / ISKCON / specific temple / gaushala} ko regular donate karte hain.

Bhaktisetu pe abhi 12 verified temples + 0 gaushalas listed hain — par hum specifically tax-receipt + monthly impact + transparent flow rakhte hain. Agar aap kabhi recurring donation move karna chahein ya parallel try karna chahein, ek subscription ₹500/month aapke specific temple ko adopt kar dein.

Sirf "aapka choice" — bilkul force nahi. Aapne donate karna hi hai, toh experience worth karna chahiye.

Link: https://darshya.online/temples

— {founder name}
```

**When to use**: known donor to other religious orgs.
**Reply tracking**: log → `script_variant: donor-C`.

## Post-reply (positive — booking made)

```
{Name} ji, dhanyavad! Aapki booking {booking number} mil gayi. Pandit ji se confirm hote hi WhatsApp pe update karunga. Video proof 72 ghante mein.

Hari Om.

— {founder name}
```

## Post-reply (silent)

No re-ping after 7 days. Donor asks have the highest "no" rate — protect the relationship.

## Founder note

The donor-script is the HARDEST one to send. Most founders avoid asking close family to be customers because they feel it cheapens the relationship. Reframe: you are giving them an opportunity to support what you're building, NOT taking money from them.

5 close-network bookings in week 1 = enormous social proof for the next 50 asks. Skip this script and the warm-net pipeline halves in value.
