# Canada pandit KYC checklist

> R-Q3-S-007 cycle slice. Canadian-context. Different from India KYC —
> Aadhaar is NOT collected; SIN is NOT collected.
> Founder confirms Vulnerable Sector Check policy (see #5) before activation.

## Identity (one-of)

- [ ] Canadian PR card (front + back scan)
- [ ] Canadian citizenship certificate / card
- [ ] Valid work permit (with employer-not-restricted clause OR contractor permit)

## Address proof (one-of, ≤ 90 days old)

- [ ] Utility bill (hydro / internet / gas)
- [ ] Lease agreement / mortgage statement
- [ ] Bank statement with mailed address

## NOT collected (privacy)

- [ ] **SIN — NEVER request.** SIN is restricted to CRA tax slips. Bhaktisetu
      issues T4A only at year-end for pandits earning > CAD 500/yr; SIN is
      collected ONLY at that point via secure Form TD1 workflow, never during
      KYC.

## References

- [ ] 2 local references (Canada-based)
- [ ] At least 1 reference must be a temple trustee or sampradaya elder

## Background check

- [ ] **Vulnerable Sector Check (VSC)** — required for any pandit who will
      perform pujas attended by children (Janmashtami, Mundan, Namkaran,
      Upanayan, school events). VSC slows onboarding ~3 weeks and requires
      RCMP coordination via local police station.
- [ ] Standard criminal record check (CPIC) acceptable for adult-only pujas.
- [ ] **Founder decision (out-of-cycle):** confirm whether VSC is mandatory
      for all Bhaktisetu Canada pandits, or only those opting into
      child-adjacent puja SKUs.

## Sanskrit / ritual credential (one-of)

- [ ] Paramparik lineage (guru-shishya parampara) — letter from guru or
      sampradaya organisation
- [ ] Formal certification from a recognised Veda Pathshala / Gurukul
- [ ] Verified ISKCON / BAPS / Hindu Sabha pandit roster entry

## Languages served

- [ ] English (mandatory for Canada NRI base)
- [ ] At least one of: Hindi, Punjabi, Tamil, Gujarati, Marathi, Bengali,
      Telugu, Kannada, Malayalam

## Banking

- [ ] Canadian chequing account (preferred for CAD payouts)
- [ ] OR India NRO/NRE account for cross-border payouts (slower, FX cost
      borne by pandit)

## Compliance attestations

- [ ] Pandit consents to video-recording mandatory per Bhaktisetu business
      rule #1 (video proof on every booking)
- [ ] Pandit consents to platform commission 20-40% (rule #3)
- [ ] Pandit understands cancellation cutoff is 24h before scheduled time
      (rule #5)

## Sign-off

- [ ] Founder review complete
- [ ] All required documents collected
- [ ] Bhaktisetu Canada listing approved
